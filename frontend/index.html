Wayfinder is a college admissions and career intelligence platform built for families who want answers grounded in real data, not generic chatbot advice. It combines structured federal datasets, distilled admissions officer expertise, and a continuously growing knowledge base to help students and parents make high-stakes decisions with clarity and confidence.

1,500+ internships, 1,000+ scholarships, and 800+ enrichment programs with verified entries and real source URLs
Financial aid planner with SAI calculator, school-by-school aid profiles, and a personalized strategy engine
Essay review engine trained on admissions officer scoring standards with voice authenticity analysis and line-by-line coaching
Admissions analytics, demographic intelligence, career data, and a 95-file knowledge base built from BLS, IPEDS, O*NET, and NCES
BETA — Volunteer module with curated programs across 17 cause categories, AI-driven service strategy planner, and live local-program discovery (coverage densest in WA + major metros, growing weekly)

Meet David

YOUR WAYFINDER CONCIERGE

David is your personal guide inside Wayfinder, powered by the same intelligence that drives the platform. He sees which tool you’re using and what you’re working on, so he can give you relevant help in real time — whether you’re reviewing your SAI results, filtering scholarships, or drafting an essay.

Ask him about FAFSA rules while you calculate your SAI. Get feedback on your essay approach before you submit. Or just ask what to do next — he tracks your session and knows where you left off.
