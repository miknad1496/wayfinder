Aid Planner Pay less. Borrow less.

For families trying to figure out what college will actually cost — and how to lower the number. Net price by income bracket, state grants, FAFSA SAI, and a personalized aid strategy in one place.

$ When and why would I use this module? click to expand

Use this when you're:

  • Comparing the real cost of 5-10 schools at your income bracket — not the inflated sticker price.
  • Figuring out whether a private school at $80K/yr might actually be cheaper than your in-state public after aid.
  • Wondering which schools meet 100% of demonstrated need vs. which leave gaps you'd have to borrow.
  • Building an aid strategy: ED vs. RD trade-offs, merit-aid targeting, FAFSA + CSS Profile timing.
  • Estimating your Student Aid Index (SAI) before filing FAFSA so you know what to expect.
  • Discovering state grants you may already qualify for — most families miss $5K-$15K/yr in state aid.

How to use this module: start in School Search to find schools by total cost or aid policy. Switch to My Strategy for a personalized plan with your income, GPA, and target schools. Use Grants & Aid to find your state's programs. Use SAI Calculator for a 60-second FAFSA estimate.

What's in our database: 1,400+ four-year colleges with verified tuition, fees, room & board, and net-price-by-income data sourced from IPEDS + CollegeBoard. Need-blind, meets-100%-need, and merit-generous flags hand-curated.
