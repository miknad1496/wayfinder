# George Washington University (GWU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current GWU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 research university, ~12,000 undergrad enrollment, Washington DC (Foggy Bottom + Mount Vernon campuses)
- Admit rate: ~43-49%
- Test policy: **Test-blind** (does not consider scores even if submitted) — distinctive for an R1 private
- Application: Common App + GW supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 5
- RD deadline: Jan 5

## What admissions actually weights
GWU's identity: **DC location is THE differentiator** — students intern at the State Department, World Bank, IMF, Capitol Hill, K Street think tanks, embassies, federal agencies. Strong applicants articulate political/policy/IR/government interest specifically AND understand the unique DC ecosystem advantage. Generic "I want to be near opportunities" answers fail.

**Elliott School of International Affairs** — top-5 IR program nationally; many students intern at embassies + think tanks. Direct admit.

**Trachtenberg School of Public Policy + Public Administration** — strong policy + government track.

**Columbian College of Arts & Sciences** + **School of Business** + **School of Engineering & Applied Science** + **Milken School of Public Health** + **Corcoran School of the Arts & Design** — six undergraduate-relevant schools.

Test-blind (since 2021). Distinguishes GWU from peer DC schools (Georgetown, American). Don't waste prep cycles for GWU specifically.

## Recent essay shifts
GWU supplements emphasize community fit + DC engagement. Articulate which DC organizations you'd want to intern at + why. Specific names + connection to your career narrative land.

## Post-SFFA stance
GWU maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + diverse student body.

## Demonstrated interest
GWU tracks demonstrated interest. Visit Foggy Bottom (right in the heart of DC, accessible by Metro). Engage with the school's social media, attend virtual sessions.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for GWU if it's a clear top choice — ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.5x for unhooked applicants)
- **Politics & Values Scholars** — competitive merit scholarship + policy-immersion experience
- **Stephen Joel Trachtenberg Scholars** — full-tuition + summer DC immersion experience for high-achieving DC public school students
- **University Honors Program** — selective; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming
- Strong programs: International Affairs (Elliott — top-5), Political Science, Public Health (Milken — top-15), Business, Computer Science, Engineering, Communication, Forensic Sciences, Art History (in collaboration with the Smithsonian), Anthropology
- **Mount Vernon Campus** — secondary 23-acre campus a few miles from main; quieter, residential feel; some students prefer it
- D-I athletics (Atlantic 10) — Colonials → Revolutionaries (mascot rebrand 2023); not the campus identity
- Greek life ~10-12% participation
- DC location: walking distance to White House, World Bank, State Department, embassies, Smithsonian, Lincoln Memorial — not exaggeration
- Strong international + diplomatic-family student community (~14% international)
- Cost is significant — sticker $85K+/year — meritsub-stantially offsets for top admits
- "GW politics culture" is real — many students do internship loops continuously throughout undergrad

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need on average (some packages include loans)
- Substantial merit: Presidential Scholarship (full-tuition + summer experience), Eckles Scholarship, Politics & Values Scholars, Trachtenberg, Center for Career Services Innovation Scholars
- District of Columbia residents: special tuition assistance via DCTAG
