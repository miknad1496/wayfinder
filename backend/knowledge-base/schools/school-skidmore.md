# Skidmore College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Skidmore admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,700 undergrad enrollment, Saratoga Springs NY
- Admit rate: ~30-35%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Skidmore supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Skidmore's identity: **"Creative thought matters"** is not a marketing slogan — it's the actual academic philosophy. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the interdisciplinary, arts-integrated, hands-on culture (vs Hamilton's writing-rigor identity, vs Vassar's intellectual-eccentric identity, vs Colgate's collegiate-classic identity).

Strong applicants demonstrate creative work in their portfolio of activities — a genuine creative project, not just "I'm in art club." Skidmore's distinctive academic structure — interdisciplinary majors, the First-Year Experience Scribner Seminar, the Tang Teaching Museum being academic infrastructure (not optional) — rewards applicants who specifically want this.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1340-1480, ACT 31-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Skidmore supplements ask "why Skidmore" + a short response that often involves a creative-thought prompt. Authentic specificity wins; generic "I love small classes" answers fail.

## Post-SFFA stance
Skidmore maintained holistic review post-SFFA with continued attention to first-gen, low-income, and underrepresented students.

## Demonstrated interest
Skidmore tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit (especially during Saratoga's beautiful spring/fall), virtual sessions, supplements that show understanding of the school's distinctive culture all count meaningfully.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **ED1 admit lift is significant** (~1.6-1.8x for unhooked applicants); ED2 admit lift smaller but still meaningful
- The **Saratoga Springs** location matters — small upstate NY city (50K), historic horse-racing town, very different from typical college-town vibes (more cultural / mature than party-college)
- 30-min drive to Albany; 3-hour drive to NYC and Boston — manageable for visits but isolated for daily life
- Strong programs: Art (Studio Art + Art History), Theater, Dance, Music, Business (with strong arts integration — distinctive), Environmental Studies + Sciences, Psychology, Neuroscience
- **Saratoga Race Course** is a real cultural touchpoint — the racing season brings the town alive late summer
- The **Tang Teaching Museum** is on campus and integral to many courses — exhibits become curriculum
- D-III athletics (Liberty League) — competitive but not the campus identity
- Greek life — none (Skidmore has not had Greek life since the 1960s — distinct from peer LACs)
- Strong international student presence (~10-12% of undergrad)
- New York Six consortium (Colgate, Hamilton, Hobart and William Smith, Skidmore, St. Lawrence, Union) — limited cross-registration

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions (international applicants: highly need-aware)
- Meets ~98% of demonstrated need on average — packages may include loans but generally good for LACs
- Modest merit scholarships: Filene Music Scholarship (audition), Porter Presidential Scholarship (full-tuition for top admits — very competitive)
- QuestBridge partner
