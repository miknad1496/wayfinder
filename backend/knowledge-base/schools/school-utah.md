# University of Utah — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Utah admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~25,000 undergrad enrollment, Salt Lake City UT
- Admit rate: ~85-88% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Utah institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Dec 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Apr 1

## What admissions actually weights
Utah's identity: Big 12 (transitioned 2024 from Pac-12) flagship + David Eccles School of Business + College of Engineering (computer graphics + entrepreneurship strong) + Pioneer Theatre Company + access to ski resorts (15-30 min from world-class skiing) + Mormon-cultural-adjacent (LDS heritage in SLC creates distinct social environment) + entrepreneur-friendly culture (Lassonde Entrepreneur Institute is a real differentiator). Strong applicants signal Honors + scholarship targeting + specific interest in tech entrepreneurship or engineering.

**Lassonde Entrepreneur Institute** — distinctive among public flagships. Lassonde Studios building is a 5-story dorm-and-makerspace. Students live AND build startups. Pierre Lassonde donated $25M+ to seed it. Undergrads pitch + iterate + launch real ventures. Top-3 entrepreneurship undergrad nationally per multiple rankings.

**Honors College** — automatic invitation for high stats; smaller seminars + Honors thesis.

**Eccles School of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; Entrepreneurship + Quantitative Finance + Finance strong.

**Computer Science** — Pixar's heritage runs through Utah (Ed Catmull was a faculty member); strong computer graphics tradition.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1170-1380, ACT 23-30.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Dec 1** for scholarship + Honors + Lassonde residence consideration
- **Eccles Scholar** — top automatic merit for high stats
- **Honors Scholar** — competitive separate scholarship
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package
- **Lassonde +X program** — first-gen + entrepreneurial students with mentorship + funding
- Strong programs: Computer Science (especially Computer Graphics — Pixar heritage), Engineering (Mechanical, Biomedical, Civil), Business (Eccles — esp. Entrepreneurship + Quantitative Finance), Music, Film & Media Arts (top-15), Architecture
- **Lassonde Studios + Entrepreneur Institute** — distinctive nationally; mandatory startup experience for interested students
- D-I (Big 12) — Utes football + basketball
- Greek life ~10-12% participation — modest
- **SLC setting** — capital city + mountains. **15 minutes from skiing** (Park City, Snowbird, Alta, Solitude, Brighton — 7 resorts within 45 min). 4 hours from Yellowstone. Outdoor lifestyle is the cultural anchor
- LDS-adjacent culture is real but campus is more secular than Provo (BYU). Religious diversity present
- Strong international student community
- Cost: in-state very low, out-of-state moderate

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Eccles Scholar, Honors Scholar, NMF package, College Engineering Scholarships, Lassonde Scholarships
- Utah residents get exceptional in-state pricing
- Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) — neighboring states reduced OOS pricing
