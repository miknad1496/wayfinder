# Syracuse University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~14,000 undergrad enrollment, Syracuse NY
- Admit rate: ~52% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Syracuse supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (binding)
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED / Jan 1 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Syracuse's identity: communications/journalism powerhouse + pre-professional + traditional + athletic culture. The Newhouse School of Public Communications is among the top undergraduate journalism programs nationally.

Schools with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts & Sciences
- Newhouse School of Public Communications (very competitive)
- Whitman School of Management
- College of Engineering and Computer Science
- College of Visual and Performing Arts

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1220-1410, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes
- ED bump real
- Newhouse Communications among strongest undergrad media programs nationally — admits write specific portfolios for journalism/PR/film/TV
- Whitman Business solid undergrad
- Athletic recruiting MAJOR (ACC basketball, football, lacrosse)
- Greek life ~25%
- Syracuse winters real (lake effect snow) — applicants who can't tolerate snow self-select out

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-aware admissions
- Coronat Scholars (full-tuition merit, very competitive)
- Founders Scholarship + Chancellor's Scholarship for high-achievers
- Newhouse-specific scholarships

## Recent essay shifts
- "Why Syracuse" expects portfolio-school specificity — generic "great communications school" answers underperform. Strong Newhouse essays cite specific tracks (broadcast journalism, magazine, PR, advertising) and Newhouse-affiliated experiences like Citrus TV or the Daily Orange.
