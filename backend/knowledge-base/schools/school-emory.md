# Emory University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Emory admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~7,000 undergrad enrollment, Atlanta GA
- Most recent admit rate: ~10-12% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Emory-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Emory's identity: pre-professional + Southern + global health emphasis (CDC/Emory Healthcare proximity) + collaborative culture. Strong applicants articulate specific career-pipeline interests, particularly in pre-med, public health, business (Goizueta), or pre-law.

Two undergraduate colleges: Emory College (Atlanta campus) and Oxford College (smaller campus, transition to Atlanta as junior). Oxford applicants face less competitive admissions but commit to the smaller campus first 2 years.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1450-1540, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Emory's supplements include "Why Emory?" — answers should reference specific academic resources (research opportunities at CDC, Goizueta Business School, Oxford option). Generic Atlanta-loves don't land.

## Post-SFFA stance
Emory's post-SFFA approach has been notable — significant first-gen and Pell-eligible enrollment, expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Emory DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment is strong signal; visits and info session attendance also factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump is significant (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked applicants)
- Oxford College vs Emory College choice: Oxford has slightly higher admit rate but requires 2 years at smaller campus (transitioning to Atlanta junior year)
- Goizueta Business School (junior + senior year only — students major in something else first 2 years, then transfer in)
- Pre-med pipeline very strong (Emory Healthcare integration, 1+ year clinical opportunities)
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports)
- Atlanta location: vibrant urban environment, growing tech sector, strong healthcare/finance industries

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $100K
- Generous merit aid: Emory Scholars Program (full tuition + research stipend), Liberal Arts Scholars, Goizueta Scholars
- QuestBridge partner
