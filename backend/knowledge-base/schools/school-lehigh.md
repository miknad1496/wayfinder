# Lehigh University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Lehigh admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~5,500 undergrad enrollment, Bethlehem PA
- Admit rate: ~32% overall, varies by college (Engineering ~30%, Arts & Sciences ~28%, Business ~25%)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Lehigh-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Lehigh's identity: pre-professional (business + engineering pipeline) + small + collaborative + traditional. Strong applicants articulate specific career-pipeline interests, particularly in finance, consulting, engineering, or pre-med.

Three undergraduate colleges with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts & Sciences
- P.C. Rossin College of Engineering and Applied Science
- College of Business

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1330-1490, ACT 30-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Lehigh supplements include "Why Lehigh?" — strong applicants reference specific programs, IBE program, Computer Science + Business dual-major, etc.

## Post-SFFA stance
Lehigh's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Lehigh DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment, visits, info session attendance all factor in. Lehigh is known for yield protection — overqualified applicants without demonstrated interest get rejected.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump is significant (~1.7-2x for unhooked)
- Yield protection real — visit if reasonable, ED if genuine top, write thoughtful Why-Lehigh
- IBE Program (Integrated Business and Engineering): 4-year combined degree, very competitive
- CSB Program (Computer Science and Business): another dual-degree pipeline
- Pre-business + pre-finance pipelines strong (Lehigh is well-represented at investment banks)
- Athletic recruiting modest (Patriot League, D1 but no scholarships in most sports)
- Greek life ~30%

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $50K
- Limited merit aid (Trustees' Scholarship — full tuition, very competitive)
