# University of Rochester — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Rochester admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,400 undergrad enrollment, Rochester NY
- Admit rate: ~36% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Rochester-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 + Early Action. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1, EA Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 5

## What admissions actually weights
Rochester's identity: strong music conservatory + research university + pre-med + flexible curriculum. Strong applicants articulate specific intellectual interests + Rochester's curriculum flexibility (no general education requirements except 1 cluster system).

Schools with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts, Sciences, and Engineering
- Eastman School of Music — separate audition-required admissions, world-class
- Simon Business School (graduate level mostly)
- School of Nursing (graduate level)

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1380-1500, ACT 30-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Rochester supplements include "Why Rochester?" — strong applicants reference cluster system, specific programs, Eastman cross-registration if relevant.

## Post-SFFA stance
Rochester's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Rochester DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment, visits, info sessions, EA enthusiasm all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 + EA all available. ED is binding, EA is non-binding non-restrictive
- Eastman School of Music: separate admissions process, audition required, world-class music conservatory
- AS&E flexibility: cluster system replaces traditional gen-eds. Students take 3 clusters (mini-majors) totaling 12 credits in different academic divisions
- Pre-med pipeline strong (Strong Memorial Hospital integration)
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports)
- Greek life ~10%
- Rochester location: small post-industrial city, lake-effect snow real
- Take Five: program lets undergrads take a 5th year tuition-free for additional study

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets ~85-90% of demonstrated need
- Generous merit aid: Renaissance Scholarship, George Eastman Scholarship, Dean's Scholarships
