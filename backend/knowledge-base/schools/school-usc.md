# University of Southern California (USC) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current USC admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~21,000 undergrad enrollment, Los Angeles
- Most recent admit rate: ~10-12% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App; USC-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding, non-restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 8

## What admissions actually weights
USC's identity: pre-professional + Trojan family network + entertainment industry pipeline + collaborative + LA location. Strong applicants articulate specific career pipeline interests (Marshall Business, Annenberg Comm, Dornsife Sciences, Viterbi Engineering, Cinematic Arts).

Multiple specialized undergraduate schools:
- Dornsife College of Letters, Arts, and Sciences — broad
- Marshall School of Business — competitive
- Viterbi School of Engineering — competitive, strong CS
- Annenberg School for Communication and Journalism — media/comm
- School of Cinematic Arts — film/TV, EXTREMELY competitive (~3-4% admit rate, audition/portfolio required)
- Roski School of Art and Design
- Iovine and Young Academy (entrepreneurship + technology + design)

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1450-1540, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
USC supplements include "Why USC?" plus several short answers. AOs look for genuine LA connection, specific program references, and "Trojan-fit" — students who'll thrive in USC's collaborative + alumni-network culture.

## Post-SFFA stance
USC's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. USC has strong first-gen enrollment.

## Demonstrated interest
USC DOES track demonstrated interest. Visits, info sessions all factor in. EA commitment is read positively but non-binding nature limits its weight.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- EA is non-binding, non-restrictive. Can apply ED1/ED2 elsewhere simultaneously
- USC Cinematic Arts is one of the most competitive programs in the world for film/TV
- Marshall Business is highly regarded undergrad business
- Trojan Network alumni connection is genuinely strong — USC alumni hire other USC alumni at notable rates
- Athletic recruiting significant (Pac-12 / Big Ten football, basketball, multiple sports)
- Greek life ~25% of campus
- LA location: entertainment industry proximity, internship advantages for media/film/business
- USC merit aid is generous — particularly the Trustee Scholarship (full tuition for ~125 students/year)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $80K
- Generous merit aid: Trustee Scholarship (full tuition), Presidential Scholarship (half tuition), Mork Family Scholars (full ride + stipend)
- QuestBridge partner
