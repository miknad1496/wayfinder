# Baylor University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Baylor admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private Baptist Christian R1 research university, ~14,500 undergrad enrollment, Waco TX
- Admit rate: ~55-60%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Apply Texas + Baylor supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
Baylor's identity: largest Baptist university in the world + Christian-affiliated (admits all faiths but Baptist heritage is significant) + Hankamer School of Business + strong Pre-Med + Honors College + Bears football culture + traditional Southern-Christian collegiate experience. Strong applicants signal genuine fit with the values-based community.

**Honors College** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated dorm.

**Hankamer School of Business** — direct admit by major; AACSB-accredited; strong placement in Texas finance + consulting + corporate.

**Pre-Med pipeline** — Baylor has strong placement to Texas medical schools (Baylor College of Medicine in Houston is SEPARATE — common confusion. The med school is in Houston, the undergrad is in Waco. They share the name but are administratively distinct).

Test scores: admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1410, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for priority scholarship + Honors consideration
- **Baylor Distinguished Scholarship** — full tuition + summer experience for top admits
- **Honors College** — competitive separate admission; thesis required
- **Provost Scholarship** — automatic for high stats
- **Religious heritage scholarships** — Baptist + other denominational; significant for Baptist students
- Strong programs: Business (Hankamer), Pre-Med + Sciences, Education, Music (School of Music), Engineering, Communication Sciences + Disorders, Religion + Theology
- **Christian-focused** — chapel attendance required for first-year students; explicit Christian identity in mission. Articulate fit with Baylor's mission in essays
- D-I (Big 12) — Bears football + basketball; basketball especially competitive (2021 men's NCAA champion)
- Greek life ~25-30% participation
- **Waco TX setting** — small city (140K), 90 min from Austin, 90 min from Dallas. Real college town
- Strong Texas + SW + Latino + Asian student communities
- Cost moderate — sticker $70K/year — meritsub-stantially offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~80% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: Baylor Distinguished (full tuition+), Provost Scholarship, Honors College awards
- Texas residents: in-state-equivalent pricing through some scholarships
