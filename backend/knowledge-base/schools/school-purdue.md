# Purdue University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~37,000 undergrad, West Lafayette IN
- Admit rate: ~57% in-state, ~62% out-of-state (relatively accessible by headline rate, but engineering + CS are MUCH lower)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 (Engineering effectively expects test scores)
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Purdue supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding (Nov 1)
- Deadline: Nov 1 EA / Jan 15 RD (priority for engineering scholarships is Nov 1)

## What admissions actually weights
Purdue is institutionally peculiar in three ways: (1) **Tuition has been FROZEN at the same in-state rate since 2012-13** (the "Purdue Promise" — Mitch Daniels-era policy continuing under current administration). For Indiana families, in-state tuition + frozen-rate guarantee make Purdue exceptionally affordable. For OOS, Purdue's OOS tuition is also notably lower than Big Ten peers. (2) **Engineering is the primary academic identity** — the College of Engineering is top-10 nationally (top-5 in Aerospace, Industrial, Civil) and admits ~30% of all undergraduates. Engineering applicants enter through First-Year Engineering (FYE) program — generic engineering admit, then internal placement to specific discipline after freshman year (Mechanical, Aerospace, etc. have GPA cutoffs ~3.2-3.5 for internal admission). (3) **Krannert/Mitch Daniels School of Business** — recently renamed (Daniels was past president of Purdue + governor of IN), top-25 business school, particularly strong in supply chain management + analytics. Direct admit competitive.

Strong programs: Engineering (top-10 overall, signature: Aerospace, Industrial, Mechanical, Computer, Agricultural & Biological), Computer Science (top-25, separately ranked from Engineering — admits via College of Science), Krannert Business, Pharmacy (top-10 nationally), Veterinary School (top-10 globally), Agricultural Sciences (signature land-grant strength).

Astronaut alumni — Purdue has produced more astronauts than any other public university (Neil Armstrong, Gus Grissom, Eugene Cernan, etc.). The "cradle of astronauts" identity is real and shapes engineering culture.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1190-1430, ACT 26-32. Engineering admits cluster much higher (~1390-1500 SAT median).

## Strategic notes
- APPLY BY NOV 1 EA for engineering scholarship consideration
- Engineering admit via First-Year Engineering (FYE) — internal placement to discipline after year 1
- Direct admit majors are competitive: CS (in College of Science, NOT Engineering), Animation Lab, Pharmacy
- Big Ten athletic culture (basketball especially — Mackey Arena, recent Final Four runs)
- Greek life ~17%, present but not dominant
- West Lafayette is a small college town — not a city; isolated central Indiana setting
- Strong international student presence (~17%, particularly Chinese + Indian + Korean)
- Purdue Polytechnic (formerly College of Technology) for hands-on tech tracks
- Purdue Global is a separate online university (don't confuse with the on-campus undergraduate experience)

## Aid + scholarship
- IN residents: in-state tuition ~$10K (frozen since 2012, dramatically lower than peer flagships)
- OOS: ~$28K tuition (low for Big Ten OOS)
- Trustees Scholarship + Presidential Scholarship — competitive merit awards for top admits
- 21st Century Scholars program for low-income IN residents
- Engineering-specific scholarships: Marquis, Beering, etc.
- John Martinson Honors College — separate selective application, full-tuition awards available

## Recent essay shifts
Purdue supplements are short and direct. The "Why Purdue" question rewards applicants who articulate Engineering specifically (not just "STEM"), who name the FYE program structure or specific engineering disciplines, and who reference the cost-frozen tuition + astronaut heritage authentically. Generic "great engineering school" essays underperform versus essays that demonstrate Purdue-specific research.
