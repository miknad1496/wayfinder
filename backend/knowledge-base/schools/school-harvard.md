# Harvard University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Harvard admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Ivy League, ~7,200 undergrad enrollment (Harvard College)
- Most recent admit rate: ~3.4-3.6% (Class of 2027 cycle, Harvard publishes)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — Harvard reinstated requirement April 2024 for the 2025-26 application cycle
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge accepted
- Early round: Restrictive Early Action (REA) — non-binding, restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Harvard's published philosophy: "we look for students with strong personal qualities, intellectual vitality, accomplishments outside the classroom, and the potential to contribute to the Harvard community." In practice, they assemble a class with extreme academic strength PLUS distinctive contribution potential — what AOs call "intellectual personality" plus "what you'd bring to the table at Harvard."

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1580, ACT 34-36.

The Harvard Personal Rating (1-6 scale, with 1 being highest) is a notable AO assessment — it captures character, leadership, and "personal qualities." Applicants with strong stats but weak personal-rating score get rejected. Strong personal-rating + strong stats = admit candidate.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Harvard's supplements were short for years; in 2023-24 cycle they expanded to require 5 short essays (~150 words each), shifting away from the prior "one main essay" model. The new format gives applicants more room to show different dimensions. Strong applicants treat each of the 5 prompts as a distinct opportunity — don't repeat themes.

## Post-SFFA stance
The June 2023 SFFA decision was specifically against Harvard's admissions practices. Harvard's post-SFFA approach has been particularly visible:
- Adversity essays expanded
- Race-conscious admissions ended; they now read for context-sensitive "lived experience" mentions
- Harvard has been explicit they won't use proxies for race
- Pell-eligible enrollment increased post-SFFA

## Demonstrated interest
Harvard does NOT consider demonstrated interest. The applicant pool is too large for visit-tracking to add signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- REA is non-binding, restrictive — can't apply ED to Penn/Columbia/etc. simultaneously
- Unhooked REA admit lift is modest (~1.1-1.3x vs RD) — pool is overwhelmingly hooked
- Athletic recruiting ~12-15% of admits
- Legacy admit boost was officially eliminated post-2023 cycle (Harvard ended legacy preference 2024)
- Harvard expects exceptional academic achievement; below-1500 SAT or below-3.95 unweighted GPA at competitive HS faces uphill battle

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for ALL applicants including international
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans
- Families under $85K income: zero parent contribution (most generous of any school in this band)
- Families $85K-$150K: parent contribution scales gradually
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
