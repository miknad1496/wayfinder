# Rutgers University–New Brunswick — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Rutgers admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~36,000 undergrad enrollment, New Brunswick NJ
- Admit rate: ~66-72% overall, but Rutgers Business School (RBS) ~50% and School of Engineering ~50-55% direct admit
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Rutgers institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for best scholarship + Honors + direct-admit consideration
- RD deadline: Dec 1 priority; final Mar 1

## What admissions actually weights
Rutgers's identity: large NJ flagship + Rutgers Business School (one of largest in the country, AACSB-accredited) + School of Engineering + School of Arts & Sciences + 5 New Brunswick residential colleges (the "campus system" — Cook/Douglass/Livingston/Busch/College Avenue). Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Business, Engineering, Pharmacy, Mason Gross arts) since those are competitive sub-admissions.

**Rutgers Business School (RBS) Direct Admit** — competitive for business applicants. Non-direct = pre-business through SAS, then internal transfer (achievable but not guaranteed).

**Mason Gross School of the Arts** — selective, audition/portfolio required for music, dance, theater, art.

**Pharmacy (PharmD)** — 6-year direct entry program, very competitive, separate application.

**Honors College** — automatic for high stats; smaller cohorts + Honors thesis + dedicated dorms.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1300-1490, ACT 28-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Rutgers supplements are minimal — primary focus is on demonstrating fit with chosen school within Rutgers (RBS, Engineering, Arts & Sciences, etc.).

## Post-SFFA stance
Rutgers continued holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to NJ residents + first-gen + diverse student body (one of most demographically diverse R1 publics in the country).

## Demonstrated interest
Rutgers does NOT formally track demonstrated interest. Campus visit is common for in-state students.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 1** EA for priority scholarship + Honors + direct-admit consideration
- **Honors College** — automatic invitation; one of the largest honors programs nationally. Includes Honors Living-Learning Community
- **Bloustein School of Planning + Public Policy** — strong undergrad public policy program, often overlooked
- **Mason Gross arts** — audition-required, top-tier MFA-feeder programs
- Strong programs: Pharmacy (PharmD direct entry — top in NE), Business (RBS), Engineering (Mechanical, Computer, Civil, BME), Computer Science, Public Health (top-25), Education, Nursing
- **Pharmacy direct entry** is THE play for pharmacy-bound students — separate competitive admission, 6-year accelerated program
- Big Ten athletics (since 2014) — Scarlet Knights football + basketball
- Greek life ~10-15% participation
- New Brunswick is a real college town integrated with the city — multiple campuses connected by bus system
- 45 minutes from NYC, 1 hour from Philadelphia — accessible, internship-rich
- Strong Korean + Asian + South Asian communities
- "Knights" + scarlet identity is real
- "RU Screw" tradition (students complain about bureaucracy at Rutgers — a real cultural phenomenon worth preparing for)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions (NJ residents prioritized for aid)
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (significant gap common for OOS)
- Substantial merit: Trustee Scholarship (full ride for top admits), Presidential Scholarship, Dean's Scholarship, Honors College Scholarship
- New Jersey residents get strong in-state pricing + NJ STARS / NJ STARS II / Tuition Aid Grant (TAG) for low-income NJ residents
- Out-of-state tuition is significant — model carefully
