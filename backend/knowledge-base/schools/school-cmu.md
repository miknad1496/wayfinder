# Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current CMU admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~7,500 undergrad enrollment, Pittsburgh PA
- Most recent admit rate: ~11-13% overall (varies by college: SCS School of Computer Science ~6%, Art ~25%)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; CMU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 3

## What admissions actually weights
CMU's identity: technical excellence + interdisciplinary + maker culture + arts integration. The "CMU applicant" tends to combine technical strength (CS, engineering, science) with creative pursuits OR arts excellence with technical depth.

Six undergraduate colleges with VERY DIFFERENT admit rates:
- School of Computer Science (SCS) — ~6% admit rate, among most competitive CS programs in world
- College of Engineering (CIT) — ~14%
- Mellon College of Science — ~22%
- Tepper School of Business — ~14%
- Dietrich College of Humanities and Social Sciences — ~26%
- College of Fine Arts (CFA) — ~25% but portfolio/audition required

CMU famously admits to specific colleges, not university-wide. Internal transfer between colleges (e.g., Dietrich to SCS) is very difficult.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1560, ACT 34-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
CMU supplements ask college-specific questions. The SCS supplement asks specifically about CS interest — generic answers don't land. Strong CS applicants demonstrate concrete experience (projects, hackathons, research, GitHub portfolio).

## Post-SFFA stance
CMU's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
CMU does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment matters significantly given admit rate gaps between colleges.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at CMU is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is significant (~1.7-2x for non-SCS; SCS ED still very competitive)
- COLLEGE CHOICE STRATEGIC: SCS at CMU is one of the world's most competitive CS programs (admit rate ~6%, comparable to Stanford CS). Applying to a less competitive CMU college (Dietrich, MCS) and trying to internal-transfer to SCS is very hard
- Tepper Business is highly regarded undergrad business
- CFA programs (Drama, Music, Art) require portfolio/audition; separate creative review
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports)
- Pittsburgh location: revitalized post-industrial city, growing tech sector (Google Pittsburgh office, Amazon, lots of robotics startups)
- The Greek life at CMU is small (~10%) and varies by college
- CMU CS culture is intensely technical/competitive

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $75K
- Limited merit aid (extremely competitive merit scholarships, mostly need-based packaging)
- QuestBridge partner
