# Trinity College (Connecticut) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Trinity admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,200 undergrad enrollment, Hartford CT
- Admit rate: ~33-37%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Trinity supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Trinity's identity: traditional NESCAC LAC + urban-Hartford location (distinctive among NESCAC's mostly-rural campuses) + strong economics + IDP (Individualized Degree Program — distinctive flexible curriculum option) + pre-professional tracks (Hartford insurance industry adjacency) + Greek-influenced social culture + traditional collegiate aesthetics. Strong applicants signal NESCAC fit + understand Hartford urban location is intentional rather than accidental.

**NESCAC consortium** — Trinity is among the 11 NESCAC LACs (Amherst, Bates, Bowdoin, Colby, Conn College, Hamilton, Middlebury, Trinity, Tufts, Wesleyan, Williams). Distinctive athletic + academic + cultural cohort.

**Hartford location** — distinctive within NESCAC. Most NESCAC schools are rural; Trinity is in Connecticut's capital city. Insurance industry internships + healthcare (Hartford Hospital) + corporate finance pipeline.

**Individualized Degree Program (IDP)** — distinctive among LACs. Students design their own major spanning multiple departments. Flexible structure for interdisciplinary thinkers.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1330-1480, ACT 30-33.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Trinity if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful (~1.4-1.6x)
- **Trinity Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- **Cleo Scholar** — first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship
- **Cornelia Cogswell Rossi Scholarship** — top merit + research
- **Tower Scholar** — selective scholarship + leadership programming
- Strong programs: Economics, History, Political Science, Philosophy, English, International Studies, Music (Trinity has historic music program), Public Policy, Pre-Health (Hartford Hospital pipeline), Engineering (3-2 dual-degree with Columbia + RPI)
- **3-2 Engineering Programs** — Trinity LAC + Columbia or RPI engineering = bachelor's from each. 5-year combined. Distinctive among LACs offering this
- D-III athletics (NESCAC) — Bantams; football historically strong among LACs
- Greek life ~25-30% participation — significant
- **Hartford CT setting** — capital city, mid-size (120K). 1.5 hours from NYC, 2 hours from Boston. Insurance industry capital
- Strong Korean + Asian + International + Latino student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $85K+/year — meets need fully for low-income, merit aid available

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: Trinity Posse Scholar, Cleo Scholar, Cornelia Cogswell Rossi Scholarship, Tower Scholar, Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
