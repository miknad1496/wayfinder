# Oberlin College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC + Conservatory, ~2,900 undergrad (College ~2,400 + Conservatory ~600), Oberlin OH (35 miles west of Cleveland)
- Admit rate: ~32%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Oberlin supplements (separate Conservatory audition required for Conservatory admit)
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 2) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 2 ED2 / Jan 15 RD (Conservatory has earlier audition deadlines)

## What admissions actually weights
Oberlin is institutionally distinct in five ways: (1) **Conservatory + College double-degree program**: Oberlin is one of very few institutions with a fully integrated music conservatory inside an LAC. The Double-Degree program lets students earn both a BA (College) and BMus (Conservatory) in 5 years — uniquely positioned. (2) **First U.S. coeducational college** (1837) AND first U.S. college to admit African-American students (1835). The institutional identity is genuinely progressive + activist. (3) **Strong activism culture**: Oberlin has been a destination for left-progressive students for decades. The college's role in the Underground Railroad, the Wellington Rescue (1858), and ongoing political activism shape student culture. Applicants who don't engage with this tradition feel like outsiders. (4) **One of the few selective LACs with both classical and popular music programs**: Oberlin's TIMARA program (Technology in Music and Related Arts) is signature. (5) **Allen Memorial Art Museum**: a major college art museum (~14,000 works) — students can borrow original artworks (Renaissance to contemporary) for $5/year as part of the Art Rental Program. Yes, undergrad students literally hang $50,000+ original paintings in their dorm rooms.

Strong programs: Music (Conservatory — top-3 U.S. music conservatory), Composition (signature), Music Theory, Politics, History, English, Creative Writing, Africana Studies, Environmental Studies, Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science (small but rigorous).

The "Obie" identity — progressive, intellectually serious, often quirky, frequently artistic — is real. Applicants must signal cultural fit.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1380-1510, ACT 31-34. Conservatory admit weighted heavily on audition; test scores secondary.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~50-55%; ED2 admit rate ~40%; RD ~25-28% — ED is meaningful leverage
- Conservatory admit is SEPARATE — audition is the primary admit factor, not test scores
- Double-Degree program: BA + BMus in 5 years, unique offering in U.S. higher ed
- Greek life: NONE (Oberlin abolished Greek system 1840s for inclusivity reasons)
- Active LGBTQ+ community (Oberlin has been LGBTQ+-affirming since the 1970s, decades before peers)
- Notable alumni: Stephen Sondheim (Conservatory drop-in courses), Yo-Yo Ma briefly, Liz Phair, Richard Strauss visited
- Distinctive features: Wilder Hall, Allen Art Museum, the Tappan Square (where Mr. Tappan's plot still preserved)
- "Co-op" residential dining: ~25% of students live in cooperative houses with shared cooking + governance

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan financial aid for low-income aid recipients
- Strong endowment per-student
- Conservatory has additional merit aid for top auditions
- John Frederick Oberlin Scholarship + various competitive merit

## Recent essay shifts
Oberlin's "Why Oberlin" supplement rewards applicants who engage with the activist + progressive + creative culture. Applicants who treat Oberlin as a backup or who don't engage with the Conservatory + College combination, the Underground Railroad heritage, or the contemporary activism culture score noticeably worse. Conservatory applicants must articulate a clear musical vision — generic "I love music" essays underperform.
