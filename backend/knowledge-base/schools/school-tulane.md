# Tulane University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Tulane admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~8,500 undergrad enrollment, New Orleans LA (uptown campus)
- Most recent admit rate: ~10-12% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App; Tulane-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 8
- RD deadline: ~Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Tulane's identity: distinctive Southern + cultural + service-oriented + strong public health/medicine pipeline + New Orleans location appeal. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with Tulane's culture (Tulane Public Service Program — TUPSP — requires every student to complete service work to graduate).

Five undergraduate schools (most students apply to first three):
- School of Liberal Arts
- School of Science and Engineering
- A.B. Freeman School of Business
- School of Architecture
- School of Public Health and Tropical Medicine (graduate only mostly, but undergrads can take coursework)

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1500, ACT 31-33.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Tulane supplements ask about service interests + Tulane-specific engagement. The Tulane Public Service Program is central to identity — applicants who reference it specifically land best.

## Post-SFFA stance
Tulane's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Tulane has been criticized for low Pell-eligible enrollment historically; has been working to increase.

## Demonstrated interest
Tulane DOES track demonstrated interest — among the most aggressive about it. ED commitment, visits, info sessions, opening admissions emails all factor in. Yield protection is real and significant at Tulane.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant (~1.7-2x for unhooked)
- Yield protection at Tulane is among the strongest of peer schools — high-stat RD applicants without demonstrated interest get rejected at notable rates. ED is the strongest defense
- Freeman Business is highly regarded undergrad business
- Pre-med pipeline strong (Tulane Medical School integration, Public Health proximity)
- Athletic recruiting significant (American Athletic Conference, football + basketball)
- Greek life ~30% of campus
- New Orleans location: vibrant culture, hurricane risk, distinctive food/music scene
- Service requirement: every Tulane undergrad must complete TWO service-learning courses to graduate (1 first year, 1 in major)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets ~80-90% of demonstrated need (less generous than peer schools)
- Loan-free for families under $75K
- VERY generous merit aid: Distinguished Scholar Award (full tuition), Stamps Scholarship (full ride + stipend), Paul Tulane Award (half tuition)
- Tulane is famous for using merit aid aggressively to attract high-stat applicants
