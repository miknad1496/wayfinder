# University of Tennessee Knoxville — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UTK admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~25,000 undergrad enrollment, Knoxville TN
- Admit rate: ~64-70% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + UTK supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Chancellor's Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Dec 15 priority; final May 1

## What admissions actually weights
UT Knoxville's identity: SEC flagship + Volunteers football culture + Haslam College of Business + Tickle College of Engineering + strong nuclear engineering (Oak Ridge National Laboratory adjacency) + Honors program + traditional Southern public collegiate + lower-cost public alternative for OOS via merit. Strong applicants signal Honors + scholarship targeting since the broad admit is accessible.

**Chancellor's Honors Program** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming + priority registration.

**Tickle College of Engineering** — direct admit; Nuclear Engineering distinctive (UT is one of top-3 nuclear programs nationally; Oak Ridge National Lab adjacency); Computer Science + Mechanical strong.

**Haslam College of Business** — AACSB-accredited; Supply Chain Management is top-3 nationally (Boyd Center for Business + Economic Research drives industry connections).

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1190-1370, ACT 25-31.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for scholarship + Chancellor's Honors consideration
- **Volunteer Scholarship** — full ride for top admits + summer + leadership
- **Tennessee Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package + stipend (UT is generous to NMF — though not as aggressive as Alabama)
- Strong programs: Engineering (Tickle — esp. Nuclear, Computer, Mechanical), Business (Haslam — esp. Supply Chain top-3), Communication + Information (top-25), Nursing, Architecture, Forestry + Natural Resources, Education
- **Oak Ridge National Lab** partnership — undergrads can intern + research at the national lab; distinctive STEM access
- D-I (SEC) — Volunteers (Vols) football + basketball; "Rocky Top" + checkerboard end zone Neyland Stadium
- Greek life ~25-30% participation
- **Knoxville TN setting** — mid-size city (190K), Smoky Mountains adjacent (45 min to Gatlinburg), 3 hours from Nashville + Atlanta
- "Big Orange" Vol identity is real
- Strong Korean + Asian + International student communities

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Volunteer Scholarship (full ride), Tennessee Scholar, NMF package, College Engineering Scholarships, Haslam Business Scholarships
- Tennessee residents get strong in-state pricing + HOPE Scholarship (lottery-funded; for TN HS grads with stats threshold) + Tennessee Promise (free community college bridge — relevant for transfer pipeline)
