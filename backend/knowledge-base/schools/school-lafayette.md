# Lafayette College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC + College of Engineering, ~2,700 undergrad, Easton PA (Lehigh Valley, 70 miles north of Philadelphia, 80 miles west of NYC)
- Admit rate: ~32%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Lafayette supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 15) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 15 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Lafayette is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **LAC + Engineering hybrid** — like Bucknell, Lafayette combines a traditional liberal arts college with an engineering college. ABET-accredited engineering disciplines: Civil, Chemical, Computer, Electrical, Mechanical, plus a unique "Engineering Studies" program (engineering-adjacent BA, not BS). The combination is uncommon; the "engineering at an LAC" model gives engineering students humanities depth. (2) **Marquis Scholars Program**: Lafayette's signature merit + leadership development program — full tuition + research + travel + cohort programming. ~40 admits/year, separate competitive application. The Marquis is comparable to Davidson's Belk or Vandy's Cornelius Vanderbilt scholarships. (3) **Lehigh-Lafayette rivalry**: The longest-running rivalry in college football (since 1884, 158 games and counting). The "Rivalry Game" is a cultural defining feature — students get tickets free, cross-town buses run between Easton and Bethlehem (Lehigh's home), and the game is broadcast nationally. (4) **East Coast traditionalist culture** — Lafayette has notable Greek life (~38%), preppy student culture, strong East Coast finance + government pipelines.

Strong programs: Engineering (Civil + Mechanical signature), Economics, Government & Law (signature pre-law program), History, English, Mathematics, Geology + Environmental Science, Religious Studies, Music.

Lafayette's "Leopard" identity — engineering-rigorous, Greek-active, East Coast traditional — is similar to Bucknell. Applicants should signal acceptance of this culture.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1320-1480, ACT 30-33. Engineering admits cluster higher.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~50-55%; ED2 admit rate ~40%; RD ~25% — ED is meaningful leverage
- Marquis Scholars Program: full-tuition merit, ~40/year, separate competitive application — high-leverage path
- Engineering direct admit is competitive but accessible
- Greek life ~38% — significantly central to social structure
- Lehigh-Lafayette rivalry is real institutional asset (and conversation starter)
- D1 athletics (Patriot League — same as Bucknell)
- Notable alumni: James A. Michener, Stephen Crane (briefly), William Marbury (of Marbury v. Madison fame, no relation)
- Distinctive features: Skillman Library, Pardee Hall, Hugel Memorial Chapel, the Quad
- Active alumni network in finance, engineering, government

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-aware for some applicants (Lafayette is partially need-blind, with a need-sensitivity threshold)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need for admitted students
- Marquis Scholars: full tuition + research + travel
- McKelvy Scholars + Trustee Scholarships
- Strong endowment per-student

## Recent essay shifts
Lafayette's "Why Lafayette" supplement rewards applicants who articulate fit with the LAC + Engineering combination, the Lehigh rivalry traditions, and the Marquis Scholars opportunity. Generic "small LAC near NYC" essays underperform. Engineering applicants should reference specific Lafayette engineering programs; pre-law applicants should reference the Government & Law department.
