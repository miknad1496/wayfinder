# Middlebury College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Middlebury admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,800 undergrad enrollment, Middlebury VT
- Admit rate: ~13-15% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Middlebury supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Middlebury's identity: top-tier languages + international relations + outdoorsy + politically engaged. Middlebury's languages program is famous — Middlebury Language Schools (graduate level) are #1 in the country for language immersion.

Strong applicants articulate language interest, international affairs engagement, OR environmental focus (Vermont environmental ethos is genuine). The Davis United World College Scholar Program brings significant international student community.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1520, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Middlebury supplements include "Why Middlebury?" + identity-related prompts.

## Post-SFFA stance
Middlebury's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Middlebury does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant. Visiting Middlebury (where feasible) is read positively given Vermont's relative isolation.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Significant ED admit lift (~1.5-1.7x for unhooked)
- Languages: 11 languages offered, including LANGUAGE PLEDGE programs in summer (live in target language)
- Athletic recruiting ~20-25% of admits (NESCAC, hockey + skiing + soccer significant)
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at Middlebury (eliminated 1990)
- Vermont location: rural, beautiful, isolated. Vermont winters are real
- Middlebury's "feb" admit: ~100 students per year admitted as Febs (start in February rather than September). Distinctive opportunity but adjusts the timeline

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants (with Davis UWC Scholarship for international students from UWC schools)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $80K
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
