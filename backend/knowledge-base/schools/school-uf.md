# University of Florida (UF) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~35,000 undergrad, Gainesville FL
- Admit rate: ~24% overall (one of the most selective public flagships in the SEC)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — Florida public universities never went test-optional during the pandemic and have not adopted it since
- Application: Common App + UF supplements
- Early round: NONE — single Nov 1 deadline for the entire applicant pool
- Deadline: Nov 1 hard. UF does NOT admit on a rolling basis after this date.

## What admissions actually weights
UF's admissions process has two non-obvious mechanics that shape outcomes: (1) Florida Bright Futures Scholarship — for FL residents with high stats (1330+ SAT for Florida Academic Scholar tier), it covers 100% of tuition + fees automatically. Combined with low FL public tuition base, this means top FL applicants attend UF essentially free. This radically changes who chooses UF: many high-stats FL students who could attend Ivy+ schools choose UF for the financial calculus, which in turn raises UF's admit profile. (2) UF requires test scores — the test-required policy filters the applicant pool toward stats-strong students and means UF's middle-50 SAT (1330-1480) is comparable to UVA/UNC despite the higher overall admit rate.

UF Innovation Academy, UF Honors Program, and PaCE (Pathway to Campus Enrollment — start online, then on-campus) are three distinct admit pathways. PaCE admits don't get on-campus access freshman year, which is a common admit-letter surprise — applicants should read the admit letter carefully.

Strong programs: Engineering (Herbert Wertheim), Warrington Business, Pharmacy, Animal Sciences/Veterinary, Journalism (Phillips Bowl), Pre-law track at the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences. UF Health (Gainesville) is a top academic medical center.

Test scores: REQUIRED. Middle-50 SAT 1330-1480, ACT 29-33. Bright Futures Florida Academic tier requires 1330 SAT or 29 ACT.

## Strategic notes
- ONE deadline (Nov 1) — applicants get all decisions in late February
- PaCE admits start online — read the admit letter; this is a common surprise for accepted students
- UF Innovation Academy is a spring-summer-only campus track (not the same as PaCE)
- Strong honors program (UF Honors) — separate competitive admit
- Big SEC athletics culture (football, basketball, gymnastics nationally competitive)
- Greek life ~15-20%, real but not dominant
- Gainesville is a mid-sized college town — hot/humid year-round, hurricane season real
- UF Health is one of the top academic medical centers in the southeast — strong pre-med pipeline

## Aid + scholarship
- FL residents: Bright Futures + Florida Prepaid + UF aid often = essentially free for high-achievers
- Lombardi Scholarship — full ride + stipend, very competitive (~50 awards/year)
- Stamps Scholarship at UF — highly selective, full-ride + research/travel stipend
- Benacquisto Scholarship — for National Merit Finalists who attend UF (matches Bright Futures + adds funding to total cost-of-attendance)
- OOS aid: limited; UF is need-aware for OOS, OOS tuition ~$28K vs in-state ~$6K

## Recent essay shifts
UF's "Why this major" supplement is the differentiator essay; AOs explicitly deprioritize generic "great university" answers. Florida-applicant essays often lean too hard on "I want to stay close to home for Bright Futures" — AOs read THROUGH that to find genuine major fit. Strong applicants name specific UF labs, specific Wertheim Engineering departments, specific Warrington tracks (Heavener finance honors, e.g.).
