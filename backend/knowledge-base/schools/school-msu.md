# Michigan State University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current MSU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~39,000 undergrad enrollment, East Lansing MI
- Admit rate: ~80-83% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or MSU-direct application; rolling admission with priority deadlines
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for best scholarship + Honors College + direct-admit consideration
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
MSU's identity: Big Ten land grant flagship + College of Education (top-5 nationally) + Eli Broad College of Business + College of Veterinary Medicine + Spartan athletics. Strong applicants understand MSU is broadly accessible BUT specific colleges (Broad business + James Madison residential college + Honors College) have more competitive direct-admit paths.

**Eli Broad College of Business** — direct admit ~50% of business applicants, competitive. Non-direct must transfer in via internal application sophomore year.

**James Madison College** (residential college focused on public + international affairs) is selective — separate application within the MSU app, GPA + essay focus.

**Honors College** — automatic invitation for high stats; competitive applications also accepted with separate essay.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1100-1310, ACT 23-29. Honors College trends ~80-150 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
MSU's main application is streamlined. Honors College + James Madison have additional essays — these matter and are read carefully.

## Post-SFFA stance
MSU maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to Michigan residents + first-gen students.

## Demonstrated interest
MSU does NOT formally track demonstrated interest. Campus visit common but not weighted.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 1** for priority scholarship consideration (significant for OOS) + Honors College + James Madison + Lyman Briggs + Broad direct admit
- **Lyman Briggs College** — selective residential STEM college within MSU; scientific reasoning emphasis; applies separately
- **James Madison College** — residential college focused on public affairs + international; separate competitive admission
- **Honors College** — automatic for high stats, also competitive for borderline. Honors thesis + smaller seminars + dedicated dorm
- Strong programs: Education (top-5), Supply Chain Management (top in nation), Hospitality Business, Veterinary Medicine, Packaging (unique), Communication Sciences, Communication Arts + Sciences (Journalism/Advertising)
- D-I athletics (Big Ten) — Spartans football + basketball + hockey. Strong basketball tradition
- Greek life ~12-15% participation
- East Lansing is a real college town — Grand River Avenue + downtown bars/restaurants
- 90 minutes from Detroit, 90 minutes from Grand Rapids, 4 hours from Chicago
- Korean + Asian + International student communities meaningful (~10% of undergrad international)
- "Spartan" identity is real — green-and-white culture is strong

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common for OOS)
- Substantial merit: MSU Distinguished Scholarship (full ride + summer abroad + leadership stipend — most prestigious; Dec 1 deadline), MSU Academic Scholarships, Honors College Scholarships, College of Engineering Scholarships
- Michigan residents get strong in-state pricing
- Spartan Advantage Initiative — ~free tuition for low-income MI residents
