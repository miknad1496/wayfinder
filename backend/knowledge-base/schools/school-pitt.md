# University of Pittsburgh — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Pitt admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~20,000 undergrad enrollment, Pittsburgh PA (Oakland neighborhood — campus blends with city)
- Admit rate: ~50-60% overall, but Honors College is ~10-15%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Pitt-direct application; rolling admission until April 1 (priority deadlines drive earlier review)
- Early action: Non-binding EA available; priority deadline early November for best aid + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: rolling, but apply by Dec 1 for priority

## What admissions actually weights
Pitt's identity: urban public R1 + premed powerhouse + healthcare ecosystem (UPMC) + Honors College as elite pipeline + strong international students. Strong applicants understand that Pitt operates on **rolling admissions** — applying early matters as much as essay quality.

**Premed track** is the differentiator. Pitt sends substantial numbers to medical school (top-10-ish nationally for med-school placement). Honors College premed is competitive with elite privates for med school admissions outcome, at significantly lower cost.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-33. Honors College trends ~80-100 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
Pitt's main application is fairly streamlined. Honors College has additional essays — these matter and are read carefully.

## Post-SFFA stance
Pitt's post-SFFA approach maintained holistic review with continued focus on first-gen + Pennsylvania residents.

## Demonstrated interest
Pitt formally tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit, virtual session attendance, email engagement all count.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY November 1** for best aid + Honors consideration. Rolling = the early bird really gets the worm here
- Honors College: separate application, separate review. Apply if eligible. Honors students get priority registration, smaller seminars, dedicated dorm (Honors community in Forbes Hall), enhanced research access
- Premed: ~60-70% of Pitt premed applicants get into med school (national avg ~40%), and outcomes from Honors College premed are higher still. UPMC clinical access gives early hospital exposure
- Strong programs: Premed/Health Sciences, Engineering (Swanson School), Computer Science, Business (Katz/CBA), Philosophy of Science (top program globally), Public + Urban Affairs
- Campus is integrated with the city — Cathedral of Learning is the iconic spot. Squirrel Hill, Shadyside, Strip District nearby
- Strong Korean + international student communities
- D-I athletics (ACC) — basketball + football. Roar from the Steel City
- Greek life present but not dominant (~10-12% participation)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially for many students (significant gap is common — model carefully)
- Substantial merit scholarships including: Chancellor's Scholarship (full ride, top ~3-5 admits), Stamps Scholarship, Nordenberg Scholarship (Honors leadership). Apply early for best chance
- Pennsylvania residents get significantly lower in-state tuition
