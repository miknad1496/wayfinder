# Fordham University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Fordham admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private Jesuit Catholic R2 research university, ~9,800 undergrad enrollment, NYC (Rose Hill in the Bronx + Lincoln Center in Manhattan)
- Admit rate: ~53-60%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Fordham supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- Early decision: Binding ED, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Fordham's identity: NYC location + Jesuit liberal-arts foundation (formation of the whole person, "men and women for others") + Gabelli School of Business + strong communication/journalism + two distinct campus experiences (Rose Hill = traditional residential collegiate + Lincoln Center = urban commuter-style). Strong applicants signal Jesuit-values fit AND specific campus preference.

**Two-campus model — pick one for application:**
- **Rose Hill** — Bronx, traditional 85-acre Gothic campus, ~6,500 undergrads, residential collegiate vibe
- **Lincoln Center** — Manhattan, urban tower campus, ~3,300 undergrads, commuter + diverse + arts-focused, near Lincoln Center performing arts complex

Each has distinct admissions process within the Fordham app.

**Gabelli School of Business** — strong NYC pipeline to investment banking + finance + consulting + Big-4 accounting. Direct admit.

**Communication and Media Studies / Film and Television** — Lincoln Center campus has strong media/film tracks; significant alumni in NYC media/entertainment.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
Fordham supplements emphasize community fit + values articulation. Articulating Jesuit values (cura personalis = "care for the whole person", magis = "more/greater service") in your own terms — without being preachy — is the Fordham move.

## Post-SFFA stance
Fordham maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + Pell-eligible + Hispanic/Latino students (Fordham is one of the largest Hispanic-Serving Institutions in the NE).

## Demonstrated interest
Fordham tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit (visit BOTH campuses if possible — they really differ), virtual sessions matter.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED Nov 1** for Fordham if clear top choice — ED admit lift ~1.4-1.6x
- **Cunniffe Presidential Scholar** — full-tuition + summer abroad + research + leadership
- **Hispanic Founders Scholars** — for Latino/Hispanic students with strong academic profiles
- **Honors Program** — both campuses have it; smaller seminars + Honors thesis
- Strong programs: Business (Gabelli), Communications + Media Studies, Theater (Lincoln Center campus has BFA programs that compete with Tisch), Film & TV, Psychology, Political Science, International Studies, Performing Arts (combined with Alvin Ailey + Ailey/Fordham BFA Dance Program — distinctive)
- **Ailey/Fordham BFA Dance** — joint program with Alvin Ailey American Dance Theater. Audition-required. One of the strongest pre-professional dance pipelines
- D-I athletics (Atlantic 10) — Rams; football has a strong tradition (Vince Lombardi played at Fordham)
- Greek life — none (Fordham eliminated Greek life in the early 1980s)
- NYC location: Rose Hill 30 min subway to Manhattan; Lincoln Center IS Manhattan
- Strong Korean + Asian + Hispanic + Italian-American + Irish-American student communities
- Cost is significant — sticker $85K+/year — but merit aid is reasonable

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~88-92% of demonstrated need on average (some packages include loans)
- Substantial merit: Cunniffe Presidential (full-tuition+), Loyola Scholarship, Dean's Scholarship, Founders Scholars, Faber Scholars (Jesuit-track)
- New York residents have access to TAP for partial relief
