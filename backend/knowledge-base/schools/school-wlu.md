# Washington and Lee University (W&L) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current W&L admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC + School of Law (graduate), ~1,900 undergrad enrollment, Lexington VA
- Admit rate: ~17-22%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + W&L supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
W&L's identity: highly selective Southern LAC with **Honor System** that's actually enforced (no proctored exams, take-home tests, dorm doors unlocked) + strong Williams School of Commerce, Economics & Politics + journalism (top undergrad J-school in country, particularly through the Knight Center) + law school feeder + traditional preppy Southern collegiate culture + Greek life centrality. Strong applicants signal genuine fit with Honor culture + Southern collegiate norms.

**The Honor System is real, not aspirational** — students cite it as the defining academic experience. Take-home final exams, unproctored exams, no plagiarism software (you don't need it because nobody cheats). This is THE cultural anchor — articulate why it appeals to you.

**Williams School of Commerce, Economics, and Politics** — distinctive from peer LACs which often don't offer business as undergrad major. Strong placement at finance + consulting + corporate strategy.

**Department of Journalism + Strategic Communication (Knight Center)** — top-3 undergrad journalism nationally. Many alumni at NYT, WSJ, Bloomberg.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
W&L supplements include questions about Honor and community + scholarship-fit responses for top merit candidates. Articulate Honor in your own words — what does academic + personal honor mean to you and how have you lived it. Generic answers fail.

## Post-SFFA stance
W&L maintained holistic review post-SFFA. The Honor culture creates a distinct community profile that requires explicit articulation in essays.

## Demonstrated interest
W&L tracks demonstrated interest. Visit Lexington (small town in Shenandoah Valley), engage with the school's social media.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for W&L if clear top choice — ED admit lift is significant (~1.5x)
- **Johnson Scholarship** — full ride + summer stipend + leadership development. Top-3 academic scholarship in the country for an LAC (some say top-5). Apply by Dec 1 — separate competitive review including interview
- **Lewis Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Honor Scholars** — for students who demonstrate exceptional academic + character profile
- Strong programs: Journalism + Strategic Communication (top-3), Politics, Economics + Business (Williams School), Accounting (CPA pipeline), History, Art History, Geology, Mathematics, Computer Science (newer, growing)
- **Greek life is significant** — ~80% of women + ~75% of men join sororities/fraternities. This is THE cultural anchor outside academics. Worth understanding before applying
- D-III athletics (ODAC) — Generals
- **Lexington VA setting** — small Shenandoah Valley town (7,000 residents). 3.5 hours from DC, 2 hours from Charlottesville. True isolated college town (some students cite this as positive — focused academic environment; others as drawback — limited diversity)
- Traditional Southern collegiate culture — preppy, formal, gentlemanly
- Strong Korean + Asian + International + Latino student communities (smaller than NE LACs but real)
- Cost significant — sticker $85K+/year — Johnson Scholarship + need-based aid offsets for top admits

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: Johnson Scholarship (full ride + summer stipend — most prestigious), Lewis, Honor Scholars, Coxe Scholarship, Spirito Scholarship
- W&L has one of the largest merit-aid programs of any top LAC (vs need-only schools like Amherst/Williams)
