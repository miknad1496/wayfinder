# NC State University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current NC State admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~26,000 undergrad enrollment, Raleigh NC (Research Triangle)
- Admit rate: ~40-46% overall, but Engineering direct admit ~30-35% and Design ~20-25%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + NC State supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Oct 15 (priority for Park Scholars + Engineering direct admit)
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
NC State's identity: applied-research land grant university + Engineering + Design + Veterinary Medicine + Textiles (top in the country) + Research Triangle ecosystem. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Engineering, Design, Wilson College of Textiles specifically) since those are competitive sub-admissions vs the more broadly accessible majors.

**Direct admit to Engineering** is the admissions decision for engineering applicants. The College of Engineering does direct admit by major (CS, Mech, Aero, etc.) — non-direct = First Year College then internal transfer (competitive, GPA + course thresholds).

**College of Design** is even more selective — admissions includes a portfolio/audition for Architecture, Industrial Design, Graphic Design.

**Wilson College of Textiles** — distinctive nationally; only top-3 textile college in the U.S.; direct admits gets fashion-tech + materials science + textile engineering tracks.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
NC State supplements include "Why NC State" + a short program-specific prompt for Design / Textiles applicants. Engineering apps need to articulate why this school's project-based approach (Senior Design Project) vs others.

## Post-SFFA stance
NC State maintained holistic review post-SFFA. North Carolina Promise + continued first-gen/Pell support.

## Demonstrated interest
NC State does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but campus tour participation is common for in-state students.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Park Scholars Program** — full ride + summer experiences + leadership development. Application opens early Oct, deadline mid-Nov. Top-10 scholarship in the country for academic merit. Apply if NC State is a serious target
- **Caldwell Fellows** — full leadership development scholarship, separate app
- **Honors College** — automatic invitation for high stats, smaller seminars + Honors thesis
- Strong programs: Engineering (esp. Computer, Mech, Aero, Materials, Industrial), Design (Architecture, ID), Wilson College of Textiles, Veterinary Medicine, Agriculture + Life Sciences, Statistics + Data Science
- D-I athletics (ACC) — Wolfpack basketball + football. Strong basketball heritage
- Research Triangle (RTP) ecosystem — IBM, SAS, Cisco, GSK, Cisco — co-op + internship pipeline is unmatched in the SE
- Greek life ~12-15% participation
- Beautiful Brickyard center campus + Hillsborough Street college vibe
- Strong tracks in pre-med + pre-vet — UNC + Duke nearby for clinical access

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- North Carolina Promise — for qualifying low-income NC residents at participating UNC system schools, tuition $500/semester
- Substantial merit scholarships: Park (full ride), Caldwell, College Achievement Scholarships, Goodnight Scholars (STEM-focused)
- North Carolina residents get exceptional in-state pricing — among the lowest tuition for any flagship public
