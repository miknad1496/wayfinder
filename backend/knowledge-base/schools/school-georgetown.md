# Georgetown University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Georgetown admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Catholic Jesuit, ~7,500 undergrad enrollment, Washington DC
- Most recent admit rate: ~12-13% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — Georgetown never went test-optional permanently
- Application: GEORGETOWN-SPECIFIC application (NOT Common App), separate process
- Early round: Restrictive Early Action (REA) — non-binding, restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 10

## What admissions actually weights
Georgetown's identity: Jesuit + DC location + international affairs + pre-professional. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with Georgetown's mission ("educating men and women for others"), often through service work or international experience.

Four undergraduate schools with distinct admissions:
- Georgetown College (Arts & Sciences) — broad academics
- McDonough School of Business — pre-business pipeline
- Walsh School of Foreign Service (SFS) — international affairs, very competitive
- School of Nursing & Health Studies — healthcare focus

SFS is famously competitive; international affairs orientation, language requirement, Capstone thesis. Strong applicants for SFS articulate specific international interests + relevant experience.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1540, ACT 32-34. Georgetown DOES NOT superscore (sends all scores from all sittings).

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Georgetown supplements include short essays specific to each school. The "Why Georgetown?" prompt rewards specific knowledge of programs, not generic DC-loves. SFS applicants need a thoughtful international affairs angle.

## Post-SFFA stance
Georgetown's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Georgetown has historically had strong Pell-eligible enrollment and continues commitment.

## Demonstrated interest
Georgetown DOES track demonstrated interest meaningfully. Visiting (where feasible), info sessions, REA commitment all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- REA non-binding restrictive — can't apply ED elsewhere. SCEA-equivalent
- Georgetown does NOT use Common App — separate Georgetown application required
- Georgetown SFS is one of the most competitive single programs in college admissions
- McDonough Business School: top-tier business pipeline
- Athletic recruiting significant (Big East basketball — primary athletic identity)
- DC location: unique access to internships at federal agencies, NGOs, think tanks. Significant career advantage for political science/IR/policy careers
- Catholic identity is genuine but less prescriptive than Notre Dame

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants (limited international aid)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $75K
- Limited merit aid: Georgetown Scholars Program (full ride for ~50 students/year)
- QuestBridge partner
