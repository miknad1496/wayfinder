# University of Georgia — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~30,000 undergrad, Athens GA (75 miles east of Atlanta)
- Admit rate: ~37% in-state, ~52% out-of-state
- Test policy: REQUIRED (Georgia Board of Regents policy — UGA never went test-optional permanently; reinstated requirements 2024)
- Application: Common App + UGA supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding (Oct 15)
- Deadline: Oct 15 EA / Jan 1 RD

## What admissions actually weights
UGA has three institutional features that shape admissions: (1) **HOPE + Zell Miller Scholarships** — Georgia state lottery-funded merit aid for GA residents. HOPE covers 90% of tuition for GA students with ~3.0 HS GPA. Zell Miller Scholarship covers 100% of tuition + fees for GA students with 3.7 HS GPA + 1200+ SAT (~26+ ACT). For top GA students, UGA tuition is effectively zero — radically changing who applies and who matriculates. (2) **The Foundation Fellowship** is UGA's signature elite-merit award — full ride + $9K/year discretionary stipend + travel/research funding + access to a residential learning community. It's the single most valuable LAC-tier merit award at any large public university (~30 awards/year, separate competitive application). (3) **Honors College** is selective and admits via separate application during the Common App process; ~600 students/year, distinctive coursework + thesis requirement.

Strong programs: Terry College of Business (top-25 public, particularly strong insurance, real estate, MIS), Grady College of Journalism + Mass Communication (top-10 nationally — particularly strong public relations), Pharmacy (top-15), Veterinary Medicine, Agricultural Sciences (signature land-grant strength), Public + International Affairs (Carl Vinson Institute strong policy connections), Music (top-25 — Hodgson School of Music).

UGA's "Bulldog Nation" sports culture is genuinely central (football especially — Sanford Stadium seats 92,000+, recent national championships in 2021 + 2022). The Athens music scene (R.E.M., B-52's, Drive-By Truckers, Widespread Panic all originated here) creates a distinctive college-town culture.

Test scores REQUIRED: middle-50 SAT 1280-1450, ACT 28-32. Foundation Fellow + Honors admits cluster ~1480+.

## Strategic notes
- APPLY BY OCT 15 EA for scholarship consideration + Honors College review + Foundation Fellowship invitation
- Zell Miller Scholarship is essentially full ride for top GA students — radically changes value calc
- Foundation Fellowship is the elite-merit path (~30/year, separate application)
- Honors College admit is separate competitive (~600/year), thesis required
- Terry Business + Grady Journalism are notable program-direct admits
- Athens GA is a defining college-town: live-music scene, restaurants, historic district
- Greek life ~25%, significant social structure
- D1 SEC athletics — football is genuinely central to campus culture

## Aid + scholarship
- GA residents: HOPE Scholarship (90% tuition for 3.0+ HS GPA) + Zell Miller (100% tuition + fees for 3.7 GPA + 1200 SAT)
- OOS: ~$33K tuition, modest institutional aid
- Foundation Fellowship: full ride + $9K stipend + research travel — separate selective application
- Charter Scholarship + various competitive merit
- Pell + state grants stack for low-income GA residents

## Recent essay shifts
UGA's "Why UGA" supplement rewards specific engagement with the university (named professors, programs, traditions like the Arch, the Chapel Bell, GoDawgs!). Foundation Fellowship applicants get an additional rigorous essay supplement — that's where AOs read for intellectual ambition + research interest + "Foundation Fellow type" character (not just stats).
