# Macalester College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,100 undergrad, Saint Paul MN (Twin Cities urban setting)
- Admit rate: ~28%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Macalester supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 1) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 1 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Macalester is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **Internationalism is the institutional identity**, not a buzzword. Macalester is consistently in the top-3 U.S. colleges for percentage of international students (~14% — higher than most peer LACs); the campus has a permanent UN flag; the institution was a founding member of the Association of American Colleges' International Programs. The "internationalist" identity dates to the 1950s when then-president Charles Turck made it explicit, and AOs read essays for engagement with this. (2) **Twin Cities urban location**: Saint Paul + Minneapolis is a major metro (~3.7M people), and Macalester students have direct access to the cities for internships, cultural activities, restaurants — markedly different from the rural-LAC peer set (Williams, Amherst, Carleton). (3) **Strong service-learning + activism culture**: Macalester is one of the more politically progressive selective LACs (peer to Wesleyan, Oberlin, Reed in left-progressive student culture). Civic engagement is central. (4) **DeWitt Wallace Library + the Reader's Digest legacy**: Macalester received a transformative gift from the DeWitt Wallace Foundation (founder of Reader's Digest) — funded a substantial endowment expansion + the library + multiple programs.

Strong programs: International Studies (signature program, naturally), Economics, Geography (one of strongest LAC geography departments — including GIS + cartography), Mathematics + Statistics (signature — Macalester Mathematics Olympiad legacy, top Putnam team for a small school), Political Science, Environmental Studies, Russian + Slavic (signature — strong language tradition).

Macalester's "Mac" identity — international, intellectually serious, urban, progressive — is a distinctive cultural fit.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1370-1500, ACT 31-33. Submission rate ~50% for admits.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~45-50%; ED2 admit rate ~35-40%; RD ~25% — ED is significant but not as dramatic as at Williams/Carleton (since base admit rate is ~28%)
- Saint Paul + Minneapolis access: Light rail to Minneapolis, Twin Cities metro, multiple Fortune 500 HQs (Target, 3M, U.S. Bancorp, General Mills)
- Greek life: NONE (Macalester has no Greek system)
- Strong international student community + study-abroad culture (~70% of students study abroad)
- Active progressive politics + LGBTQ+ community
- Distinctive features: Old Main building, Macalester Plymouth Congregational Church, Lampert Building (recent), Janet Wallace Fine Arts Center
- Strong Peace Corps placement rate (consistently one of top per-capita Peace Corps producers)
- Kofi Annan attended Macalester briefly — alumni connection celebrated

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants (need-aware for international)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need for U.S. + Canadian + Mexican applicants
- DeWitt Wallace Distinguished Scholarship — competitive merit, ~50/year
- Catharine Lealtad Scholarship for first-gen + diverse backgrounds
- Strong study-abroad funding (Macalester International Roundtable + annual conference)
- Posse Foundation partnership

## Recent essay shifts
Macalester's "Why Macalester" supplement explicitly asks how internationalism + multiculturalism fit the applicant. AOs read for genuine engagement with international perspectives (not just travel) — applicants from diverse backgrounds, who've engaged with global issues, who articulate why "internationalism as institutional ethos" matters score noticeably better than generic "small LAC near a city" essays.
