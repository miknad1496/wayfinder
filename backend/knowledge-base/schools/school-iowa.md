# University of Iowa — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Iowa admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~22,000 undergrad enrollment, Iowa City IA
- Admit rate: ~83-86% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Iowa institutional application; rolling admission
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: May 1

## What admissions actually weights
Iowa's identity: Big Ten flagship + Tippie College of Business + College of Engineering + **Iowa Writers' Workshop** (THE premier creative writing MFA in the country — connected via Iowa Center for the Book and undergrad creative writing tracks) + Hawkeyes athletics + traditional Midwest public + low cost. Strong applicants signal Honors targeting + specific interest in Creative Writing or pre-med.

**Iowa Writers' Workshop** — graduate-level, but undergrad creative writing tracks at Iowa benefit from the institutional culture + faculty visiting from the Workshop. **For undergrad creative writing, Iowa is among the top-3 in the U.S.**

**Honors at Iowa** — automatic invitation for high stats; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming.

**Tippie College of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; one of the strongest finance + accounting programs in the Midwest.

**Iowa College of Public Health** — top-15 nationally; strong undergrad pipeline.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1170-1390, ACT 23-29. Honors trends ~100-150 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for priority scholarship + Honors consideration
- **Old Gold Scholarship** — top automatic merit for high stats
- **Iowa Scholars Award** — second-tier
- **National Scholars Award** — for OOS high-stats students; meaningful tuition reduction
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package
- **Presidential Scholar** — selective; full-ride competitive scholarship
- Strong programs: Creative Writing (top-3 undergrad nationally), Business (Tippie), Engineering, Public Health, Communication Sciences + Disorders, Pharmacy (PharmD), Nursing, Pre-Med (Iowa pipeline to medical schools)
- D-I (Big Ten) — Hawkeyes football + basketball + women's basketball (Caitlin Clark heritage); wrestling top-tier
- Greek life ~15-20% participation
- **Iowa City IA setting** — quintessential college town (75K), University dominates economically + culturally. UNESCO City of Literature designation
- Strong Korean + Asian + International student communities
- Cost: in-state very low, out-of-state moderate

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Old Gold, Iowa Scholars, National Scholars (OOS), NMF package, Presidential Scholarship, Tippie Direct Admit Scholarships
- Iowa residents get strong in-state pricing + Iowa Tuition Grant (state-funded need-based aid)
- Midwest reciprocity (Wisconsin, Minnesota, Nebraska partial) — limited but real
