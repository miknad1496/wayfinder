# Brandeis University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Brandeis admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~3,600 undergrad enrollment, Waltham MA (Boston suburb)
- Admit rate: ~36% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Brandeis-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Brandeis' identity: Jewish-founded + secular + intellectually rigorous + social justice oriented + small. Founded 1948 as a non-sectarian Jewish-sponsored university. Strong applicants articulate engagement with Brandeis' distinctive values — intellectual integrity, justice, community.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1370-1500, ACT 31-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Brandeis supplements include "Why Brandeis?" + identity-focused prompts.

## Post-SFFA stance
Brandeis' post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Brandeis DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment, visits, info sessions all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked)
- 4+1 BS/MS programs in business + computer science available
- Strong international relations + politics programs (proximity to Hyde Park-style Brandeis tradition of public intellectual engagement)
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports, no athletic scholarships)
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at Brandeis (no fraternities/sororities)
- Waltham location: Boston suburb, 30-40 min train to Boston
- Heller School of Social Policy + Management graduate-level integration

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Limited no-loan threshold
- Generous merit aid: Justice Brandeis Scholarship, Wien International Scholarship (full ride for international), Presidential Scholarship
