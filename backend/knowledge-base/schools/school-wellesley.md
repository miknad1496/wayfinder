# Wellesley College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Wellesley admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, women's college, ~2,400 undergrad enrollment, Wellesley MA (Boston suburb)
- Admit rate: ~13-15% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Wellesley supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 8

## What admissions actually weights
Wellesley's identity: women's college + intellectually rigorous + alumni-network powerful + politically engaged. Wellesley's mission focuses on educating women who will make a difference in the world. Famous alumni: Hillary Clinton, Madeleine Albright, Diane Sawyer.

Wellesley admits women + non-binary students who are comfortable in a women's college environment. Trans women are admitted; trans men generally not (admissions policy explicitly addresses gender identity questions).

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 31-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Wellesley supplements include "Why Wellesley?" + identity-focused prompts. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with single-gender education + Wellesley's specific community.

## Post-SFFA stance
Wellesley's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Wellesley does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Significant ED admit lift (~1.4-1.6x for unhooked)
- MIT cross-registration: Wellesley students can take classes at MIT (and vice versa). Significantly expands STEM access
- Wellesley's MIT shuttle service is a real practical thing (5 round-trips/day during academic year)
- Athletic recruiting ~15% of admits (Seven Sisters athletic conference, NEWMAC)
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at Wellesley (single-gender college tradition)
- Wellesley's alumni network is among the strongest of any college, particularly for women in business/politics/media
- Wellesley-Boston commute: 30 min by car, 45 min by T

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $100K
- Davis Degree Program (older students) has separate aid
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
