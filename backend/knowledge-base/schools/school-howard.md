# Howard University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 HBCU (Historically Black College/University), ~9,000 undergrad enrollment, Washington DC
- Admit rate: ~37% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Howard supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding
- Deadline: Nov 1 EA / Feb 15 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Howard is the most prestigious HBCU and a top historically Black research university. Strong applicants articulate engagement with Howard's mission of educating Black leaders. The "Mecca" identity is genuine — Howard has educated Thurgood Marshall, Toni Morrison, Kamala Harris, and many Black leaders.

Schools with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts & Sciences
- School of Business
- College of Engineering and Architecture
- College of Communications
- School of Education
- College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1180-1350, ACT 22-29.

## Strategic notes
- HBCU identity central — applicants should engage with Howard's mission
- Howard Bison athletic identity (MEAC conference)
- Greek life — Divine Nine fraternities/sororities historically central to campus culture
- Pre-law + pre-med pipelines very strong
- DC location internship advantages
- Howard Hospital integration for clinical opportunities

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-aware admissions
- Generous merit aid for high-achieving Black students
- Capstone Scholarship for top admits (full tuition/major scholarship)
- Founder Scholars, Presidential Scholars + various
- Howard also participates in QuestBridge Match for low-income high achievers

## Recent essay shifts
- Howard supplements ask applicants to engage with Howard's mission ("The Mecca," educating Black leaders) — generic "diverse community" answers underperform. Strong supplements engage with a specific Howard tradition (Divine Nine, a specific alum, a Howard institution like the Hilltop newspaper or Hospital).
