# Yale University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Yale admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,500 undergrad enrollment
- Most recent admit rate: ~3.7-4.5% (Class of 2027, Yale publishes)
- Test policy: SAT/ACT REQUIRED as of 2025-26 cycle — Yale reinstated requirement February 2024
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge accepted; Yale-specific supplements
- Early round: Single-Choice Early Action (SCEA) — non-binding, restrictive (cannot apply ED elsewhere). Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 2

## What admissions actually weights
Yale's stated philosophy emphasizes "intellectual curiosity, character, and engagement with the world." Their admissions reads heavily for evidence of self-directed learning — students who pursue interests beyond the classroom because they're genuinely curious, not for credential. The Why-Yale essay is high-leverage: AOs publicly say they can tell within 2 sentences whether the writer has done real research on Yale or is recycling generic prestige language.

Yale's recent SAT-required announcement (Feb 2024) included an unusually candid statement about admissions: scores are diagnostic of academic preparation in context, particularly helpful for applicants from under-resourced schools where strong scores demonstrate capability that wouldn't otherwise be visible. The framing positions test-required as pro-equity, not anti.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 is roughly 1500-1560 SAT, 33-35 ACT.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Yale's supplements are short — emphasis on authentic voice over polish. The "tell us about a community you belong to" prompt landed in 2023-24 and has been generative. Yale AOs publicly criticize over-coached essays; they say they can detect adult-coached prose vs authentic teenage voice and treat the former skeptically.

## Post-SFFA stance
Yale's post-SFFA approach has been to lean harder into context-sensitive reading of applications. The supplements now have more room for applicants to discuss community/identity context if relevant. Yale has been explicit that they will not use proxies for race in admissions decisions.

## Demonstrated interest
Yale does NOT consider demonstrated interest in admissions decisions. Like Stanford, the applicant pool is too large for visit-tracking to add signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- SCEA is non-binding BUT restrictive — can't apply ED to Penn, Columbia, etc. simultaneously. If your top is a school WITH ED, going SCEA at Yale forfeits that ED slot
- The unhooked SCEA admit lift is modest (~1.1-1.3x vs RD) — pool is overwhelmingly hooked
- Yale College has 14 residential colleges — students randomly assigned, becomes core social structure. Some applicants reference this in essays; many don't and that's fine
- Yale-NUS (Singapore) is a separate institution and being phased out — don't reference for current admissions
- Athletic recruiting is significant (~12-15% of admits), particularly for swimming, fencing, lightweight crew, and Ivy-League fall sports

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international (in transition — Yale moving toward need-blind for international)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for all admitted students
- Families under ~$75K income pay nothing
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
