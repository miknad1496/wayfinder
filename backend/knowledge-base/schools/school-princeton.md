# Princeton University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Princeton admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Ivy League, ~5,500 undergrad enrollment
- Most recent admit rate: ~4.5-5.5% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: REQUIRED for 2025-26 cycle (returned to required)
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge accepted
- Early round: Single-Choice Early Action (SCEA) — non-binding, restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Princeton's stated philosophy emphasizes "intellectual engagement" and "service to humanity" (the school motto: "Princeton in the Nation's Service"). They look for students who pursue ideas with genuine intellectual depth and demonstrate care about something beyond themselves. The supplemental essays are critical — Princeton publishes more supplements than most peers (5-7 short answer + 2-3 medium essays in recent cycles).

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1570, ACT 33-35.

The "Princeton Profile" tends toward students with: clear academic passion (often visible from early HS), evidence of independent intellectual pursuit (research, deep self-directed projects), and meaningful contribution to a community. Generic high-achiever profiles get rejected at high rates here even with strong stats.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Princeton's supplements include the famous "engineering essay" for engineering applicants and a strong civic-engagement question for all applicants. The "Tell us about a time you advocated for something you cared about" prompt is high-leverage — answers that show genuine action (not abstract values) land best.

## Post-SFFA stance
Princeton issued a measured statement after SFFA, similar to peer Ivies. Their adversity-essay opportunity expanded slightly. Princeton has been publicly committed to socioeconomic diversity (high Pell-eligible enrollment) and continues that emphasis.

## Demonstrated interest
Princeton does NOT consider demonstrated interest in admissions decisions.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- SCEA is non-binding but restrictive — can't apply ED elsewhere. Forfeit the ED slot at Penn/Columbia/Brown/Cornell etc.
- The unhooked SCEA admit lift is modest (~1.1-1.3x vs RD)
- A.B. (humanities + soft sciences) and B.S.E. (engineering) are separate admit pathways. B.S.E. is more competitive for unhooked applicants
- Athletic recruiting is ~14-15% of admits — significant for Ivy lightweight crew, fencing, etc.
- Princeton has eliminated loans for ALL students on aid, regardless of family income — strongest aid policy of the Ivies for middle-income families
- The Princeton thesis (required for graduation) is unique among Ivies — only school where undergrad thesis is universal

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and (since 2024) ALL international applicants — the only Ivy fully need-blind for international
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL students (most generous Ivy aid)
- Families under $100K: zero parent contribution
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
