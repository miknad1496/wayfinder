# Virginia Tech — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current VT admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~30,000 undergrad enrollment, Blacksburg VA
- Admit rate: ~57-65% overall, but Engineering direct admit ~30-40% of engineering applicants
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + VT supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- Early decision: Binding ED1, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
VT's identity: large public engineering powerhouse + Corps of Cadets military tradition + Hokies athletics + collegial small-town vibe in Blacksburg. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Engineering or Pamplin Business specifically) since those are competitive sub-admissions.

**Direct admit to Engineering (College of Engineering)** is THE admissions decision for engineering applicants. Direct admits go through a "Galipatia" or general-engineering first year, then declare specific major (Aerospace, Computer Engineering, Mechanical, etc.) sophomore year. Non-direct must transfer in via internal application — competitive.

**Direct admit to Pamplin College of Business** similar — direct admits get business core Day 1.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1230-1410, ACT 27-32. Engineering trends ~50-100 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
VT supplements include the **"Ut Prosim" (That I May Serve) prompt** — articulate how you'd contribute to the VT motto. This is the differentiator. Generic service answers fail; specific projects + reflection on what you learned land.

## Post-SFFA stance
VT continued holistic review post-SFFA with continued attention to first-gen + Virginia residents.

## Demonstrated interest
VT does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but campus visit (especially during Hokie weekend) is common for in-state students.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for Engineering/Pamplin direct admit + best scholarship consideration
- **Corps of Cadets** is real — ~1,100 cadets, military tradition with Army/Navy/AF/Marine ROTC tracks. Optional, very tight community
- **Hokie Stone** campus aesthetic — distinctive limestone buildings, real visual identity
- Strong programs: Engineering (esp. Aerospace, Computer, Civil, Mechanical), Architecture, Business (Pamplin), Veterinary Medicine, Forestry + Natural Resources, Hospitality + Tourism
- Pamplin direct admit ~40% of business applicants
- D-I athletics (ACC) — Hokies football is THE campus event
- Greek life ~15-18% participation
- Strong international student community
- "Hokies" mascot + culture — unique nickname
- Blacksburg is a small town (45K) with VT being the dominant institution — true college town
- 4 hours from DC, 4 hours from Atlanta, 5 hours from Charlotte — accessible but felt isolated by some

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions (Virginia residents prioritized for aid)
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (significant gap common)
- Substantial merit scholarships: Presidential Scholarship (full ride for top admits), Jerome A. Niles Faculty Scholarship, Pamplin Leader Scholarship, Honors College stipends
- Virginia residents get strong in-state pricing + Virginia Guaranteed Assistance Program (VGAP) for low-income VA residents
- Out-of-state tuition is significant — model carefully
