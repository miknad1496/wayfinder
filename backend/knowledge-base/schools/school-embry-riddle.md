# Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Embry-Riddle admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private aviation/aerospace-focused R2 research university, ~5,000 undergrad enrollment at Daytona Beach FL (residential, primary campus); also Prescott AZ campus + worldwide online
- Admit rate: ~67-72% (Daytona); ~75-80% (Prescott)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Embry-Riddle institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: rolling; Jan 15 for priority

## What admissions actually weights
Embry-Riddle's identity: **THE aviation + aerospace university in the U.S.** (no public flagship comes close in pilot training + aviation engineering specialty depth) + College of Aviation (Bachelor of Science in Aeronautical Science = professional pilot pathway with FAA certifications integrated into the degree) + College of Engineering (Aerospace Engineering — ~1/3 of all U.S. aerospace bachelor's degrees come from ERAU) + College of Business + cyber programs + flight simulator + airport-on-campus access. Strong applicants signal aviation/aerospace seriousness AND understand the pilot vs engineering tracks differ significantly.

**Two primary residential campuses:**
- **Daytona Beach FL** — primary, larger (~5,000 undergrads). Aerospace Engineering + College of Aviation (pilot training) + Cyber + Business
- **Prescott AZ** — smaller (~2,500 undergrads). Aviation + Aerospace Engineering + Global Security + Forensic Psychology

**College of Aviation (Aeronautical Science)** — distinctive professional pilot pathway. Students earn FAA certifications (Private Pilot, Instrument, Commercial, Multi-Engine, CFI, ATP-eligibility) integrated into the bachelor's degree. Cost is significant due to flight time (~$60K+ above tuition for flight fees), but ROI is major airline pilot pathway.

**Aerospace Engineering** — top-3 nationally; ~1/3 of all US aerospace BS degrees are awarded here. Strong NASA + Boeing + SpaceX + Lockheed pipeline.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1140-1330, ACT 23-29.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for scholarship consideration
- **Eagle Scholarship** — top automatic merit
- **Aerospace Engineering scholarships** — competitive, separate review
- **ROTC scholarships** — ERAU has Air Force + Army + Navy ROTC; can offset tuition substantially
- **Veterans benefits** — ERAU is military-friendly; significant veteran community
- Strong programs: Aviation (professional pilot pathway), Aerospace Engineering, Computer Science (Cyber), Mechanical Engineering, Aviation Maintenance Sciences, Air Traffic Management, Unmanned Aerial Systems, Forensic Psychology (Prescott), Global Security & Intelligence Studies (Prescott)
- **Costs to budget**:
  - Daytona tuition + room/board: ~$60-70K/year
  - **Flight fees additional ~$60K-90K total over 4 years for the pilot pathway** (Aeronautical Science majors). Engineering majors don't pay these
- D-I (Sun Belt) — Eagles
- Greek life small (~5%)
- **Daytona Beach setting** — beach 1 mile, near Daytona International Airport (operations training site)
- Strong international student community (especially from Asia + Latin America + Middle East — international pilots train there)
- **NOT a traditional liberal arts experience** — explicitly applied/professional curriculum
- Pilot career placement at major airlines (Delta, United, American) is the pipeline; rotorcraft + corporate aviation also strong

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Eagle Scholarship, Aerospace Engineering Scholarships, ROTC packages, Veterans benefits
- **Pilot pathway financial planning is critical** — model flight fees + fuel costs separately from tuition
