# Olin College of Engineering — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private specialty engineering college, ~400 undergrad (one of the smallest engineering schools in the U.S.), Needham MA (Boston metro, 11 miles west of Boston)
- Admit rate: ~14-16% (with ~25% yield — extremely high for a small school)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Olin supplements + Candidates Weekend (interview/group activities — REQUIRED for finalists)
- Early round: ED (Nov 15) — non-restrictive
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED / Jan 1 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Olin is institutionally distinct in five ways no other U.S. engineering school matches: (1) **Founded 2002 with no faculty tenure track and a project-based curriculum from day 1**. Olin reconstructed engineering education from scratch — no traditional lectures, no separate engineering disciplines (Olin awards a single "Engineering" BS with concentrations in Mechanical, Electrical/Computer, Bioengineering, Computing, or Robotics), entire curriculum built around hands-on team-based projects. The "Olin model" influenced engineering education globally. (2) **Candidates Weekend is the central admit experience**: Finalists are invited (free, all expenses paid) for a 2-day Candidates Weekend in February — they participate in design challenges, group activities, and interviews. The CW determines admit decisions; standardized testing + GPA are secondary. AOs explicitly hire FOR collaborative + creative + technical aptitude observed during the weekend. (3) **No traditional grades** — Olin uses a four-tier rubric (E/N/B/D where N = no credit, B = below standard, D = ditto, E = exceptional but the system has been simplified) and provides extensive faculty feedback. The lack of letter grades is intentional. (4) **All students receive ~50% tuition scholarship** — the Olin Scholarship is automatic and equal for all admitted students (originally a full-tuition scholarship at founding; reduced in 2010 to ensure institutional financial sustainability). (5) **Cross-campus partnerships**: Olin students have full cross-registration at Wellesley + Babson + Brandeis (the "Wellesley-Olin-Babson" consortium), giving Olin engineers access to humanities + business + social sciences they don't have on campus.

Strong programs: Engineering (single BS with concentrations in Mechanical, Electrical/Computer, Bioengineering, Computing, Robotics, Sustainable Energy), Affiliated Arts + Humanities + Entrepreneurship (cross-registered).

The Olin "type" — collaborative engineering, project-based learning, interdisciplinary by design — is a specific cultural fit. Applicants must show genuine interest in non-traditional engineering education.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1490-1560, ACT 33-35. Submission rate ~70%+ for admits.

## Strategic notes
- Candidates Weekend is THE admit decision — applicants who don't engage well don't get in regardless of stats
- 50% Olin Scholarship for all admits — combined with need-based aid, Olin is among the most-affordable selective engineering schools
- Greek life: NONE (Olin has no Greek system)
- ~400 students total — every student knows every other student; cohort is intensely tight
- Notable alumni: many in early-stage startup founding (Olin alumni have founded ~30+ companies), some in academia, several MIT Media Lab affiliates
- Distinctive features: the Olin Way (institutional approach to engineering education), Crescent Manufacturing, Olin Hall, Milas Hall

## Aid + scholarship
- 50% Olin Scholarship: AUTOMATIC for all admits
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants beyond the scholarship
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need above the 50% guarantee
- No-loan policy for low-income aid recipients
- Strong endowment per-student

## Recent essay shifts
Olin's "Why Olin" supplement specifically asks how the project-based + collaborative + interdisciplinary engineering model fits the applicant. Generic "I want to be an engineer" essays underperform. AOs read for whether the applicant has actually thought about non-traditional engineering education. The Candidates Weekend is the dominant admit factor; supplements are the gateway to CW invitation.
