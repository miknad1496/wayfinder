# Auburn University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Auburn admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~26,000 undergrad enrollment, Auburn AL
- Admit rate: ~70-75% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Auburn institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Dec 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
Auburn's identity: Tigers football + traditional Southern public + Harbert College of Business + College of Engineering (especially Aerospace + Mechanical) + College of Veterinary Medicine + Auburn Family culture (alumni network is fierce). Strong applicants signal Engineering or Business direct-admit targeting since those are competitive sub-admissions.

**Harbert College of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited.

**College of Engineering** — direct admit by major; Aerospace (Auburn has strong NASA + DoD connections) and Mechanical particularly strong.

**Honors College** — automatic invitation for high stats; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated dorm.

**College of Veterinary Medicine** — Auburn is one of top vet schools nationally; 4+4 (undergrad + DVM) or direct entry programs.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1180-1340, ACT 24-30.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Dec 1** for scholarship + Honors consideration
- **Spirit of Auburn Scholarship** — full ride for top admits + leadership + research
- **Presidential Scholarship** — top automatic merit
- **Foundation Scholarship** — second-tier merit
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed merit package
- Strong programs: Business (Harbert), Engineering (Aerospace, Mechanical, Civil), Veterinary Medicine, Architecture (5-year B.Arch), Pharmacy (PharmD), Forestry + Wildlife Sciences, Hospitality Management, Education
- D-I (SEC) — Tigers football + basketball; "War Eagle" battle cry; Iron Bowl (vs Alabama) is THE cultural event
- Greek life ~30% participation — significant
- **Auburn AL setting** — small college town (~75K including students), Alabama-Georgia border. 90 min from Atlanta, 2 hours from Birmingham
- "Auburn Family" identity is real — alumni network hires aggressively
- Strong Korean + Asian + International + Latino student communities

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Spirit of Auburn (full ride), Presidential, Foundation Scholar, NMF guaranteed package
- Alabama residents get strong in-state pricing
- Out-of-state pricing competitive with merit; less aggressive scholarship engine than Alabama-Tuscaloosa but still strong
