# Scripps College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Scripps admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private women's LAC, ~1,100 undergrad enrollment, Claremont CA (Claremont Colleges Consortium / 5C + Seven Sisters)
- Admit rate: ~25-32% (less competitive than Pomona/CMC/Mudd within the 5Cs but more rigorous than national average)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Scripps supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Scripps's identity: women's college + 5C consortium + humanities-and-arts heritage + serious intellectual culture (the "Core" interdisciplinary humanities sequence is required and famous). Strong applicants articulate why they specifically want a women's college experience AND why the 5C structure adds rather than dilutes that.

The Core: every Scripps first-year takes a 3-semester interdisciplinary humanities sequence covering "issues that have shaped global civilizations." This is the academic spine and a real differentiator vs Pomona/CMC.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1380-1500, ACT 31-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Scripps supplements ask why Scripps specifically (vs the other 4 Cs nearby) and invite identity-and-context discussion. Post-SFFA the prompts continue to invite adversity essays and identity-formation reflection without race-asking.

## Post-SFFA stance
Scripps's post-SFFA messaging affirmed continued commitment to admitting diverse classes including women of color, first-gen women, low-income women.

## Demonstrated interest
Scripps does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but the supplements reward applicants who can articulate the women's college difference AND why this matters to them.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED admit lift particularly meaningful here (RD admit rate is significantly lower than ED)
- Seven Sisters heritage: Scripps + Wellesley + Smith + Mount Holyoke + Bryn Mawr + Barnard + Vassar (Vassar is co-ed now). Cross-registration agreements exist
- 5C consortium: Scripps students cross-register at Pomona/CMC/Mudd/Pitzer freely
- Strong programs: Politics + Philosophy + Economics (PPE), Studio Art, Media Studies, Psychology, Biology (pre-med pipeline)
- Beautiful campus — gardens, Mediterranean architecture, considered one of America's most beautiful campuses
- Student culture: more intellectually serious than Pomona's, more collaborative than CMC's
- Athens-Sparta tradition: weekly campus tea (genuine, not a parody)
- Athletic teams: Claremont-Mudd-Scripps (CMS) Athenas in NCAA D-III SCIAC

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need (some packages include loans)
- Merit scholarships available — including the James E. Scripps Scholarship (~$25K/yr), more generous than most peer LACs
- QuestBridge partner
