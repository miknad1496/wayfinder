# Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current RPI admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 STEM-focused, ~6,000 undergrad enrollment, Troy NY
- Admit rate: ~57% (Class of 2027 cycle — much higher admit rate than peer STEM schools)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + RPI-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
RPI's identity: oldest technological university in U.S. (founded 1824) + STEM-focused + research-heavy + pre-professional engineering. Strong applicants combine technical strength with specific engineering/CS pipeline interests.

Five undergraduate schools with distinct admissions:
- School of Engineering (largest, most students)
- School of Science
- Lally School of Management
- School of Architecture
- School of Humanities, Arts, and Social Sciences (smallest)

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1370-1500, ACT 30-33.

## Recent essay shifts
RPI supplements include "Why RPI?" — strong applicants reference specific engineering programs, research opportunities, the IDEA program (interdisciplinary entrepreneurship).

## Post-SFFA stance
RPI's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
RPI DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment, visits, info sessions all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump is real (~1.4-1.6x for unhooked)
- The higher overall admit rate (~57%) vs peer STEM schools (Caltech ~3%, MIT ~4%, CMU SCS ~6%) reflects RPI's larger size + slightly less selective applicant pool. Still legitimate elite-tier engineering education
- IDEA Program (Inventor's Studio): semester-long entrepreneurship + design course
- Co-op options available but not as central as Northeastern
- Athletic recruiting significant (Liberty League D3, but RPI hockey is D1 with strong tradition)
- Greek life ~35% (high for elite STEM school)
- Troy NY location: smaller industrial city, near Albany. Real winter

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets demonstrated need
- Generous merit aid: Rensselaer Medal (full tuition), Rensselaer Leadership Award, Dean's Scholarships
- RPI is famous for using merit aid aggressively
