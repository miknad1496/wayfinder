# Babson College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private business-only college, ~2,400 undergrad, Wellesley MA (Boston metro, 14 miles west of Boston)
- Admit rate: ~22-24%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Babson supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 1) + ED2 (Jan 5) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 1 ED1 / Jan 5 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Babson is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **THE U.S. business-only college** — Babson is the only U.S. selective college that offers ONLY business-related degrees. There is no English major, no History major, no Biology. Every student earns a Bachelor of Science in Business with a concentration (Entrepreneurship, Finance, Marketing, Strategic Management, etc.). The institutional focus is total. (2) **Entrepreneurship is the institutional identity** — Babson has been ranked #1 in undergraduate entrepreneurship by U.S. News for ~30 consecutive years. The "Foundations of Management & Entrepreneurship" (FME) course is taken by every freshman: teams of ~30 students start an actual business with $3,000 funding, run it for the full academic year, and donate profits to charity at year-end. Every Babson grad has launched a real business. (3) **Wellesley-Olin-Babson Consortium**: cross-registration at Wellesley (humanities + science) and Olin (engineering) — Babson students take English/Bio/Math/Engineering courses elsewhere, ensuring intellectual breadth despite the business-only major. (4) **Strong international student presence**: Babson is one of the most international U.S. colleges (~30% international students), reflecting the global business orientation.

Strong programs: Entrepreneurship (consistently #1 nationally), Finance (signature program — strong investment banking + private equity placement), Marketing, Strategic Management, Operations Management, Analytics + Data Science, International Business, Sustainability.

The Babson "type" — entrepreneurial, business-focused, often international, willing to be in a single-discipline school — is a specific cultural fit.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1330-1480, ACT 30-32.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~50-55%; ED2 admit rate ~40%; RD ~20% — ED is meaningful leverage at Babson
- FME (Foundations of Management & Entrepreneurship) is a freshman-year defining experience — every student
- Wellesley-Olin-Babson cross-registration provides academic breadth
- Greek life: ~25% (significant social structure)
- Notable alumni: Akio Toyoda (Toyota CEO), Edward Brennan (Sears CEO), Elizabeth Holmes (Theranos — left briefly), William Cohen (former US Sec Defense), Michael Dukakis (former MA governor)
- Distinctive features: Trim Hall, the Sorenson Center for the Arts, Olin Hall (the original Babson; not Olin College's), the Globe (rotating outdoor sculpture)
- Strong alumni network in finance, consulting, family offices, entrepreneurship
- Roger Babson (founder) was a fascinating polymath — gravity-research-pioneer + business educator

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-aware for U.S. applicants (Babson is partially need-blind; need-sensitive for some applicants)
- Meets ~85-90% of demonstrated need
- Trustee Scholarship: full-tuition merit, separate competitive application
- Babson Diversity Leadership Scholarship
- Strong endowment per-student given size

## Recent essay shifts
Babson's "Why Babson" supplement specifically asks how the entrepreneurship-only focus fits the applicant. Generic "I want to study business" essays underperform versus essays that articulate genuine entrepreneurial vision (e.g., naming a real business idea, real problem the applicant has worked on, real entrepreneurial activity in HS). FME is the conversation starter — applicants who reference what they'd contribute to/gain from FME score better.
