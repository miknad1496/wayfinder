# Bucknell University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC + College of Engineering (uniquely combined), ~3,800 undergrad, Lewisburg PA (central PA, 3 hours from Philadelphia/Pittsburgh/NYC)
- Admit rate: ~30%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Bucknell supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 15) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 15 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Bucknell is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **LAC + Engineering hybrid** — Bucknell is officially a "university" but functions as an LAC for most departments PLUS has a full College of Engineering with 7 ABET-accredited engineering disciplines. This combination is rare — most LACs don't have engineering, most engineering schools don't have LAC humanities depth. Bucknell threads the needle. (2) **Engineering admit is direct** — students apply to specific engineering disciplines (Mech, Civil, Chem, Biomedical, Computer, Electrical, Environmental). Internal transfer between disciplines is straightforward; transfer FROM engineering TO Arts & Sciences is also accommodated. (3) **Strong Greek system + traditional culture** — Bucknell has ~40% Greek participation, traditional Christian heritage (Baptist founding 1846, no longer formal), and a generally more pre-professional + East Coast traditionalist culture than peer LACs. The "Bucknell type" tends preppy. (4) **Lewisburg PA is small but charming** — historic central-PA town, walkable downtown, real (if small) cultural scene. Not as isolated as Kenyon's Gambier or Bates' Lewiston.

Strong programs: Engineering (College of Engineering — top-30 undergrad-only engineering program in U.S., signature: Mechanical, Civil, Chemical, Biomedical), Economics, Management (signature LAC business adjacent program), Animal Behavior (rare program), Biology, Chemistry, English, History, Music + Music Performance, Psychology.

The Bucknell "Bison" identity — engineering-rigorous, Greek-active, East Coast traditional — is real cultural fit. Applicants should signal acceptance of this culture.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1330-1470, ACT 30-33. Engineering admits cluster ~1430+.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~50-55%; ED2 admit rate ~40%; RD ~25% — ED is meaningful leverage
- Engineering direct admit is competitive but accessible for strong STEM applicants
- Greek life ~40% — significantly higher than typical LAC; central to social structure
- D1 athletics (Patriot League — football, basketball, etc.) — unusual for a school this size
- Notable alumni: Christy Mathewson (baseball HOF), William Bucknell himself (founder), Marc Mero (WWE wrestler)
- Distinctive features: The Quad, Bertrand Library, Coleman Hall, the "Bucknell Forest" 350-acre nature preserve
- Active alumni network in finance, engineering, healthcare

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants (mostly — has need-aware tiebreak for some applicants)
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need (not 100%)
- Bucknell Scholars: full-tuition merit, ~30/year, separate competitive application
- Posse Foundation partnership for Posse Scholars
- Engineering-specific scholarships available

## Recent essay shifts
Bucknell's "Why Bucknell" supplement rewards applicants who articulate fit with the LAC + Engineering hybrid + Greek-active culture. Engineering applicants who name specific Bucknell engineering departments score better. Applicants who treat Bucknell as a "Lehigh backup" or who don't engage with the Greek/traditional culture score worse.
