# University of Washington Seattle — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~36,000 undergrad, Seattle WA
- Admit rate: ~46% in-state (WA), ~38% out-of-state (UW is unusual — OOS slightly less competitive than in-state by raw rate, though OOS pool is self-selecting)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + UW supplements (NOT the UC application — UW uses Common App, distinct from the UC system despite both being West Coast publics)
- Early round: NONE — single deadline only
- Deadline: Nov 15 priority / no formal "final" — earlier is better

## What admissions actually weights
UW reads holistically but capacity-weights by major. The "direct admit" mechanic is the single biggest thing applicants miss: most majors at UW (especially Allen Computer Science, Foster Business, Engineering, and Nursing) admit students DIRECTLY into the major from high school. If you're not direct-admitted, you enter as "pre-major" and have to re-apply to the major sophomore year — and that internal admit can be MORE competitive than freshman admit, especially for CS and Foster.

Allen School direct CS admit is one of the most competitive freshman admits in the country (~5-7% admit rate, comparable to MIT/Stanford CS). Foster direct admit is similarly competitive (~10-12%). For pre-major applicants targeting those programs, GPA in pre-reqs (CSE 142/143 for CS, ECON 200 for Foster) and the second-stage essay matter more than the freshman application.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1240-1450, ACT 28-33. Median in-state admit GPA ~3.85 unweighted, OOS ~3.9.

## Strategic notes
- Direct-admit majors: Allen CS, Foster Business (direct + standard), Computer Engineering, Bioengineering, several Engineering disciplines, Nursing (one of the hardest direct admits in the country)
- Husky Promise: full tuition for WA residents under ~$80K family income — significant aid lever for in-state families
- WA has Running Start (community college dual enrollment) — many WA applicants enter UW with 30-90 transferable credits, which compresses degree time but does NOT improve admit odds
- UW joined Big Ten in 2024 (was Pac-12) — athletic recruiting profile evolving
- Seattle weather is real for OOS applicants — gray winters October-April are a fit factor not a stat
- Greek life ~25% participation, less central than at Big Ten peers
- Honors Program (separate application) — meaningful for top students seeking small-class experience inside a large public

## Aid + scholarship
- WA residents: Husky Promise (full tuition under ~$80K), Pell + WA state grants stack
- OOS: Limited institutional need-based aid — UW is need-aware for OOS
- Mary Gates Endowment, Bonderman Travel Fellowship, Ronald E. McNair are competitive merit/research awards available after enrollment
- Husky Experience Fund covers research/internship costs for low-income students

## Recent essay shifts
UW's "Maximum Diversity" supplement (the one about communities/identity/perspective) increased in weight post-SFFA 2023 — it's now where AOs read for context that race-conscious admissions can no longer provide directly. Authentic specificity beats generic diversity essays here.
