# New York University (NYU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current NYU admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~28,000 undergrad enrollment (NYC + Abu Dhabi + Shanghai), Manhattan-based
- Most recent admit rate: ~9-12% overall, varies dramatically by school (Tisch ~14% MORE selective than overall, Stern Business ~9-10%)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App; NYU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 5

## What admissions actually weights
NYU's identity: urban + global + arts/business pre-professional + diverse. Strong applicants articulate specific NYC/global engagement and clear professional direction.

Numerous undergraduate schools with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts and Science (broad)
- Stern School of Business (highly competitive)
- Tisch School of the Arts (audition/portfolio, separate process)
- Steinhardt School of Culture, Education, and Human Development
- Tandon School of Engineering (Brooklyn campus)
- Liberal Studies (different academic structure, Manhattan)
- Gallatin School of Individualized Study (custom major design)
- Rory Meyers College of Nursing
- NYU Abu Dhabi + NYU Shanghai (separate liberal arts campuses, EXTREMELY competitive)

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1470-1560, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
NYU's supplements ask explicitly about NYU choice + specific school choice. Generic NYC-loves don't land. AOs value specificity — naming specific NYC contexts, specific NYU programs, specific career pipeline goals.

## Post-SFFA stance
NYU's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. NYU has strong international student representation and continues that emphasis.

## Demonstrated interest
NYU DOES track demonstrated interest meaningfully. ED commitment, info sessions, visits all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked applicants)
- Yield protection IS real at NYU — overqualified applicants assumed to be using as safety can get rejected
- Stern Business is most competitive (yield-protected heavily)
- Tisch arts requires portfolio/audition; separate creative review
- NYU Abu Dhabi has ~3% admit rate — among the most selective programs in the world
- Greek life ~2% (very small)
- NYU is famously LACKING in financial aid — historically among the least generous of top privates. This has improved post-2018 but still a pain point

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens (since 2022 — relatively recent shift)
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need (since 2023, finally)
- Limited no-loan threshold (around $100K family income)
- Generous merit aid: Trustee Scholarship (~$30K/year), AnBryce Scholarship, Stern Scholarships
- QuestBridge partner
