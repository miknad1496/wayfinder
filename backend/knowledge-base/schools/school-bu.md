# Boston University (BU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current BU admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~17,000 undergrad enrollment, Boston (Charles River campus)
- Most recent admit rate: ~14-19% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — BU returned to required for 2025-26 cycle (announcement 2024)
- Application: Common App; BU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 6
- RD deadline: ~Jan 6

## What admissions actually weights
BU's identity: urban + research university + diverse + pre-professional. BU is large, with many undergraduate schools serving distinct interests. Strong applicants articulate specific BU program engagement.

Multiple undergraduate schools:
- College of Arts & Sciences (most students)
- Questrom School of Business — competitive business pipeline
- College of Communication — strong journalism/PR program
- College of Engineering
- College of Fine Arts (CFA)
- College of General Studies (broad core curriculum, transitions to other schools)
- School of Hospitality Administration
- Sargent College of Health & Rehabilitation Sciences

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1390-1500, ACT 31-34.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
BU supplements ask about BU-specific interests. The "Why BU?" essay rewards specific knowledge of programs.

## Post-SFFA stance
BU's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
BU DOES track demonstrated interest — among schools that watch this closely. ED commitment, visits, info sessions all factor in. BU is also known for yield protection — overqualified applicants without demonstrated interest get rejected.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant (~1.6-1.9x for unhooked applicants)
- Yield protection real — visit Boston, ED if genuinely top, write thoughtful Why-BU
- Questrom Business is highly regarded undergrad
- College of Communication is one of strongest undergraduate communications programs
- Athletic recruiting significant (Hockey East, basketball — modest D1)
- Greek life ~10% of campus (smaller than peer schools)
- Boston location: similar advantages to Tufts/BC but more urban (BU campus literally on Commonwealth Ave, within Boston city limits)
- BU is significantly larger than Tufts/BC — more course variety + more anonymity

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $100K (recently increased from lower threshold)
- Generous merit aid: Trustee Scholarship (full tuition), Presidential Scholarship, BU Honors Program
- QuestBridge partner
