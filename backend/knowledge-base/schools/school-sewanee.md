# Sewanee — University of the South — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Sewanee admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private Episcopal-affiliated LAC, ~1,750 undergrad enrollment, Sewanee TN (atop the Cumberland Plateau)
- Admit rate: ~52-58%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Sewanee supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Dec 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
Sewanee's identity: traditional Anglican/Episcopal LAC + Honor Code (real, enforced — academic + personal) + **gown tradition** (faculty + Order of Gownsmen seniors wear academic gowns to class) + 13,000-acre Domain (the entire University owns the surrounding mountain plateau — distinctive among LACs) + traditional Southern collegiate aesthetic + literary + theological heritage (T.S. Eliot, Walker Percy, Robert Penn Warren, Tennessee Williams all had Sewanee connections). Strong applicants articulate traditional + intellectual + Southern fit + Episcopal-friendly cultural ease.

**The Domain** — Sewanee's 13,000 acres of forest, lakes, hiking trails, climbing, caves, and conservation land. The University literally owns the mountain. Outdoor culture is real.

**Order of Gownsmen** — students who maintain certain GPA + leadership + academic standards earn the right to wear academic gowns. ~40-50% of seniors. Cultural anchor.

**Sewanee Review** — oldest continuously-published quarterly literary magazine in U.S. (since 1892). Distinctive among LACs.

**Sewanee Writer's Conference** — summer faculty-attended; major American writers attend (Joyce Carol Oates, Ann Patchett, etc.). Undergrads can interact with this ecosystem.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1430, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Sewanee if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Wilkins Scholar** — top merit + summer experiences + leadership cohort
- **Tonya Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- Strong programs: English (creative writing pipeline), Religion + Theology (Episcopal seminary connection), Politics, History, Environmental Studies (Domain access integral), Forestry + Geology, Music, Theater
- **English / Creative Writing** is distinctive — alumni include Pulitzer winners + major American novelists
- D-III athletics (SAA) — Tigers; football has SE LAC heritage
- Greek life ~70-80% participation (very Southern Greek culture)
- **Sewanee TN setting** — atop the Cumberland Plateau; truly isolated mountain town (residents are mostly faculty + students). 1.5 hours from Nashville + Chattanooga. ~2,500 ft elevation
- Episcopal heritage real — School of Theology + Chapel of the Apostles are central. Non-Episcopal students welcome but the campus has explicit religious identity
- Strong Southern + Conservative + International student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $75K+/year — meets need fully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95-100% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Wilkins Scholar, Tonya Scholar, Posse, Episcopal Heritage Scholar (for Episcopal students), Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
