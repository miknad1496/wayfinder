# University of Minnesota Twin Cities — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UMN admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~38,000 undergrad enrollment, Minneapolis-St. Paul MN
- Admit rate: ~70-75% overall, but **Carlson School of Management direct admit ~30%** and **CSE engineering direct admit ~50-60%**
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Golden Gopher application (institutional)
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for Carlson + best scholarship/Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Dec 1 for top scholarship consideration; final deadline Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
UMN's identity: large public flagship, urban campus blended with Twin Cities, strong in business (Carlson), engineering (CSE), and pre-health. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Carlson, CSE, Design) since those are competitive sub-admissions vs the broadly accessible College of Liberal Arts.

**Direct admit to Carlson** is the most important admissions battle for business applicants. Direct admits begin business core Day 1. Non-direct must transfer in via internal application after sophomore year — competitive.

**Direct admit to CSE** (College of Science & Engineering) for engineering majors is similar — direct admit is recommended over the indirect path through CLA.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1330-1480, ACT 26-32. Carlson trends ~50-100 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
UMN supplements are minimal vs Big Ten peers. Single short supplement most years. Quality matters less than for Carlson/CSE applicants.

## Post-SFFA stance
UMN maintained holistic review post-SFFA with continued commitment to Minnesota residents (especially first-gen, rural, and Native American students via the Tribal Nations Education Initiative).

## Demonstrated interest
UMN does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but campus visit before application is common for in-state students.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 1** for Carlson/CSE direct admit + automatic merit scholarship consideration (significant for OOS)
- **Honors Program** — automatic invitation to high stats. Honors community in Frontier Hall, smaller seminars, thesis option
- **President's Scholarship** (~$10K-13K/yr automatic for high stats), Dean's Scholarship, Maroon & Gold (smaller)
- Strong programs: Business (Carlson), Computer Science + Engineering (CSE), Public Health (top-5), Pharmacy (PharmD), Veterinary Medicine, Human Factors / Industrial Engineering (very strong), Journalism + Strategic Communication (Hubbard School)
- Twin Cities = real city environment. Light rail connects East Bank → West Bank → St. Paul campuses. Mall of America, lakes, music scene (Prince's hometown)
- D-I athletics (Big Ten) — Gophers football, hockey (huge), basketball
- Greek life ~6-8% participation — much smaller than peer Big Tens
- Korean + Hmong + East African + Latinx communities are meaningful
- Cold winters are real — non-issue if you're from MN, often-cited objection from out-of-state visitors
- "Minnesota nice" + "Iron Range" cultural mix is distinctive

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap is common — model carefully)
- Strong merit scholarship structure including Bentson, Heisig, Hagstrum (full ride + research stipend tier — apply by Dec 1)
- Minnesota residents get strong in-state pricing + Promise Scholarship for qualifying low-income MN residents
- North Star Promise (2024+) — free tuition for MN residents from families under $80K AGI
- Reciprocity agreements: Wisconsin + North Dakota + South Dakota + Manitoba residents get in-state-equivalent pricing (huge for those students)
