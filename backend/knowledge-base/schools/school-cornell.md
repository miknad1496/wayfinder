# Cornell University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Cornell admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Ivy League (with state-affiliated colleges), ~16,000 undergrad enrollment — LARGEST Ivy
- Most recent admit rate: ~7-8% overall; varies dramatically by college (Engineering ~6%, ILR ~10%, Hotel ~17%, A&S ~7%)
- Test policy: REQUIRED for Engineering, A&S, ILR, Hotel for 2025-26 cycle. Other Cornell colleges remain optional. Returned to required selectively
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge accepted
- Early round: Early Decision (ED) — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 2

## What admissions actually weights
Cornell admits BY COLLEGE — there are 8 undergraduate colleges with distinct admissions:
- College of Arts & Sciences (CAS) — broad academics
- College of Engineering — STEM rigor, most competitive of state-affiliated tracks
- ILR (Industrial and Labor Relations) — distinctive labor/social science focus
- Hotel Administration (Nolan School) — hospitality industry
- Architecture, Art, and Planning — portfolio-required for Architecture/Art
- Agriculture and Life Sciences (CALS) — state-affiliated, agriculture/biology
- Human Ecology — interdisciplinary
- College of Business (Dyson) — applied business

Each college has its own admit rate, profile, supplemental essay, and matriculation expectations. Picking the right college is strategic — Engineering at Cornell is harder than CALS for similar applicants.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1560, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Cornell requires school-specific supplements that vary substantially. The "Why this college at Cornell" essay is high-leverage and college-specific. Generic Cornell essays don't land — applicants need to articulate why their specific college within Cornell.

## Post-SFFA stance
Cornell's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay opportunities. Cornell has substantial first-generation enrollment and continues that focus.

## Demonstrated interest
Cornell DOES consider demonstrated interest, particularly visiting Ithaca (where feasible) and ED commitment. The Ithaca location's relative isolation means visits are read as serious fit-research.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- College choice STRATEGIC: applying to a less-competitive Cornell college (Hotel, CALS) gets you in if you're qualified for that program. Internal transfer between Cornell colleges is possible but not guaranteed.
- ED at Cornell is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is real (~1.5-1.8x vs RD)
- Cornell + Tech Connect (Cornell Tech NYC, Roosevelt Island campus) — separate graduate programs
- Athletic recruiting ~10-12% of admits, big fall and winter sports
- The Ithaca location: very rural, harsh winters, isolated. Real fit consideration

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international (with limited international aid)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for families under $100K (income-based scaling)
- For families above $100K, modest loan expectations
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
