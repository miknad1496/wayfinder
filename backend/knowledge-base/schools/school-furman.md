# Furman University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Furman admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,400 undergrad enrollment, Greenville SC
- Admit rate: ~50-58%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Furman supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 15
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Dec 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Furman's identity: Southern LAC + **Furman Advantage** (4-year program guaranteeing engaged learning experiences — research, internship, study abroad, capstone) + Riley Institute (Public Affairs + Civic Engagement) + traditional Southern collegiate aesthetic + Paladins athletics + Greek-centric culture. Strong applicants articulate engagement-oriented LAC fit + Southern cultural ease.

**The Furman Advantage** — institutional commitment to pair every student with a faculty advisor + ensure each graduates with engaged learning experiences. Differentiator vs peer LACs that don't formalize this.

**Riley Institute** — for Public Affairs + Civic Engagement. Strong undergrad pipeline to South Carolina policy + government + nonprofit careers.

**Beautiful campus** — Furman Lake + Italian gardens + bell tower + Asian gardens. Among the most aesthetically distinctive LAC campuses in the South.

**Furman is a former Baptist-affiliated school** — secular since 1992, but Southern Christian heritage is part of cultural fabric.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1230-1410, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Furman if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Furman Scholar** — top merit + Furman Advantage signature experience
- **Bell Tower Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Townes Scholarship** — for science + engineering + math majors with merit + research
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- Strong programs: Business (top-50 LAC business), Education, Music (audition required), Politics (Riley Institute), Pre-Med, Public Health, Sustainability Science, Asian Studies (distinctive for a Southern LAC)
- D-I (Southern Conference, FCS) — Paladins football + basketball
- Greek life ~30-35% participation — significant
- **Greenville SC setting** — vibrant mid-size SE city (~75K residents, growing). Downtown Greenville has been heavily revitalized — pedestrian Main Street, restaurants, breweries. 1.5 hours from Atlanta
- Strong Southern + Latino + Asian + International + first-gen student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $65K+/year — meets need + merit aid offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~92-95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Furman Scholar, Bell Tower, Townes, Posse, Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- South Carolina residents: in-state-equivalent through some scholarship structures + LIFE Scholarship for SC HS grads
