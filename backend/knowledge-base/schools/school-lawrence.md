# Lawrence University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Lawrence admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC + Conservatory, ~1,400 undergrad enrollment, Appleton WI
- Admit rate: ~63-68%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Lawrence supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Lawrence's identity: small Midwestern LAC + **on-campus Conservatory of Music** (one of two LACs in U.S. with degree-granting integrated conservatory — Oberlin is the other) + Bachelor of Music + double-degree (BA + B.Music in 5 years) + intentional academic intimacy (avg class size 13) + Björklunden (waterfront retreat in Door County for academic + creative work). Strong applicants signal serious music interest OR pure liberal arts intellectual orientation.

**Conservatory of Music** — degree-granting, on-campus. Music majors earn B.Music; many students pursue **Double Degree (BA + B.Music)** in 5 years, distinctive nationally.

**Björklunden** — Lawrence operates a 425-acre lakeshore retreat in Door County WI (5 hours from main campus). Students go for weekend or week-long academic + creative + outdoor programs. Distinctive among LACs.

**3-2 Engineering** — bachelor's from Lawrence + bachelor's from Columbia or Washington University. 5-year combined dual-degree.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1450, ACT 28-33.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for Lawrence if clear top choice
- **Conservatory audition required** for B.Music or Double Degree programs — separate process within app
- **Lawrence Scholar** — top merit + Honors-style cohort
- **Trustee Distinguished Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- Strong programs: Music (Conservatory — Performance + Composition + Music Theory + Music Education), Liberal Arts (Anthropology, Economics, English, Mathematics), Pre-Med, Computer Science (newer, growing), Government, History
- **Music ↔ Liberal Arts integration** — even non-music majors take music classes; the Conservatory infuses campus culture
- D-III athletics (MWC) — Vikings
- Greek life — small (~5-8%) but distinctive history (Lawrence was an early LAC to integrate fraternities)
- **Appleton WI setting** — small Midwest city (75K), 90 min from Milwaukee, 30 min from Green Bay (Packers), 4 hours from Chicago + Minneapolis. Real college town
- Strong Wisconsin + Midwest + International student communities
- **Björklunden trips** are real and frequent — students visit during long weekends
- Cost significant — sticker $75K+/year — meets need partially; merit is substantial

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~92-95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Lawrence Scholar, Trustee Distinguished Scholar, Posse, Conservatory Scholarships (audition-based), Davis UWC Scholars
- QuestBridge partner
