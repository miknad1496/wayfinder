# Johns Hopkins University (JHU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current JHU admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,000 undergrad enrollment, Baltimore MD
- Most recent admit rate: ~6-7% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; JHU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 3
- RD deadline: ~Jan 3

## What admissions actually weights
JHU's identity: research university with intense pre-professional culture (especially pre-med) + collaborative academic environment. Strong applicants show evidence of independent intellectual pursuit — research experience, internships, deep self-directed projects.

Pre-med is a major draw — JHU's medical school proximity and Hopkins Hospital integration mean ~30% of undergrads are pre-med. Strong pre-med applicants articulate specific clinical/research interest, not generic "I want to help people."

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1510-1560, ACT 34-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
JHU's supplements include the "share something you cherish, something you're curious about, something you advocate for" prompt — designed to surface intellectual personality. AOs read for genuine engagement, not curated achievement lists.

## Post-SFFA stance
JHU's post-SFFA approach has been visible — expanded adversity-essay options. JHU has substantial first-gen and Pell-eligible enrollment relative to peers.

## Demonstrated interest
JHU does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment matters significantly.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH available, both binding
- Unhooked ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.6-1.9x vs RD for comparable applicants)
- Pre-med pipeline is real but PUNISHING. Average pre-med GPA around 3.5-3.6; competition for limited research positions is intense. Many pre-meds switch off pre-med
- Engineering at JHU (Whiting School) is research-strong but smaller than peer engineering programs
- Public Health major is unique (Bloomberg School of Public Health proximity)
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 most sports, no athletic scholarships)
- Baltimore location: complex. Some areas feel unsafe, campus itself is well-protected. Real consideration for some students

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $80K
- Reduced loans for higher-income families
- Limited merit aid (Hodson Trust Scholarship — full ride for ~20 students/year)
- QuestBridge partner (since 2018)
