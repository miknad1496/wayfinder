# University of Oregon — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UO admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~19,000 undergrad enrollment, Eugene OR
- Admit rate: ~85-88% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Oregon institutional application; rolling admission
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Oregon's identity: Big Ten (transitioned 2024 from Pac-12) flagship + Lundquist College of Business + School of Journalism + Robert D. Clark Honors College + Phil Knight (Nike co-founder) heritage on campus + outdoor culture + Ducks football. Strong applicants signal Honors targeting + specific Journalism or Business interest.

**Robert D. Clark Honors College** — selective separate admission; ~700 students/cohort. Smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming. Applies separately within UO app.

**School of Journalism + Communication (SOJC)** — top-15 nationally; strong sports journalism (Phil Knight + Nike heritage drives sports media pipeline) + advertising + public relations.

**Lundquist College of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; strong Sports Business + Sustainable Business pillars.

**Phil Knight Campus for Accelerating Scientific Impact** — Knight's $500M+ philanthropy; cutting-edge research + grad-level facilities undergrads can access.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1140-1340, ACT 22-29.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for Honors + Stamps + Apex + scholarship consideration
- **Apex Scholarship** — top merit + summer abroad + research stipend
- **Stamps Scholarship** — separate national; full ride + summer enrichment
- **Diamond Scholar** — second-tier OOS merit
- Strong programs: Journalism (SOJC top-15), Business (Lundquist — Sports Business + Sustainable Business), Architecture (5-year B.Arch), Music (audition), Computer Science, Environmental Studies, Psychology (top-15)
- D-I (Big Ten) — Ducks football (Phil Knight money + Autzen Stadium); basketball
- Greek life ~15% participation
- **Eugene OR setting** — mid-size college town (175K including students), 2 hours from Portland, Pacific Coast 60 min drive
- Outdoor culture is real — climbing, biking, river rafting, Pacific Coast access
- **Phil Knight philanthropy on campus is visible** — Hayward Field (track), Knight Library, Knight Campus all funded by Knight family
- Strong Korean + Asian + International student communities
- Oregon residents get strong in-state pricing
- Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) — neighboring states get reduced OOS pricing

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Apex, Stamps, Diamond Scholar, Honors College Scholarships, Departmental Scholarships
- WUE participants get OOS pricing benefit
- Oregon residents: Oregon Promise (free CC bridge for low-income) + Oregon Opportunity Grant
