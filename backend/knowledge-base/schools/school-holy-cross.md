# College of the Holy Cross — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private Catholic LAC (Jesuit), ~3,200 undergrad, Worcester MA (40 miles west of Boston)
- Admit rate: ~28%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Holy Cross supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Dec 15) + ED2 (Feb 1) — both binding
- Deadline: Dec 15 ED1 / Feb 1 ED2 + Jan 15 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Holy Cross is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **Jesuit Catholic identity** is genuinely central — Holy Cross was founded 1843 as the first Catholic college in New England, and it remains one of the most academically rigorous Catholic colleges in the U.S. The Jesuit tradition (intellectual rigor + service + social-justice orientation, "men and women for others") shapes institutional culture. Applicants don't need to be Catholic to attend, but the religious heritage is a real fit factor. (2) **The Montserrat Program**: Required first-year integrated course for ALL students — not a single class but a year-long thematic cluster combining seminars + community + advising in clusters of ~15 students with one faculty mentor. Defines the Holy Cross intellectual experience. (3) **Strong pre-professional pipelines**: Despite being a small Catholic LAC, Holy Cross is a prominent feeder to investment banking, consulting, law, and medicine. The "Crusader Network" (alumni network) is unusually active, particularly in NYC + Boston finance. (4) **Patriot League athletics**: D1 athletics in Patriot League (same as Bucknell + Lafayette + Lehigh + Bucknell + Colgate + American + Boston University + Loyola Maryland). Football, basketball, hockey are real campus events.

Strong programs: Economics, Political Science (signature pre-law track), Psychology, English, History, Classics (Holy Cross has uniquely strong Greek + Latin programs for an American Catholic LAC), Religious Studies (Jesuit tradition), Pre-medicine track (very strong med school placement), Music.

The "Crusader" identity (yes, the mascot is "Crusaders" — controversially renamed from "the Crusaders" debates ongoing) — Catholic, pre-professional, athletic, traditional New England — is a real cultural fit signal.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1340-1480, ACT 30-32. Submission rate ~50-55% for admits.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~45-50%; ED2 admit rate ~35%; RD ~25% — ED is meaningful leverage
- Greek life: NONE (Holy Cross does not have Greek system, atypical for a school with otherwise traditional culture)
- Jesuit identity is real — applicants should engage with it authentically (don't need to be Catholic)
- D1 athletics in Patriot League — recruited athletes get edge
- Strong NYC + Boston finance + law alumni network
- Notable alumni: Clarence Thomas (US Supreme Court), Anthony Fauci, Joseph Califano Jr., Bob Cousy (basketball HOF)
- Distinctive features: Fenwick Hall, the Hoval, Saint Joseph Memorial Chapel, the College Bowl
- Worcester MA: mid-size city (180K population), 40 minutes west of Boston by Mass Pike

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan policy for families under ~$100K AGI
- Bishop Healy Scholarships + various competitive merit
- Strong alumni-funded financial aid

## Recent essay shifts
Holy Cross's "Why Holy Cross" supplement rewards applicants who engage with the Jesuit Catholic intellectual heritage authentically — generic "small LAC" essays underperform. Applicants who articulate Jesuit values + service orientation + Montserrat-style learning + pre-professional ambition score better. Non-Catholic applicants are welcome but should articulate why a Jesuit institution appeals.
