# Boston College (BC) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current BC admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Jesuit Catholic, ~9,500 undergrad enrollment, Chestnut Hill MA (Boston suburb)
- Most recent admit rate: ~14-16% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; BC-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
BC's identity: Jesuit + community-focused + pre-professional + athletic. Strong applicants articulate engagement with BC's "men and women for others" mission. Service work, leadership, faith engagement read favorably.

Four undergraduate schools:
- Morrissey College of Arts and Sciences (most students)
- Carroll School of Management — competitive business pipeline
- Lynch School of Education and Human Development
- Connell School of Nursing

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
BC supplements include "Why BC?" — specific Jesuit values references and program-specific knowledge land best.

## Post-SFFA stance
BC's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
BC DOES track demonstrated interest — among the schools that watch this closely. ED commitment, visits, info sessions all factor in. BC is ALSO known for yield protection on high-stat applicants without demonstrated interest.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant
- Yield protection real — visit, ED if genuinely top, Why-BC essay critical
- Carroll School Business is highly regarded undergraduate program
- Athletic recruiting significant (ACC conference, football + basketball + hockey major)
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at BC (replaced by community-living structures)
- The BC Eagles football culture is a major part of campus identity
- Catholic identity genuine but less prescriptive than Notre Dame — many non-Catholic students thrive
- Chestnut Hill location: T accessible to Boston (~25 min), but campus feels self-contained suburban

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $100K (Eagle Scholarship-eligible families)
- Limited merit aid: Presidential Scholars Program (full tuition + summer programs)
- QuestBridge partner
