# Duke University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Duke admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,700 undergrad enrollment, Durham NC
- Most recent admit rate: ~5.7-6.5% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Duke-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (ED) — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 2

## What admissions actually weights
Duke's identity: research university + pre-professional + Southern + athletic culture. Strong applicants combine intellectual depth with social engagement — Duke is famous for "passion-with-leadership" expectation. The supplements ask explicitly about extracurricular impact.

Trinity College of Arts & Sciences (most students) and Pratt School of Engineering are separate admissions tracks. Pratt is roughly equally competitive but with a different applicant profile.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1560, ACT 34-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Duke's "Why Duke?" essay is critical. AOs explicitly value specific knowledge of Duke programs (FOCUS, Bass Connections, specific research centers). The "intellectual passion" essay rewards depth in one area over breadth.

The "perspective" supplement (added 2023-24) asks applicants about identity/community context — important post-SFFA tool.

## Post-SFFA stance
Duke's post-SFFA approach has been particularly visible — expanded supplements with explicit space for identity/community context. Duke has been public about commitment to socioeconomic diversity.

## Demonstrated interest
Duke does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment matters significantly. Visiting Durham is read as genuine fit interest given the location.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Duke is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.6-1.9x vs RD for comparable applicants)
- Trinity vs Pratt choice matters strategically
- DUKE BASKETBALL: highly visible part of campus culture. The "Cameron Crazies" student section is integral to campus identity
- Athletic recruiting ~13% of admits, ACC conference (basketball, football, lacrosse significant)
- Greek life ~30% of campus — significant social structure for many students
- Pre-med pipeline is strong (Duke Medical School proximity, Bass Connections research)
- The Robertson Scholars Program (cross-enrollment with UNC) is a small selective program — apply separately

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants (with strong international aid pool)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for families under $80K
- Reduced loans for higher-income families
- Limited merit aid (Robertson Scholars, Reginaldo Howard Memorial, A.B. Duke Scholarship — all very competitive)
- QuestBridge partner
