# Amherst College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Amherst admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,900 undergrad enrollment, Amherst MA (Pioneer Valley)
- Admit rate: ~8-10% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Amherst supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (binding). Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 4

## What admissions actually weights
Amherst's identity: open curriculum + intellectual rigor + tight community. Amherst has NO general education requirements except 1 first-year seminar — students design their own academic path. This is similar to Brown's Open Curriculum but with even fewer constraints.

Five Colleges Consortium: Amherst students can take classes at UMass Amherst, Smith, Mount Holyoke, Hampshire — significantly expanding course availability.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1430-1540, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Amherst supplements include short essays focused on intellectual interests and academic flexibility. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the open curriculum philosophy.

## Post-SFFA stance
Amherst's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Amherst has historically had strong Pell-eligible enrollment (~22% of class) and continues focus on socioeconomic diversity.

## Demonstrated interest
Amherst does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Amherst is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.5-1.7x vs RD)
- Open curriculum: zero gen-ed requirements except first-year seminar. Real fit-test for students who want structured curriculum
- Athletic recruiting ~30% of admits (NESCAC conference, similar to Williams)
- No Greek life
- Amherst-Williams rivalry: campus culture references this often
- Five Colleges Consortium course access is genuine value-add — significantly expands course catalog
- Pioneer Valley location: bucolic, intellectual community, but limited urban access

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, undocumented students, AND international applicants (Amherst is one of few LACs need-blind for international)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
