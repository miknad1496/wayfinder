# University of Connecticut (UConn) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UConn admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~19,500 undergrad enrollment, Storrs CT (with regional campuses)
- Admit rate: ~55-60% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + UConn supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
UConn's identity: New England public flagship + School of Business (Stamford + Hartford + Storrs campuses) + School of Engineering + School of Pharmacy + Honors Program + Huskies basketball (women's basketball dynasty + men's basketball recent run). Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Business or Engineering) + Honors consideration.

**School of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; strong Insurance + Risk Management (Hartford insurance hub adjacency) + Finance + Accounting tracks.

**School of Engineering** — direct admit by major; Computer Science + Biomedical + Mechanical strong.

**School of Pharmacy** — 6-year direct entry PharmD; one of the top pharmacy schools in the NE.

**Honors Program** — automatic invitation for high stats; Honors Living-Learning Community + Honors Thesis + dedicated programming.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1430, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for direct admit + Honors + scholarship consideration
- **Day of Pride Scholarship** — full ride for top-tier OOS + leadership cohort
- **STEM Scholar** — for STEM-focused students with merit + research stipend
- **Holster Scholar** — first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship
- **Babbidge Scholar** — high-stats automatic merit
- Strong programs: Engineering (Computer Science, Biomedical, Mechanical), Business (Insurance/Risk Mgmt, Finance), Pharmacy (PharmD direct entry), Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Education, Animal Science (UConn Dairy Bar is famous, agricultural program is strong)
- D-I (Big East for basketball, MAC for football) — Huskies men's + women's basketball (multiple national championships, Geno Auriemma dynasty for women)
- Greek life ~10-12% participation
- **Storrs setting** — quiet rural college town; main campus is residential + integrated
- **Stamford + Hartford branch campuses** — for business + commuter students
- 90 minutes from Boston, 2.5 hours from NYC, 3.5 hours from DC
- Strong international + Asian + Latino student communities
- Connecticut residents get strong in-state pricing

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- Substantial merit: Day of Pride (full ride+), STEM Scholar, Holster, Babbidge, Engineering Scholarships
- Connecticut residents get strong in-state pricing + CT Roberta B. Willis Need-Based Grant
- New England Regional Student Program (RSP) — neighboring states get reduced OOS pricing for selected majors
