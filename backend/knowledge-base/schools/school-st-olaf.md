# St. Olaf College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current St. Olaf admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private Lutheran-affiliated LAC, ~3,000 undergrad enrollment, Northfield MN
- Admit rate: ~55-62%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + St. Olaf supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 15
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
St. Olaf's identity: traditional Lutheran-heritage LAC (admits all faiths) + St. Olaf Choir (one of the most respected college choirs in the U.S.) + strong sciences (chemistry, biology, premed pipeline) + interdisciplinary "Great Conversation" program + study abroad culture (top-3 LAC for study abroad rate) + traditional Norwegian-American Midwestern heritage. Strong applicants signal genuine fit with the values-based community + interest in music or sciences.

**St. Olaf Choir** — one of the most prestigious college choirs in the U.S. (founded 1912; tours Carnegie Hall + abroad annually). The Music Department itself is similarly prestigious.

**The Great Conversation** — interdisciplinary humanities sequence; selective application within first-year, ~70 students/cohort. Students cite it as transformative.

**Study Abroad** — ~70-75% of St. Olaf students study abroad (one of the highest rates among U.S. LACs).

**Strong Sciences** — Chemistry + Biology + Biochemistry + Physics; strong premed placement (St. Olaf premed acceptance rate is among the highest of LACs nationally).

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for St. Olaf if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Buntrock Scholarship** — full tuition + leadership programming. Top-tier merit
- **Davis United World College Scholars** — for IB + UWC graduates
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- **St. Olaf Heritage Scholar** — for Lutheran-affiliated students; merit + heritage tie-in
- Strong programs: Music (Choir + Band + Orchestra all top-tier), Chemistry (premed), Biology (premed), Mathematics, Computer Science, Religion + Philosophy, Asian Studies (distinctive for an LAC), Education, Nursing
- **Christian-affiliated but admits all faiths** — Lutheran heritage; most students Christian-Protestant background; significant non-Christian + non-religious students. Chapel attendance not required (though available)
- D-III athletics (MIAC) — Oles
- Greek life — none
- **Northfield MN setting** — small town (~20K), shared with Carleton College (rivals + collaborators across town). 45 min from Minneapolis, near apple orchards + small-town culture
- Strong Korean + Asian + International + first-gen Hmong (Minnesota's Hmong-American community is significant) student communities
- **Norwegian-American heritage** is real — Lutheran Lutefisk + Lefse traditions persist; many students with Scandinavian heritage
- Cost significant — sticker $75K+/year — meets need fully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: Buntrock Scholarship (full tuition+), Davis UWC Scholars, Posse, Heritage Scholar, Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
