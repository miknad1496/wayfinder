# Drexel University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Drexel admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 research university, ~14,000 undergrad enrollment, Philadelphia PA
- Admit rate: ~74-80%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Drexel supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Drexel's identity: **co-op program is THE differentiator** — undergrads complete 18 months of full-time paid co-op work integrated into the degree (not summer internships, but full 6-month co-op cycles). Strong applicants signal that they understand the co-op model and want it specifically vs traditional 4-year + summer internships.

**The Drexel Co-op:**
- 4 or 5 year programs available (5-year = 3 co-op cycles)
- Co-ops are FULL-TIME paid jobs, often 6 months each
- Students rotate between school + work — typical pattern: classes → co-op → classes → co-op → classes
- Average co-op pay $19-21/hr; many students earn $30K+ during their undergrad
- Drexel's career services + employer network is unmatched in the Philadelphia + Northeast corridor
- Co-op experience translates directly to job placement post-graduation (top employers hire from co-op pool)

**LeBow College of Business** + **College of Engineering** + **Westphal College of Media Arts & Design** — three flagship colleges. Strong applicants signal fit with one of these specifically.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1400, ACT 27-31.

## Recent essay shifts
Drexel supplements include "Why Drexel" — articulate why the co-op model specifically matters to you. Generic "I want hands-on experience" answers fail; specific reflection on what you'd want to learn from co-op cycles + how it'd shape your career path lands.

## Post-SFFA stance
Drexel maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + underrepresented students in STEM.

## Demonstrated interest
Drexel tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit + virtual sessions matter, especially for ED applicants.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED if Drexel is your clear top choice** — ED admit lift ~1.4-1.6x for unhooked applicants
- **Pennoni Honors College** — automatic invitation for high stats. Smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming + Honors Living-Learning Community
- **A.J. Drexel Autism Institute** — strong undergrad research access for psychology + neuroscience + education students interested in autism
- **STAR Scholars Program** — first-gen / underrepresented student scholarship + summer research + mentorship
- Strong programs: Engineering (Mechanical, Computer, BioMed, Civil), Computer Science, Business (LeBow), Game Design + Digital Media, Architecture (the only NAAB-accredited 5-year B.Arch in PA), Music Industry, Fashion Design, Nursing
- **Westphal Music Industry program** — distinctive, places students at major labels via co-op
- D-I athletics (CAA) — Dragons; not the campus identity
- Greek life ~10% participation
- Philadelphia — Drexel + Penn share University City. Strong urban setting + multiple co-op employers within walking distance
- Strong international student community (~15-18%)
- Korean + Asian + South Asian + East European communities meaningful
- Cost is significant — sticker $80K+/year — but merit + co-op earnings offset substantially
- "Drexel grit" — students often describe the program as intense due to the alternating school/work cycles, but rewarding

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~78-82% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: A.J. Drexel Scholarship (~$30K/yr — top admits), Liberty Scholarship, Engineering + Business + Westphal merit awards
- **Co-op earnings** offset cost meaningfully (most students earn $30-60K+ during 5-year co-op cycle)
- International applicants: highly need-aware
