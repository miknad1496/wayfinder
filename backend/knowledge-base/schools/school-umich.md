# University of Michigan — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UMich admissions site + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~32,000 undergrad enrollment, Ann Arbor MI
- Admit rate: ~18% in-state, ~17% out-of-state (relatively similar — UMich is rare in this)
- Test policy: STRONGLY RECOMMENDED but technically optional (functionally required for competitive applicants)
- Application: Common App + UMich-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding, non-restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
UMich evaluates by school. Strongest applicants articulate specific UMich school/program engagement.

Schools with distinct admissions:
- College of Literature, Science, and the Arts (LSA) — broad
- College of Engineering — competitive
- Ross School of Business — very competitive (~7-8% admit)
- School of Music, Theatre & Dance — audition required
- Ford School of Public Policy
- School of Information
- Architecture — portfolio required
- Stamps School of Art & Design — portfolio required

UMich uniquely values BOTH academic strength AND extracurricular leadership. Students who demonstrate impact in their high school community land well.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1370-1530, ACT 31-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Supplements include "Why this college at UMich?" (school-specific) and "Describe your community" — answers should be specific.

## Post-SFFA stance
Michigan was race-blind since 2006 (Proposal 2). Like UC, minimal change post-SFFA.

## Demonstrated interest
UMich does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but EA commitment + thoughtful supplements are signals.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- EA is non-binding non-restrictive. Get early read without commitment
- Ross Business is one of most competitive undergraduate business programs nationally
- Engineering EA admit rate ~14% — significantly lower than overall
- College choice strategic — internal transfer to Ross is very difficult
- Athletic recruiting major (Big Ten football, basketball, hockey)
- Greek life ~17%
- Michigan winters are real — campus life adapts but students should know

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For Michigan residents: very generous (Michigan Promise, Go Blue Guarantee = full tuition for families under $75K)
- For OOS: less generous — typical aid covers ~30-50% for need-eligible families
- Generous merit aid: Stamps Scholar Program (full ride), various competitive named scholarships
- QuestBridge partner
