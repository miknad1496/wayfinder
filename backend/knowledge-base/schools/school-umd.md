# University of Maryland College Park (UMD) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~30,000 undergrad, College Park MD (DC metro — Metro Green Line direct to downtown DC)
- Admit rate: ~45% in-state, ~37% out-of-state
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + UMD supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding (Nov 1) — EA admits notified late January, RD by April
- Deadline: Nov 1 EA / Jan 20 RD

## What admissions actually weights
UMD's three big-deal programs — Computer Science, Business (Smith), and Engineering — admit students by major directly out of high school. CS at UMD is particularly strong (top-15 nationally, with strength in cybersecurity and machine learning given the proximity to NSA, DARPA, and the Applied Physics Laboratory at JHU). The CS direct admit is highly competitive (~12-15% admit rate within the broader admit pool).

Banneker/Key Scholarship is UMD's signature merit award — full tuition + fees + living stipend, ~50 awards per year, separate competitive application after admit. This program meaningfully changes the value calculus for top-stats applicants comparing UMD to Ivy+ schools that offer no merit aid.

UMD's Honors College (separate selective admit at application time) provides smaller-class experience inside the larger university — applicants who indicate Honors interest and are competitive get auto-considered. The Honors College has multiple tracks (ACES for cyber, Gemstone for research-team-based, Honors Humanities, Design Cultures and Creativity).

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1370-1500, ACT 31-34. Engineering and CS admits cluster at the top of those ranges.

## Strategic notes
- DC-corridor location is a real strategic advantage: federal agencies (NSA, NIH, NIST, NASA Goddard, DOE), defense contractors, think tanks, Capitol Hill internships are all directly accessible by Metro
- UMD has a formal CIVICUS living-learning program for politics/civic-engagement interest
- ACES Honors College (Advanced Cybersecurity Experience for Students) is unique — direct pipeline to NSA and federal cyber roles
- Greek life ~15%
- Big Ten athletics (joined 2014); Maryland basketball historically strong, football competitive
- Smith Business School ranks top-25; direct admit very competitive
- UMD is the de facto state flagship; in-state demand is strong, OOS applicants compete for limited capacity

## Aid + scholarship
- MD residents: Maryland Promise (need-based, generous for under ~$100K family income)
- Banneker/Key Scholarship: full tuition + fees + stipend, separate selective application
- President's Scholar Award: lower-tier merit, automatic for top admits
- Maryland Pathways middle-class commitment (capped loans for under ~$200K AGI)
- OOS: less generous institutional aid; merit awards still competitive for top applicants

## Recent essay shifts
UMD's "Tell us your story" prompt and the major-fit prompt both increased weight post-SFFA. AOs explicitly read for context that race-conscious admissions can no longer provide. Specific UMD-fit answers (mentioning ACES, Gemstone, Banneker/Key research themes, specific labs in the Iribe Center) materially help — generic "great public university" essays score noticeably worse.
