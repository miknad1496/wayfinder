# University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~35,000 undergrad, Champaign-Urbana IL
- Admit rate: ~45% overall (much lower per-major: Grainger Engineering ~30%, CS ~7-14%, Gies Business ~25%)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + UIUC supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding, non-restrictive
- Deadline: Nov 1 EA / Jan 5 RD

## What admissions actually weights
UIUC's biggest non-obvious feature is direct major admit. You apply TO a specific major (or one of the seven Grainger Engineering majors), and the admit decision is per-major, not "to UIUC." This means a strong applicant denied for CS may be admitted to a different Engineering major or to LAS (Liberal Arts and Sciences). Some applicants strategically choose a less-competitive Engineering discipline (e.g. General Engineering, Industrial) to maximize admit odds, then attempt internal transfer — but internal transfer into CS is extraordinarily competitive and the path is not reliable.

Computer Science at UIUC is one of the top-5 CS programs in the country (peer with Berkeley/MIT/Stanford/CMU for systems and theory). CS+X programs (CS+Linguistics, CS+Astronomy, CS+Stats etc.) are intentionally less competitive than pure CS but offer real CS coursework — strategically valuable for applicants who want UIUC CS but find pure CS unattainable.

Gies College of Business: strong accounting (perennially top-3 nationally), direct admit competitive. Engineering Physics + Math are also signature programs.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1340-1500, ACT 28-34. Engineering admits cluster at the top of those ranges.

## Strategic notes
- CS+X programs are the underrated route into UIUC CS — significantly higher admit rate than pure CS, full CS access
- OOS admit rates are roughly 1.5-2x harder than IL resident admit rates per major
- Champaign-Urbana is rural — 2 hours from Chicago by car or train. This is a real fit factor that screens applicants
- Greek life ~20%, large but not dominant
- Big Ten athletics including football, basketball
- Strong international recruiting (China, India, Korea heavy presence) — international tuition fully OOS rate
- James Scholar Honors Program (honors college within colleges) — apply after admit, provides smaller-class experience

## Aid + scholarship
- IL residents: Illinois Promise (full tuition + fees for under $67K family income)
- IL Commitment for middle-income families ($67K-$110K)
- OOS: limited need-based, modest merit awards
- Chancellor's Scholar program (full-tuition merit for top admits) — competitive
- Provost Scholar awards smaller merit at admit

## Recent essay shifts
The "Why this major" supplement (300 words) is unusually high-stakes at UIUC because admit is per-major. AOs read for major fit specifically — generic CS-applicant essays fail badly here. Strong supplements name specific UIUC labs (PRAIRIE, CSL), specific courses (CS 425 Distributed Systems, CS 526 Field Theory), specific faculty research that connects to the applicant's articulated interest.
