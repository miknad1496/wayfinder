# University of Notre Dame — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Notre Dame admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Catholic, ~8,800 undergrad enrollment, South Bend IN
- Most recent admit rate: ~12-13% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Notre Dame-specific supplements
- Early round: Restrictive Early Action (REA) — non-binding, restrictive. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Notre Dame's identity: Catholic + tight-knit + athletic-cultural + alumni-loyal. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the Notre Dame community values — service, faith, intellectual rigor, athletic culture. Generic "great academics" essays don't land.

Notre Dame is one of few schools where Catholic faith + tradition is genuinely central to admissions read. Non-Catholic applicants need to show genuine engagement with the values, not religious conversion.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1480-1550, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Notre Dame's supplements include short answers about specific Notre Dame interests. AOs explicitly value evidence of community engagement (service work, leadership in faith communities, etc.).

## Post-SFFA stance
Notre Dame's post-SFFA approach has been notable — emphasis on socioeconomic diversity and continued commitment to first-generation enrollment. Adversity essays expanded.

## Demonstrated interest
Notre Dame DOES track demonstrated interest. The Catholic identity means visits, attending Notre Dame masses (where applicable), connection to the community read meaningfully. ED commitment is a strong signal but Notre Dame uses REA not ED.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- REA is non-binding but restrictive — can't apply ED elsewhere
- Notre Dame is unusually alumni-loyal — legacy preference still significant
- Athletic recruiting MAJOR — Notre Dame football is a defining feature of campus culture
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at Notre Dame — replaced by 32 residential dorms (similar to Rice's residential colleges)
- Business school (Mendoza) is highly regarded — separate admissions
- The Glynn Family Honors Program is a distinguished cohort program
- South Bend location: small-town Indiana, intense football weekends, snowy winters
- Strong international student support (Notre Dame International)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for families below $50K
- Reduced loans for families up to $150K
- Limited merit aid: Hesburgh-Yusko Scholars Program, Maeder Family Scholars, McGuire Scholars
- QuestBridge partner
