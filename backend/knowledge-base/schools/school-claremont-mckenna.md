# Claremont McKenna College (CMC) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,400 undergrad, Claremont CA (Claremont 5C Consortium with Pomona, Mudd, Scripps, Pitzer)
- Admit rate: ~10%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + CMC supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 1) + ED2 (Jan 10) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 1 ED1 / Jan 10 ED2 / Jan 10 RD

## What admissions actually weights
CMC is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **Pre-professional + applied focus** — CMC was founded 1946 explicitly as the "Claremont college for economics and government" — the institutional DNA is policy + business + leadership rather than pure liberal arts. CMC graduates pursue investment banking, consulting, government, and law at higher per-capita rates than peer LACs. The "Claremont type" who picks CMC vs. Pomona is choosing pre-professional applied vs. liberal-arts foundational. (2) **Robert Day Scholars Program**: CMC's signature merit + leadership development program — 5-year combined BA + MA in Finance with a stipend + research + summer programming. ~30 admits/year, separate competitive application. (3) **The Athenaeum**: A daily speaker forum (lunches + dinners with politicians, business leaders, scholars, journalists) — CMC students dine with prominent figures regularly. The Athenaeum + 5C network is a real differentiator. (4) **5C Consortium**: full cross-registration with Pomona, Mudd, Scripps, Pitzer. CMC students take humanities/arts at Pomona, STEM at Mudd, social-justice at Pitzer.

Strong programs: Economics (signature — top-3 LAC econ program nationally), Government, International Relations, Philosophy + Politics + Economics (PPE — combined major, signature), Finance (Robert Day MA pipeline), Psychology, Mathematics + Economics joint.

CMC's culture is more pre-professional + politically diverse than peer Claremont schools (Pomona is more progressive, Pitzer is more activist). CMC has more conservative + libertarian student presence than typical selective LACs.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1430-1530, ACT 32-34. Submission rate ~50% for admits.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~25-30%; ED2 admit rate ~22%; RD ~7-9% — ED is critical leverage
- Robert Day Scholars Program is the high-leverage pathway for top admits (full ride + MA in Finance)
- Greek life: NONE
- 5C cross-registration is essential — name specific peer-school courses in supplements
- Strong feeder to investment banking, consulting (McKinsey/Bain/BCG), and government policy positions
- Notable alumni: Henry Kravis (KKR), Robin Williams (briefly), David Dreier (former US Rep)
- D3 athletics in 5C combined teams
- CMC is academically rigorous + institutionally pre-professional in a way that distinguishes it from peer LACs

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan policy for low-income aid recipients
- Robert Day Scholars: full tuition + stipend + research, ~30/year
- Strong endowment per-student
- McKenna Scholars + various competitive merit awards

## Recent essay shifts
CMC's "Why CMC" supplement specifically asks how CMC's pre-professional applied identity (economics + government + leadership) fits the applicant's interests. Generic "small LAC" essays underperform. Applicants who name specific CMC features (Athenaeum, RDS Program, Lowe Institute of Political Economy, Keck Center for International Strategic Studies) signal genuine fit. Pre-professional ambition is a feature, not a bug, at CMC.
