# UC Davis — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~32,000 undergrad, Davis CA (15 miles from Sacramento, 70 miles from SF)
- Admit rate: ~37% in-state, ~50% out-of-state (notably higher than Berkeley/UCLA — UC Davis is the most accessible UC for strong-but-not-transcendent applicants)
- Test policy: TEST-BLIND through 2026-27 (UC system policy)
- Application: UC application + 4 PIQs (350 words each)
- Early round: NONE
- Deadline: Nov 30 hard

## What admissions actually weights
UC Davis is institutionally distinct in three ways most applicants miss: (1) The College of Agricultural and Environmental Sciences (CA&ES) is the LARGEST agricultural college in the United States and admits separately from the College of Letters and Science. Applicants targeting agriculture, sustainability, food science, viticulture/enology, or environmental policy should apply to CA&ES — admit profile is more favorable than the headline rate suggests for fit applicants. (2) The UC Davis School of Veterinary Medicine is ranked #1 in the world (US News + QS), and the pre-vet pipeline (Animal Science majors + research opportunities at the vet school) is the strongest in the nation. Applicants who articulate veterinary intent and have measurable animal-handling experience get a noticeable edge. (3) UC Davis Health (Sacramento) provides one of the strongest UC pre-med pipelines outside UCLA — research opportunities + clinical exposure at the med school + nursing school create a competitive pre-med ecosystem.

Strong programs: Agricultural Sciences (top-3 globally), Veterinary Medicine (top-1), Wildlife/Environmental Sciences, Plant Sciences, Viticulture & Enology, Food Science, Biological Sciences, Engineering (notably Biomedical Engineering).

PIQs are the entire writing portfolio — same as all UCs. AOs read for fit + intellectual maturity in 4-5 minutes total. Specificity on UC Davis programs (e.g., naming the Robert Mondavi Institute for Wine and Food Science, the California National Primate Research Center) materially helps.

## Strategic notes
- Lowest-competitive of the top UCs — best target for applicants competitive at non-UC top-50 schools
- CA&ES college admit is more accessible than the L&S college admit
- Pre-vet pipeline is uniquely strong — articulate genuine veterinary interest
- Bicycle culture is a real fit factor: Davis is consistently rated America's #1 bike-friendly city, no parking culture
- Sacramento Valley climate: hot/dry summers (100°F+ regularly), mild wet winters
- Only D1 athletic UC outside Berkeley/UCLA in football (Big Sky → recently joined Big West)
- Greek life ~10%
- Honors Program (separate competitive admit) — meaningful for top students seeking small-class experience

## Aid + scholarship
- CA residents: Cal Grant + Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan (full tuition for families under ~$80K)
- Limited OOS institutional need aid — UC Davis is need-aware for OOS
- Regents Scholarship — competitive merit award (~1.5% of admits), provides full-tuition equivalent + research stipend
- Aggie Grant + various smaller need-based awards
- Veterinary Medicine Scholar program for top pre-vet admits

## Recent essay shifts
PIQ #6 (academic interest) is the highest-leverage at UC Davis specifically — the AOs explicitly read for fit with one of the signature programs. Applicants who write generic "I love biology" essays score worse than applicants who reference specific UC Davis labs (Genome Center, Mondavi Institute, Center for Watershed Sciences) or specific Davis programs (Animal Behavior, Sustainable Agriculture).
