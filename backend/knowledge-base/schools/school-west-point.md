# U.S. Military Academy (West Point) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Federal service academy (Department of the Army), ~4,400 undergrad ("cadets"), West Point NY (50 miles north of NYC, Hudson Valley)
- Admit rate: ~10%
- Test policy: REQUIRED
- Application: USMA-specific online application (NOT Common App)
- Early round: NONE — single application cycle (rolling consideration)
- Deadline: Jan 31

## What admissions actually weights
West Point is institutionally peculiar in five ways: (1) **Five-year service obligation as a commissioned officer in the U.S. Army** post-graduation (with 3 additional years in Reserve). This is the institutional purpose — not negotiable. (2) **Congressional nomination required** — same process as USNA. Multiple sources: 2 Senators, 1 House Rep, VP, plus Service-Connected nominations (children of Medal of Honor recipients, dependents of disabled veterans, etc.). (3) **Free education + monthly stipend** (~$1,200/month) — total compensation ~$500K over 4 years. (4) **CFA + DODMERB medical** — physical fitness assessment + military medical exam. Both real disqualifiers. (5) **Honor Code**: "A cadet will not lie, cheat, steal, or tolerate those who do." The "tolerate" clause makes West Point's honor code more demanding than peers — cadets are required to report violations.

Strong programs: Engineering (signature — particularly Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Systems, Computer), Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Operations Research, Cyber Science, History (West Point's military history program is signature), Geography, Foreign Languages (Arabic, Chinese, Russian, Korean — all national-security focused), Economics.

West Point's "Long Gray Line" tradition is genuinely central — the institutional connection to alumni dating to the founding (1802) is real. Famous alumni include Eisenhower, Patton, MacArthur, Lee, Grant, Schwarzkopf, Petraeus, Tim Boyle (CEO of Columbia Sportswear).

Test scores: REQUIRED. Middle-50 SAT 1240-1430, ACT 27-32. Math heavily weighted.

## Strategic notes
- Same prep timeline as USNA: junior spring start, summer Congressional nominations, fall senior year application
- Beast Barracks (6-week boot camp before academic year) is the actual cadet entry experience
- Greek life: NONE
- Athletics: D1 (Patriot League non-football, Independent in FBS football — Army-Navy game is the climactic rivalry)
- Distinctive features: Cadet Chapel, Trophy Point overlooking the Hudson, the Plain (parade grounds), Eisenhower Hall, Battle Monument
- "Bow tie" Branch Selection at end of senior year — cadets choose Army branch (Infantry, Armor, Artillery, Aviation, Engineers, Signal, Intelligence, Military Intelligence, etc.)
- 5-year active duty + 3-year reserve obligation
- One of three federal service academies (along with USNA, USAFA) — same broad mechanics

## Aid + scholarship
- TUITION-FREE
- Monthly stipend
- All books, room, board, uniforms, health care covered
- Service obligation = effective payment

## Recent essay shifts
West Point applications focus essays on "Why Army?" + "Why West Point?" Applicants who frame USMA as "free Ivy" without genuine commitment to Army service score poorly. AOs read for leadership track record (athletic teams, Eagle Scout, JROTC, leadership in any domain) + military commitment + intellectual capability. ROTC scholarship recipients sometimes apply to West Point as a backup; AOs notice and weight commitment seriously.
