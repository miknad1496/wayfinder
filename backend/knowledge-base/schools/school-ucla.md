# UCLA — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UCLA admissions + UC system policies.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~32,000 undergrad enrollment, Los Angeles
- Admit rate: ~9% overall in-state, ~6% out-of-state
- Test policy: TEST-BLIND through 2026-27 (UC system policy)
- Application: UC application + 4 PIQs
- Early round: NONE (UC system policy)
- Deadline: Nov 30

## What admissions actually weights
UCLA uses the same UC Comprehensive Review framework as Berkeley. GPA + course rigor + 4 PIQs + extracurricular depth + special talents.

UCLA's identity vs Berkeley: more "campus feel" (Westwood is residential, Berkeley is more urban-integrated), strong arts/film integration, Big Ten athletics (recently joined). UCLA is the most-applied-to university in the U.S. (~150K+ applicants annually).

Test scores: TEST-BLIND.

## Recent essay shifts
PIQ structure stable. UCLA reads holistically with strong weight on California public-school context.

## Post-SFFA stance
UC system was already race-blind for 25+ years. Minimal change post-SFFA.

## Demonstrated interest
UCLA does NOT consider demonstrated interest.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- Major choice critical: CS, Engineering, Business Economics, Pre-major Engineering all hyper-competitive
- Anderson Business School: graduate only. Pre-business path is Business Economics major in L&S
- Direct admit to certain colleges (Engineering, Arts) limits ability to switch later
- TFT (Theater, Film, TV) admissions separate, portfolio/audition required
- OOS aid limited; California residents have huge cost advantage
- The size: 32K undergrads is large. Real consideration for students wanting smaller community

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Generous for California residents (same Cal Grant + Blue and Gold framework as Berkeley)
- OOS: limited institutional aid
- Regents' Scholarship — top merit award
- Alumni Scholarship Program competitive
