# Pitzer College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Pitzer admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,150 undergrad enrollment, Claremont CA (Claremont Colleges Consortium / 5C)
- Admit rate: ~13-19% (cycle-dependent — Pitzer admit rate fluctuates more than other 5Cs)
- Test policy: Test-blind (does not consider scores even if submitted) — distinctive among 5Cs
- Application: Common App + Pitzer supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Pitzer's identity: progressive, interdisciplinary, social-justice oriented, environmental sustainability, alternative to traditional grading culture. Strong applicants signal genuine engagement with **Pitzer's five "core values"**: social responsibility, intercultural understanding, interdisciplinary learning, student engagement, environmental sustainability.

The supplemental essay asking students to engage with one of these core values is THE differentiator. Generic "I want a small LAC" answers fail. Specific examples of past activism, sustainability work, justice-oriented projects, or interdisciplinary inquiry land.

Test-blind (since 2020) — admissions evaluates without scores. This is NOT the same as test-optional. Even strong scores will not be factored. Don't waste prep cycles for Pitzer specifically.

## Recent essay shifts
Pitzer supplements explicitly ask students to discuss engagement with one of the core values. Post-SFFA the prompts continue to invite identity-and-context discussion.

## Post-SFFA stance
Pitzer's post-SFFA messaging emphasized continued commitment to underrepresented students, first-gen, and adversity-essay invitation.

## Demonstrated interest
Pitzer does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but the supplements heavily reward applicants who clearly understand Pitzer's distinct culture vs the rest of the 5C consortium.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Strong ED admit lift
- The 5C consortium access — Pitzer students take classes across Pomona/CMC/Mudd/Scripps without extra cost
- Pitzer has the most student-led activism culture among the 5Cs (vs Pomona's collaborative, CMC's pre-professional, Mudd's STEM, Scripps's literary)
- Strong programs: Environmental Analysis, Media Studies, Sociology, Anthropology, Africana Studies, Latinx Studies
- New Resource Center programming and Outback (campus garden) are real student touchpoints
- Sagehens athletics shared with Pomona-Pitzer combined teams in NCAA D-III SCIAC
- Active study-abroad program — among highest LAC participation rates

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need (loans included in some packages — not loan-free)
- Limited merit aid (vs CMC and Mudd which have more)
- QuestBridge partner
