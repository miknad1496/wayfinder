# Harvey Mudd College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~900 undergrad (smallest of the Claremont 5C consortium), Claremont CA (35 miles east of LA)
- Admit rate: ~10-13%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Harvey Mudd supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 5) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 5 ED2 / Jan 5 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Harvey Mudd is institutionally distinct in five ways: (1) **The all-STEM LAC** — Harvey Mudd offers majors ONLY in Engineering, Computer Science, Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and combined STEM majors (Math + Physics, Math + CS, etc.). There's no English major, no History major, no Government. Applicants self-select for STEM intensity. But — and this is critical — the **Common Core curriculum** requires every student (regardless of major) to take humanities + social science courses (~33% of total credits), so Mudd graduates are not narrow technicians. The institutional motto: "the leaders the nation deserves in science and engineering." (2) **Per-capita PhD production rate is THE HIGHEST in the U.S.** — Mudd consistently sends more graduates to STEM PhD programs per capita than Caltech, MIT, or any peer school. (3) **Claremont 5C Consortium**: Mudd students cross-register at Pomona, Claremont McKenna, Scripps, Pitzer (and access their courses, libraries, dining, social life). Mudd students get the LAC humanities + social-science depth via cross-registration — which is the institutional design. (4) **Clinic Program**: Junior + senior students work on real industry-sponsored engineering projects (Boeing, NASA, Tesla, etc.) in 4-person teams supervised by faculty. The Clinic is uniquely intensive among LACs. (5) **Honor Code** governs all academic + social conduct — final exams unproctored, take-home exams common, students self-schedule.

Strong programs: Computer Science (top-5 LAC nationally + comparable to small elite programs), Engineering (general engineering — ABET-accredited, broader than discipline-specific), Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology + Joint Mathematics-Computer Science.

The Mudd "type" — intellectually intense, academically rigorous, slightly nerdy, collaboratively competitive — is a real culture that AOs read for. Applicants must signal genuine STEM interest + collaborative bent (Mudd is famously NOT a competitive grind culture).

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1490-1560, ACT 33-35. Submission rate ~70% for admits (highest rate among test-optional peer LACs — applicants self-select to submit).

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~25-30%; ED2 admit rate ~20%; RD ~9% — ED is high leverage at Mudd
- Claremont 5C cross-registration is essential — Mudd has only ~900 students, so the cultural breadth comes from cross-registration
- Greek life: NONE (5C Consortium has limited Greek life overall)
- Mudd campus is the smallest 5C — adjacent to Pomona (a 5-min walk between campus centers)
- D3 athletics in 5C combined teams (Claremont-Mudd-Scripps for women's, CMS for men's)
- Notable alumni: Stacy Pittman (CTO Tesla), Lily Hsia (NASA), academic luminaries in math/CS/physics
- Distinctive features: Clinic, Mudd Garden, the Hoch-Shanahan Dining Commons (5C joint)
- "5C" social life: Mudd students freely socialize at all 5C campuses

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan financial aid for low-income aid recipients
- Strong endowment per-student
- Harvey Mudd Scholar awards + various competitive merit
- Notable: Mudd is one of MORE-EXPENSIVE schools nominally, but the financial aid is generous

## Recent essay shifts
Harvey Mudd's supplements include a quirky problem-solving prompt + a "Why Mudd specifically" question. AOs read for genuine STEM intensity + collaborative orientation (NOT cutthroat). Applicants who signal a "lone-wolf math kid" identity score worse than applicants who signal "I want to do hard problems with brilliant peers." The 5C consortium is meaningful — applicants should articulate why they want LAC humanities access alongside their STEM major.
