# Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current WPI admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private STEM-focused research university, ~5,200 undergrad enrollment, Worcester MA
- Admit rate: ~55-60%
- Test policy: Test-blind (does not consider scores even if submitted) — distinctive
- Application: Common App + WPI supplements
- Early decision: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA — deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
WPI's identity: project-based STEM education + the **WPI Plan** (no traditional letter grades for many courses, instead A/B/C/NR — a 4th option that's "no record" rather than failure) + project-based capstones (IQP and MQP). Strong applicants articulate that they specifically want the project-based pedagogy vs traditional STEM (vs MIT's problem sets, vs CMU's specialized depth).

**The WPI Plan in concrete terms:**
- A 7-week term system (4 terms per year + summer)
- 3 courses per term (very intensive)
- "NR" = no record on transcript if you don't pass — protects experimentation
- IQP (Interactive Qualifying Project) — junior-year team project, often abroad at WPI's 50+ Project Centers
- MQP (Major Qualifying Project) — senior-year capstone, often industry-partnered
- The 50+ international project centers (London, Bangkok, Cape Town, Wellington, Worcester-local, etc.) — this is THE differentiator

Test-blind (since 2020-21) — admissions does not consider scores. Don't waste prep cycles for WPI specifically.

## Recent essay shifts
WPI supplements ask why WPI specifically AND ask about a project, problem, or experience that shaped your STEM interest. Generic "I love engineering" answers fail. Specific projects + reflection on what you learned land.

## Post-SFFA stance
WPI maintained holistic review post-SFFA with continued commitment to underrepresented students in STEM.

## Demonstrated interest
WPI tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit + virtual sessions help meaningfully — especially for showing you understand the WPI Plan.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **ED1 admit lift is significant** (~1.5x for unhooked applicants) — apply ED if WPI is your clear top choice
- **Project Centers** are the real magic — applying with a clear sense of which project center you'd target adds depth
- Strong programs: Robotics Engineering (top-tier nationally, undergraduate major), Computer Science, Mechanical + Aerospace Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Game Development (IMGD), Data Science
- The **Robotics Engineering** undergraduate major is rare among peers and a real differentiator (vs taking ME + CS double-major elsewhere)
- Worcester is a real city (~200K) — second-largest in MA, college-rich (Holy Cross, Clark, Becker, MCPHS all within minutes)
- 50 minutes from Boston by car/train
- D-III athletics (NEWMAC) — competitive but not the campus identity
- Greek life present but small (~25% participation)
- 7-week term cadence is intense — students often describe sophomore year as the hardest semester of their lives
- Strong career services + co-op pipeline; med school placement weaker than premed-focused schools (premed at WPI is a lonely path)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (significant gap common — model carefully)
- Substantial merit scholarships including Presidential Scholarship (~$30K/yr), Trustee Scholarship (~$25K/yr), Dean's Scholarship (~$20K/yr) — automatic based on application strength
- Foisie Innovation Scholarship for first-gen / underrepresented STEM students
- IGNITE program for underrepresented STEM students with full-ride packages
