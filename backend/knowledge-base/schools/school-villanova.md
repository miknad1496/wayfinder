# Villanova University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Catholic Augustinian, ~7,000 undergrad enrollment, Villanova PA (Philadelphia suburb)
- Admit rate: ~28% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Villanova supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 15
- Deadline: Jan 15 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Villanova's identity: Catholic + collaborative + business-strong + athletics. Strong applicants articulate engagement with Augustinian values (caritas, veritas, unitas).

Schools with distinct admissions:
- Villanova School of Business (VSB) — very competitive
- College of Liberal Arts and Sciences
- College of Engineering
- College of Nursing

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1380-1500, ACT 31-34.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant
- VSB Business is among most competitive undergraduate business programs (~10% admit)
- Athletic recruiting MAJOR (Big East — Villanova Wildcats basketball won 2 NCAA championships recently)
- Greek life ~25%
- Catholic identity central but less prescriptive than Notre Dame
- Philadelphia Main Line suburb, train to Center City

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need
- Presidential Scholarship + various competitive merit

## Recent essay shifts
- "Why Villanova" supplement remains required and is scored heavily — generic "great academics + nice campus" answers are weeded out fast. Engagement with Augustinian values (caritas, veritas, unitas) or specific VSB / Engineering / Nursing programs lands better.
