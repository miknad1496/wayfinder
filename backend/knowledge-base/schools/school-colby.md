# Colby College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,200 undergrad, Waterville ME
- Admit rate: ~7-8% (one of the lowest LAC admit rates nationally, comparable to Williams/Amherst/Pomona)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Colby supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 1) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 1 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Colby is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **Jan Plan (January Term / Mini-Term)** — a 4-week intensive between fall and spring semesters where students take ONE course or do an internship/study-abroad/research project. Jan Plan is genuinely transformative for many students — applicants should articulate how they'd use it. (2) **The Davis Connects program**: Every Colby student is guaranteed funding for AT LEAST ONE substantial experiential opportunity (internship, research, or study abroad). This is unusual — most LACs offer pools of funding, but Colby commits to every student. (3) **Recent strategic transformation under President David Greene** (2014-present): Colby has aggressively raised endowment + admissions selectivity + financial aid commitments, transforming from a "second-tier NESCAC" school to genuinely peer-ranked with Williams/Amherst. The transformation is visible in the new admit rate (down from ~25% in 2015 to ~7% now). (4) **Outdoor + winter culture**: Maine location creates a distinctive seasonal rhythm — outdoor sports + winter sports are central. Colby Outing Club is one of the most active campus organizations.

Strong programs: Economics, Government, Biology, Environmental Studies (signature program — Maine environment + Allen Island conservation property), Global Studies, Computer Science (small but rigorous), Art History (Colby Museum of Art is an unusually strong campus museum), Geology (Maine has rich geological context).

Colby's "NESCAC" identity — small but deeply rigorous, athletic, traditional — shapes campus culture.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1430-1540, ACT 32-35. Submission rate ~45-50% for admits.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~35-40%; ED2 admit rate ~25-30%; RD ~5-7% — ED is HIGH leverage
- Jan Plan articulation is high-leverage in supplements (specific projects, courses, internships)
- Davis Connects is a distinctive funding feature — name it explicitly
- NESCAC athletic conference (D3) — recruited athletes get real edge
- Greek life: NONE (Colby abolished Greek system in 1980s)
- Waterville ME: rural Maine, harsh winters (-20°F windchills realistic), small-town isolation is a real fit factor
- Outing Club + Colby Outdoor Education program highly active
- Distinctive features: Colby Outing Club cabin trips, the Spa, Pugh Center for diversity
- Low admit rate is recent — signal that the "Colby is a backup to Williams" framing is outdated

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants (need-aware for international)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan financial aid for families under ~$200K AGI
- Davis Connects funding for every student (study abroad / internship / research)
- Strong endowment per-student (~$700K+)
- Notable recent transformation: tripled financial aid budget under Greene presidency

## Recent essay shifts
Colby's "Why Colby" + Jan Plan-engagement supplements reward authentic specificity. AOs read for whether the applicant has researched Jan Plan (4-week intensive — what would the applicant do with it?), Davis Connects (which experiential opportunity?), the Maine environment + Outing Club culture. Generic "small LAC near nature" essays underperform — Colby's transformation under Greene has not yet penetrated all applicant pools, so applicants who DO know the recent changes signal genuine fit research.
