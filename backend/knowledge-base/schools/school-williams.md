# Williams College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Williams admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,100 undergrad enrollment, Williamstown MA (rural Berkshires)
- Admit rate: ~7-9% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Williams supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (binding). Deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Williams' identity: small + intense + intellectual + outdoorsy. Strong applicants combine genuine intellectual depth with engagement in something beyond academics. The famous Williams tutorial system (Oxford-style 2-student-1-professor classes in junior/senior year) is central to academic identity.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1430-1530, ACT 32-35.

## Recent essay shifts
Williams supplements include "Why Williams?" — strong essays reference specific tutorials, the Mountain Day tradition, the Berkshires setting, specific faculty/programs.

## Post-SFFA stance
Williams' post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Williams does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Williams is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.5-1.7x vs RD)
- The Williams tutorial system: junior + senior students take ~1 tutorial per semester. Real intellectual rigor
- Athletic recruiting ~30% of admits — Williams is among the most athlete-heavy elite schools (NESCAC conference)
- Greek life DOES NOT EXIST at Williams (eliminated 1962)
- Williamstown location: rural, beautiful, isolated. Real fit consideration — students who want urban access often unhappy
- Williams-Mystic (marine sciences semester), Williams-Exeter Programme at Oxford — distinctive study-away options

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants (limited international aid)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
