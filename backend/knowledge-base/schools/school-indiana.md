# Indiana University Bloomington — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current IU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~32,000 undergrad enrollment, Bloomington IN
- Admit rate: ~78-82% overall, BUT direct admit to Kelley School of Business is ~30-35% of applicants
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or IU-direct application; rolling admission with priority deadlines
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for best Hudson/Holland scholarship + Kelley direct admit consideration
- RD deadline: Feb 1 for guaranteed scholarship consideration

## What admissions actually weights
IU's identity: large public flagship with two crown jewels — **Kelley School of Business** (top-10 undergrad business nationally) and **Jacobs School of Music** (top-3 conservatory in the U.S.). Most other admissions are relatively accessible. Strong applicants signal which crown jewel (or which Hutton Honors College direction) they're targeting.

**Direct admit to Kelley** is the single most important admissions decision for many applicants. Direct admits enter Kelley Day 1; non-direct must complete a sophomore "standards admission" with GPA + course thresholds (achievable but not guaranteed). The direct admit profile: ~3.85+ GPA, ~1380+ SAT, demonstrated leadership/business interest.

**Jacobs School of Music** requires audition + separate process. Acceptance is by instrument/voice — extremely competitive in some studios.

## Recent essay shifts
IU supplements ask why IU + which school within IU. For Kelley/Jacobs/Media School applicants, supplement quality matters significantly more than for College of Arts & Sciences applicants.

## Post-SFFA stance
IU continued holistic review post-SFFA with continued commitment to Indiana residents and underrepresented students.

## Demonstrated interest
IU formally tracks demonstrated interest. Visit + virtual sessions help, especially for direct-admit consideration.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Direct admit to Kelley** is THE play if you want business. Apply BY Nov 1 priority deadline. Don't gamble on standards admission unless you're confident in Indiana residency + Honors path
- **Hudson and Holland Scholars Program** — for first-gen, low-income, students of color: full-ride + summer bridge + community
- **IU Honors College / Hutton Honors** — automatic invitation to high stats; Honors thesis option
- Strong programs: Business (Kelley), Music (Jacobs), Informatics + CS (Luddy School), Media + Journalism (Media School), Public Health (Fairbanks), Education
- Big Ten campus culture — football, basketball (Hoosiers), Little 500 bike race, fraternity row visible
- Greek life is significant (~20% participation), more so than peer Big Tens
- Bloomington is a real college town — bars, restaurants, live music scene shaped by Jacobs grad students
- Korean + Asian student community is meaningful (~5-7% of undergrad)
- Out-of-state tuition is significant; Indiana resident pricing is very strong

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common for OOS)
- Heavy merit scholarships: Wells Scholarship (full ride + summer abroad — most prestigious), Provost Scholarships, Dean's Scholarships, Hutton Honors awards. OOS automatic merit based on stats — meaningful tuition reduction
- Indiana residents get strong in-state pricing + 21st Century Scholars program (free tuition for qualifying low-income IN residents)
