# Case Western Reserve University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current CWRU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,000 undergrad enrollment, Cleveland OH
- Admit rate: ~27% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + CWRU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Action (non-binding) + Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 15, EA Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
CWRU's identity: STEM-strong + research-heavy + pre-med pipeline + collaborative. Strong applicants articulate specific research/medical interests + engagement with Cleveland's medical hub (Cleveland Clinic + University Hospitals proximity).

Schools with distinct admit pathways:
- College of Arts & Sciences
- Case School of Engineering
- Weatherhead School of Management
- Frances Payne Bolton School of Nursing

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 31-34.

## Recent essay shifts
CWRU supplements ask about specific interests + Case-specific engagement.

## Post-SFFA stance
CWRU's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
CWRU DOES track demonstrated interest. Visits, EA/ED commitment all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 + EA all available. EA is non-binding non-restrictive — most applicants apply EA + something elsewhere
- Pre-med pipeline very strong (Cleveland Clinic + UH partnerships, 1+ year clinical opportunities possible)
- BS/MD program (Pre-Professional Scholars Program for Medicine) — guaranteed admit to CWRU Medical, EXTREMELY competitive (~3-5% of applicants)
- Engineering at Case is well-regarded — particularly Polymer Science (#1 in country) and Biomedical Engineering
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports, no athletic scholarships)
- Greek life ~30%
- Cleveland location: revitalized post-industrial city, lower cost of living, snowy winters

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Generous merit aid: Bolton Scholar (full ride + summer enrichment), Provost's Scholar, University Scholar
- CWRU is famous for using merit aid aggressively to attract high-stat applicants
