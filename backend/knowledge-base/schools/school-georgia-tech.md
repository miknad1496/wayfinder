# Georgia Tech — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Georgia Tech admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 STEM-focused, ~16,500 undergrad enrollment, Atlanta GA
- Admit rate: ~30% in-state, ~10% out-of-state (HUGE gap — GA residents preferred)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — Georgia Tech reinstated requirement for 2024-25 cycle
- Application: Common App + Georgia Tech-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Action 1 + Early Action 2 — both non-binding, non-restrictive. EA1 Oct 15, EA2 Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 4

## What admissions actually weights
Georgia Tech is STEM-focused — Engineering, Computing, Sciences, Industrial Design. Strong applicants combine technical strength with evidence of independent technical pursuits (research, projects, hackathons, robotics, code portfolios).

Test scores: REQUIRED. Middle-50 SAT 1450-1550, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts
Georgia Tech supplements ask about specific interests + technical motivation. Generic engineering essays don't land — AOs want to see specific technical curiosity.

## Post-SFFA stance
Georgia Tech's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Georgia Tech does NOT formally track demonstrated interest.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- GEORGIA RESIDENTS get massive preference (state public university preference)
- EA1 + EA2: non-binding non-restrictive, can apply ED elsewhere
- Major-specific admits: CS most competitive (~5-7% admit for OOS), Computational Media also very competitive
- College of Computing has multiple specializations (Intelligence, People, Devices, Systems & Architecture, Theory, Information)
- Athletic recruiting significant (ACC, but stronger in basketball/baseball than football)
- Greek life ~25%
- Atlanta location: vibrant city, strong tech presence, internship advantages
- Strong international student presence (~10-12% of class)

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For Georgia residents: very generous (HOPE Scholarship — full tuition for B average; Zell Miller Scholarship — full tuition + fees for top students)
- For OOS: meets demonstrated need but limited
- Stamps President's Scholars Program — full ride + summer enrichment + research, extremely competitive
- QuestBridge partner
