# Texas Christian University (TCU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current TCU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R2 research university (Disciples of Christ heritage), ~10,500 undergrad enrollment, Fort Worth TX (DFW)
- Admit rate: ~46-50%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + TCU supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- Early decision: Binding ED, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
TCU's identity: traditional Southern + Christian-affiliated (admits all faiths) + Neeley School of Business (top business in TX) + Bob Schieffer College of Communication (journalism) + Horned Frogs football culture + DFW location for internships. Strong applicants signal Texas/Southern fit + specific interest in business or communication.

**Neeley School of Business** — direct admit by major; Energy Finance + Business Information Systems are distinctive. AACSB-accredited, top-50 nationally.

**Schieffer College of Communication** — strong journalism + strategic communication; named after Bob Schieffer (CBS News).

Test scores: admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1410, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED** for clear top choice — ED admit lift ~1.4-1.6x
- **Chancellor's Scholar** — full ride + summer experiences
- **John V. Roach Honors College** — selective separate admission, Honors thesis
- Strong programs: Business (Neeley), Communication, Nursing, Pre-med pipeline (Texas medical schools), Education, Engineering (newer)
- D-I (Big 12) — Horned Frogs football + basketball; football is a real cultural anchor
- Greek life ~40% participation — significant; Greek life and football culture central
- **DFW location** — internship pipeline strong (American Airlines, Pier 1, Lockheed, AT&T HQ)
- Strong Texas + SW + Latino + Asian student communities
- Cost ~$80K/year sticker — merit aid offsets meaningfully for high stats

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~75-80% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: Chancellor's Scholar (full ride), Faculty Scholarship, Roach Honors Scholarship
- Texas residents: marginal pricing benefit; out-of-state students still attractive cost vs other private R1s
