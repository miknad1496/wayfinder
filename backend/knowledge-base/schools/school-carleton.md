# Carleton College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,000 undergrad, Northfield MN (45 minutes south of Twin Cities)
- Admit rate: ~17%
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + Carleton supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 15) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 15 ED2 / Jan 15 RD

## What admissions actually weights
Carleton is institutionally peculiar in three ways that drive admissions outcomes: (1) **Trimester academic calendar** (3 terms × 10 weeks each, 3 courses per term) — this creates an intense academic culture that's a real fit factor; applicants who articulate why they want trimester intensity score better. (2) Carleton has the highest per-capita PhD production rate of any U.S. college outside Caltech and Harvey Mudd — particularly in STEM (Carleton sends more graduates to PhD programs in physics, math, and chemistry than schools 10x its size). The "intellectual seriousness" is the institutional identity. (3) Carleton + St. Olaf rivalry is real (St. Olaf is across town in Northfield); the two schools share resources but have meaningfully different cultures (Carleton is secular + intellectual, St. Olaf is Lutheran + community-oriented).

Strong programs: Mathematics + Statistics (top LAC math department, Putnam team consistently top-20 nationally), Physics + Astronomy (own observatory + Cowling Arboretum for fieldwork), Computer Science, Economics, English, Geology (rare for a LAC, signature program), Music (strong department for a school this size).

The "Carleton vibe" — quirky, intense, slightly nerdy, intellectually playful — is a genuine cultural feature. AOs read essays for fit with this culture; sterile achievement-resume essays underperform versus essays showing intellectual quirk.

Test scores when submitted: middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 32-34. Submission rate ~50-60% for admits.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~30-35%; ED2 admit rate ~25-30%; RD ~10-15% — ED is meaningful leverage at Carleton
- Trimester calendar is intense + 3-week winter break + spring "reading" days create distinctive rhythm
- 80% of students study abroad (one of highest rates among U.S. colleges)
- "Comps" (senior comprehensive exam/project, ~6-month integrative work) is required and academic-defining
- Greek life: NONE (Carleton is one of few selective LACs without Greek system)
- Northfield MN: rural Minnesota, harsh winters (real fit factor), small-town culture
- Cowling Arboretum (880 acres of restored prairie, woodland, river) is a signature campus feature
- Strong feeder school for graduate programs in STEM and social sciences

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants (need-aware for international)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan policy for families under ~$80K income (Carleton replaces loans with grants)
- Cap on student loan debt at graduation (~$25K total max)
- Strong endowment per-student ($800K+ per undergrad)

## Recent essay shifts
Carleton's "Why Carleton" supplement explicitly asks applicants to engage with intellectual culture. AOs read for whether the applicant connects to specific Carleton features (trimester intensity, Comps, Cowling Arb, the academic seriousness without pre-professional pressure). Generic "small classes + great professors" essays underperform versus essays that reference specific quirks (the "Schiller statue tradition," the cabin in the Arb, cookies at midnight in 4th Burton, Convocation in Skinner Memorial Chapel).
