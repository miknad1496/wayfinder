# Northwestern University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Northwestern admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~8,200 undergrad enrollment, suburban Chicago (Evanston)
- Most recent admit rate: ~7-8% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Northwestern-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (ED) — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 2

## What admissions actually weights
Northwestern's identity: pre-professional + academically rigorous + collaborative. Strong applicants articulate specific career-pipeline interest (Medill journalism, Bienen music, McCormick engineering, Kellogg pre-business pipeline, pre-med via Feinberg connections). Generic "I love Northwestern" essays don't land.

The school has 6 undergraduate divisions with distinct profiles:
- Weinberg College of Arts and Sciences
- Medill School of Journalism — separate competitive admissions
- Bienen School of Music — portfolio-required
- McCormick School of Engineering
- School of Communication
- School of Education and Social Policy

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1560, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Northwestern's "Why Northwestern?" essay is short but consequential. AOs explicitly look for specific program references — courses, faculty, research centers, clubs by name. The supplement asks about "what excites you intellectually" — answers that show specific intellectual obsessions land best.

## Post-SFFA stance
Standard Ivy-peer post-SFFA approach: expanded adversity-essay options, context-sensitive reading.

## Demonstrated interest
Northwestern DOES consider demonstrated interest. Visiting Evanston, attending info sessions, opening admissions emails all factor in. ED commitment is the strongest signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Northwestern is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is significant (~1.7-2x vs RD for comparable applicants)
- Medill journalism is the most competitive Northwestern division — apply directly if interested, internal transfer is hard
- Bienen requires audition (music students)
- Athletic recruiting ~13-15% of admits, Big Ten conference (basketball, football significant)
- Northwestern's quarter system (3 quarters/year, more intense pace) is a real lifestyle consideration
- The campus is suburban Evanston, 30 min from Chicago — best of both worlds appeal but commuter-feel for some

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for families under $90K
- Reduced loans for families up to ~$130K
- No merit-only scholarships (limited Posse Scholar partnership)
- QuestBridge partner
