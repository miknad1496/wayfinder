# Brown University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Brown admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Ivy League, ~7,200 undergrad enrollment
- Most recent admit rate: ~5.0-5.5% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: REQUIRED — Brown reinstated requirement March 2024 for 2025-26 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Brown-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (ED) — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 5

## What admissions actually weights
Brown's "Open Curriculum" is central to admissions identity. Brown has no general education requirements — students design their own academic path. Successful applicants articulate genuine engagement with this freedom: a clear sense of intellectual direction PLUS willingness to explore widely. AOs read for "intellectual self-direction" — students who can drive their own learning without prescriptive structure.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1570, ACT 34-35.

The Brown applicant tends toward intellectually independent, often quirky, and values-driven. Students who want a structured Core (Columbia) or pre-professional pipeline (Penn Wharton) are rarely the right Brown fit.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Brown's supplements include the well-known "Why Brown?" essay focused specifically on the Open Curriculum. Generic "I love freedom in academics" answers don't land. AOs want concrete evidence: specific courses across departments you'd take, a thoughtful articulation of how the Open Curriculum maps to your particular intellectual goals.

The "What you'd bring to Brown" essay rewards specificity — concrete contributions, not abstract values.

## Post-SFFA stance
Brown's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay opportunities and explicit messaging about contextual reading of applications.

## Demonstrated interest
Brown does NOT explicitly track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is a strong signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Brown is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.6-1.8x vs RD for comparable applicants)
- The Program in Liberal Medical Education (PLME) — 8-year combined BA/MD program — has separate ultra-competitive admissions (~3% admit rate). Apply directly to PLME if interested
- Brown + Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) dual-degree — separate competitive process
- Athletic recruiting ~14% of admits, significant in Ivy fall sports
- Open Curriculum is a real fit-test — students who do better with structure (or want pre-med pipeline focus) often don't thrive at Brown

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for most international applicants (limited international aid)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students (since 2022)
- Families under $125K income: zero parent contribution (one of more generous policies)
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
