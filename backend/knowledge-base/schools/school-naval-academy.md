# U.S. Naval Academy (Annapolis) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Federal service academy (Department of the Navy), ~4,500 undergrad ("midshipmen"), Annapolis MD
- Admit rate: ~9% (one of the most selective admit rates in U.S. higher ed; comparable to MIT/Stanford)
- Test policy: REQUIRED
- Application: USNA-specific online application (NOT Common App)
- Early round: NONE — single application cycle
- Deadline: Jan 31 (with rolling considerations starting fall)

## What admissions actually weights
USNA is institutionally peculiar in five ways no other U.S. college matches: (1) **Five-year service obligation post-graduation**: Every USNA graduate commits to 5 years of active-duty service in the Navy or Marine Corps after commissioning as an officer (Ensign in Navy, 2nd Lieutenant in Marine Corps). This is not optional — applicants must accept this commitment. (2) **Congressional nomination required**: Most applicants need a nomination from a U.S. Senator, House Representative, or the Vice President to even be considered. The nomination is its own competitive process — applicants apply to multiple Members of Congress simultaneously starting in spring of junior year of high school. (3) **Free education with stipend**: USNA is free (tuition + room + board + health care + uniforms + books all covered) AND midshipmen receive monthly pay (~$1,200/month). The total compensation value is ~$500K for 4 years. (4) **CFA + Medical Exam required**: Candidate Fitness Assessment (push-ups, sit-ups, pull-ups, basketball throw, mile run, shuttle run) AND a DODMERB medical exam. Both are real disqualifiers. (5) **Honor Concept** governs all conduct — "A midshipman does not lie, cheat, or steal." Violations result in dismissal.

Strong programs: Engineering (signature — top undergraduate engineering programs in U.S., particularly Aerospace, Mechanical, Naval Architecture), Mathematics, Physics, Operations Research, Cyber Operations (USNA was first U.S. school with a Cyber Operations major, 2013), Political Science, Economics, Languages (Arabic, Chinese, Russian, Korean, Spanish, French — all signature for national security).

The "Mid" identity — disciplined, physical, mission-focused, high-stakes — is not for everyone.

Test scores: REQUIRED. Middle-50 SAT 1310-1490, ACT 28-32. Math is heavily weighted (engineering core).

## Strategic notes
- BEGIN PROCESS JUNIOR SPRING — the pre-application work (nomination, fitness, medical) takes 6+ months
- Apply for Congressional nomination in summer before senior year — multiple sources (your 2 Senators + 1 House Rep + VP)
- Service academies do NOT use Common App — separate USNA portal
- Greek life: NONE
- Athletics: D1 in Patriot League (some), Independent in football (notable Army-Navy game)
- Notable alumni: 6 Presidents have visited as Commander-in-Chief; many alumni include Sen. John McCain, Adm. Chester Nimitz, astronauts (15+ alumni went to space), Jimmy Carter
- Distinctive features: Tecumseh, Bancroft Hall (largest single dormitory in U.S.), Plebe Summer (6-week boot camp before academic year), Herndon Monument climb (plebe-year tradition)
- Service Selection (5L → Navy or Marine Corps + community choice) at end of senior year

## Aid + scholarship
- TUITION-FREE — no financial aid considerations needed
- Monthly stipend ~$1,200/month
- All books, room, board, uniforms, health care covered
- Service obligation = effective payment for the education

## Recent essay shifts
USNA admissions essays specifically ask why applicants want to serve as commissioned officers in the Navy/Marines. Generic "I want a free college" essays fail catastrophically. AOs read for genuine commitment to military service + leadership orientation + intellectual capability for the engineering-heavy curriculum. The "why service" question is the entire admit profile.
