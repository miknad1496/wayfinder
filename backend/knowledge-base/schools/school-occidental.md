# Occidental College ("Oxy") — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Occidental admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,900 undergrad enrollment, Eagle Rock (LA) CA
- Admit rate: ~38-42%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Oxy supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Occidental's identity: small LAC with a top-25 LAC ranking + Los Angeles location (rare for selective LACs — most are New England) + strong international relations + deep diplomatic + UN-feeder pipeline + diverse student body (Oxy is consistently among the most demographically diverse top LACs). Strong applicants articulate why specifically the LA-LAC combination matters — most "small LAC" applicants are choosing between New England schools, so Oxy needs you to explain LA fit.

**Diplomacy and World Affairs (DWA)** is THE differentiator program. Top-tier IR placement at State Department + UN + NGOs. Combined with the Kahane United Nations Program (semester at the UN).

**Kahane UN Program** — Oxy students spend a fall semester living + interning + studying at the UN in NYC. Ranked best UN-immersion program of any U.S. LAC.

**Barack Obama spent his freshman + sophomore years at Oxy** before transferring to Columbia. Real institutional pride.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1330-1490, ACT 30-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Oxy supplements include "Why Oxy" and questions about community fit. The DWA program + LA location + Latino/Latina-focused programs (Oxy is a Hispanic-Serving Institution) are differentiators worth articulating.

## Post-SFFA stance
Occidental maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to underrepresented + first-gen + Hispanic students (Oxy is an HSI — among the only top-25 LACs designated as Hispanic-Serving).

## Demonstrated interest
Oxy tracks demonstrated interest. Visit Eagle Rock (15 min from downtown LA), engage with virtual sessions.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Oxy if clear top choice — ED admit lift is significant (~1.5x)
- **Oxy Promise** — full need met for low-income students (under $50K AGI for many years)
- **Mathilde Karamooz Scholars** — for Iranian-American + Middle Eastern students
- **Robert Sun Scholars** — STEM-focused scholarship + research stipend
- Strong programs: Diplomacy and World Affairs (DWA — top-tier IR), Politics, Economics, Geology (the LA geology is academically valuable), Sociology, Theater, Art History, Cognitive Science, Critical Theory
- **Eagle Rock + LA location** — campus is in NE LA. 15 minutes from downtown. Strong career pipeline in entertainment + tech + government + nonprofit
- D-III athletics (SCIAC) — Tigers; not the campus identity
- Greek life — none (Oxy doesn't have it)
- Strong Hispanic + Latino + Asian + International + Black student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $80K+/year — but meets need fully for low-income students
- "Unofficial Cosby Hall" + "Stearns Hall" + the "Quad" — students cite specific places by name

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need (some packages include loans for higher-income families)
- Limited merit aid — Oxy is primarily need-based (vs CMC + Mudd which have more merit at peer LACs)
- QuestBridge partner
