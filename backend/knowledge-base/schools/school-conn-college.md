# Connecticut College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Conn College admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,800 undergrad enrollment, New London CT
- Admit rate: ~36-42%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Conn College supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Connecticut College's identity: NESCAC LAC + coastal location (overlooks Long Island Sound + Thames River) + **Honor Code** (real, enforced — self-scheduled exams, take-home tests) + **CISLA** (Center for International Studies and the Liberal Arts — funded research/internships abroad) + strong dance + theater + music + study abroad. Strong applicants articulate Honor Code fit + interdisciplinary curiosity.

**Honor Code** — students self-schedule exams (you choose when in finals week), take-home tests are common, doors stay unlocked. Cultural anchor since 1922.

**CISLA (Center for International Studies and the Liberal Arts)** — distinctive among LACs. Selective program funds international internship + senior thesis combining a foreign language + study abroad + culminating project. Strong post-grad placement at NGOs + foreign service + nonprofits.

**CELS (Center for Career and Professional Development)** — institutional commitment to funded summer internships for ALL students. Distinctive among LACs.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Conn College if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Stewart Scholar** — top merit + summer experiences + leadership
- **CISLA Scholar** — selective program, separate competitive review; international internship funding
- **Holleran Scholarship** — for first-gen / underrepresented students with merit + need
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- Strong programs: Government, International Studies, Environmental Studies, Music (audition-required for major), Dance, Theater, Art History, English (strong creative writing), Botany, Anthropology
- **Dance program** is among the top LAC dance programs — pre-professional pipeline
- **Botanical garden** on campus (Connecticut College Arboretum) — academic resource for botany + ecology + environmental studies
- D-III athletics (NESCAC) — Camels (one of the more unusual LAC mascots)
- Greek life — none
- **New London CT setting** — coastal small city, on Long Island Sound. 90 min from Boston, 2 hours from NYC. Working-class town with maritime history (US Coast Guard Academy adjacent)
- Strong international + arts-focused student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $85K+/year — meets need fully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens + permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need (some packages include loans)
- Substantial merit: Stewart Scholar, CISLA, Holleran, Posse Scholar, Music + Dance Talent Awards (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
