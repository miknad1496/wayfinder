# University of Chicago — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UChicago admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~7,500 undergrad enrollment, Hyde Park Chicago
- Most recent admit rate: ~5-6% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 + Early Action — UChicago offers ALL THREE. ED1/ED2 binding, EA non-binding non-restrictive
- RD deadline: ~Jan 6

## What admissions actually weights
UChicago's identity: intellectual rigor at the extreme, theory-heavy, "the life of the mind." Strong applicants articulate genuine intellectual obsessions — specific ideas, books, problems they've grappled with. The Core Curriculum (broad foundational requirements across humanities/social sciences/biological sciences/physical sciences/civilizations/arts/math) is central to UChicago identity.

The famous UChicago supplements: weird, philosophical, deliberately challenging prompts. Past examples: "Find x," "What can actually be divided by zero?", "What does Play-Doh have to do with Plato?". Strong applicants embrace the weirdness with genuine intellectual playfulness — generic earnest answers don't land.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1570, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
The supplement structure remains: 1 standard "Why UChicago?" + 1 weird/creative prompt of your choice from a published list. The creative prompt list rotates each year; pick the one that genuinely sparks something for you.

## Post-SFFA stance
UChicago's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. UChicago has been less publicly vocal than peers but continues commitment to socioeconomic diversity.

## Demonstrated interest
UChicago does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment + EA enthusiasm read meaningfully.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 + EA all available simultaneously. Most strategic: apply EA + ED1 (if comfortable with binding); EA gives early read while ED1 maximizes commitment signal
- Unhooked ED admit lift is significant (~1.7-2x vs RD)
- The Booth-bound undergrad track: economics/business/statistics is major-heavy; pre-business pipeline is strong
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports, no athletic scholarships)
- The Core Curriculum is real intellectual rigor — students who want a flexible curriculum (Brown's Open Curriculum) are often less happy at UChicago
- Hyde Park location: urban, intellectual neighborhood, but isolated from rest of Chicago. Real fit consideration

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-blind for international applicants (since 2018)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans
- Families under $125K income: zero parent contribution (Empower Initiative)
- Limited merit aid: Odyssey Scholarship (need-based with merit boost), Police/Fire/Educator scholarships
- QuestBridge partner
