# Wake Forest University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Wake Forest admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~5,500 undergrad enrollment, Winston-Salem NC
- Admit rate: ~21% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently (Wake Forest was test-optional pre-pandemic)
- Application: Common App + Wake Forest-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Wake Forest's identity: small + Southern + rigorous academics + tight-knit. The "Pro Humanitate" motto reflects emphasis on character and service. Strong applicants articulate engagement with the Wake Forest culture (smaller-than-peer-school feel, deep community, balance of academic + social).

Wake Forest's "Why Wake Forest?" essay carries particular weight — they explicitly value applicants who genuinely fit the culture vs prestige-chasing.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1380-1500, ACT 31-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Wake Forest supplements famously ask creative/personality-revealing questions. Past examples: "What's your favorite word and why?", "List your top 5 books." Authentic + specific answers land best.

## Post-SFFA stance
Wake Forest's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Wake Forest DOES track demonstrated interest. ED commitment, visits, info session attendance all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump is significant (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked)
- School of Business (junior-year admit, internal application)
- Athletic recruiting significant (ACC conference)
- Greek life ~50% of campus (very high — central social structure)
- Strong pre-med + pre-law pipelines
- Smaller alumni network than peer schools but high engagement
- Winston-Salem location: smaller Southern city, traditional feel

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $50K (lower threshold than peer schools)
- Generous merit aid: Reynolds Scholarship (full ride), Carswell Scholarship, Stamps Scholar
