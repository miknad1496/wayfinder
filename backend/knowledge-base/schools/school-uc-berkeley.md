# UC Berkeley — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Berkeley admissions + UC system policies.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~32,000 undergrad enrollment, Berkeley CA
- Admit rate: ~12% overall in-state (CA), ~7% out-of-state
- Test policy: TEST-BLIND through 2026-27 (UC system policy, all 9 campuses)
- Application: UC application (separate from Common App), 4 personal insight questions (PIQs) — pick 4 of 8 prompts
- Early round: NONE (UC system doesn't offer early decision/action)
- Deadline: Nov 30 (UC system universal deadline)

## What admissions actually weights
Berkeley's Comprehensive Review uses 13 factors. Most heavily: GPA (UC-weighted), course rigor, personal insight questions, extracurricular leadership, special talents/skills. NO recommendation letters required. NO interviews.

The 4 PIQs are your only essay opportunity — each 350 words. Strong applicants treat each as distinct facets, not repetitive.

In-state advantage: California residents get meaningfully higher admit rates than out-of-state (~12% vs 7%). California applicants also pay $14K/year tuition vs $44K out-of-state.

Test scores: TEST-BLIND. Don't submit. Berkeley will not consider scores.

## Recent essay shifts
PIQ structure stable. Berkeley reads for "Berkeley fit" — civic engagement, intellectual seriousness, evidence of independent learning.

## Post-SFFA stance
UC system was already race-blind for 25+ years (Prop 209, 1996). Berkeley's post-SFFA approach was minimal change — they had already developed contextual reading without race.

## Demonstrated interest
Berkeley does NOT consider demonstrated interest. UC system policy.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- College/major choice matters: Engineering and Computer Science (in L&S or EECS) are FAR more competitive than other majors. CS/EECS admit rates ~6% even for in-state
- Haas Business School: junior-year admit, separate process. Not a freshman path
- M.E.T. (Management, Entrepreneurship, Technology) — combined CS+Business 4-year program, EXTREMELY competitive direct freshman admission
- Berkeley does NOT offer Early Action
- For OOS applicants: Berkeley is need-aware in admissions and provides limited financial aid to OOS. Cost is substantial
- Berkeley grade deflation is real in some majors

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For California residents: very generous (Cal Grant + Pell + Blue and Gold Opportunity Plan = no tuition for families under $80K)
- For out-of-state: limited institutional aid; OOS pay full freight typically
- Regents' Scholarship (most prestigious merit award) — full tuition + research stipends for ~3% of admits
