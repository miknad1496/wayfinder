# Rhode Island School of Design (RISD) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current RISD admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private art and design college, ~2,000 undergrad enrollment, Providence RI
- Admit rate: ~21-26%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + RISD supplements + **portfolio + RISD-specific drawing assignments** (the Bicycle drawing + 2 portfolio drawings — the famous "Bicycle Drawing" requirement)
- Early decision: Binding ED, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
RISD's identity: **the most selective art and design school in the U.S.** + interdisciplinary studio-based curriculum + Brown University cross-registration (Brown/RISD dual-degree available) + Foundation Year (year-long required intro to all studio practices before declaring major) + post-graduate placement at every major design firm + "RISD culture" (rigorous, conceptual, conceptual-art rather than commercial-art orientation). Strong applicants signal serious portfolio + intellectual + conceptual engagement with art-making.

**Portfolio is THE central admissions factor** — not test scores, not GPA. Applicants submit 12-20 pieces of varied work demonstrating drawing skill, conceptual thinking, range of media, willingness to experiment.

**RISD's famous "Bicycle Drawing" requirement** — applicants must submit a drawing of a bicycle. This sounds quirky but is taken seriously — it tests observation, line quality, spatial reasoning. **Don't draw a generic bicycle. Engage with the assignment intentionally.**

**Foundation Year** — required for all undergrads. First year is shared studio practice (drawing, design, spatial dynamics, painting) before declaring a major sophomore year. Students often describe it as transformative.

**RISD/Brown Dual Degree** — 5-year program; receive BFA from RISD + BA from Brown. Selective separate admission. ~10-15 students per year.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1300-1450, ACT 28-32. Test scores matter less here than at peer privates due to portfolio-centric admissions.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **The portfolio + Bicycle Drawing is THE differentiator** — invest 6-12 months on portfolio before applying. Take art classes, get critique, iterate
- **Apply ED Nov 1** for clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful (~1.4-1.6x for portfolio-strong applicants)
- **RISD/Brown Dual Degree** — apply if interested in combining academic + studio practice
- **National Portfolio Day** — RISD admissions reps review portfolios at NPDA events nationwide. Attend if possible — feedback shapes your final portfolio
- **Wattles + Hammer Foundation Scholarships** — competitive merit + portfolio-based
- **RISD merit aid** — limited but available for top portfolios
- Strong programs: All BFA tracks — Painting, Sculpture, Illustration, Graphic Design, Industrial Design, Architecture (5-year B.Arch), Interior Architecture, Apparel Design, Furniture Design, Glass, Printmaking, Photography, Film/Animation/Video (FAV), Ceramics, Textiles
- **RISD Industrial Design** is among top in the world — alumni at Apple, Tesla, Nike, Patagonia, Herman Miller, IDEO
- **RISD Graphic Design** — top-tier; alumni at every major design firm
- **RISD Architecture** — distinctive; 5-year B.Arch is NAAB-accredited
- Brown University across the street — cross-registration is real (most RISD students take 2-4 Brown courses)
- D-III athletics — minimal campus athletics culture
- Greek life — none
- **Providence RI setting** — historic mill city, vibrant arts scene, walkable, 90 min from Boston
- Cost is significant — sticker $80K+/year — meets need partially; merit is portfolio-based

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~80-85% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Wattles Scholarship, Hammer Foundation, RISD Merit Awards (portfolio-based)
- International applicants: highly need-aware
