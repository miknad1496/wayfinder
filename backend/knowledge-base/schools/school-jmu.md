# James Madison University (JMU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current JMU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R2 research university, ~20,000 undergrad enrollment, Harrisonburg VA (Shenandoah Valley)
- Admit rate: ~78-82% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + JMU supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
JMU's identity: mid-size Virginia public + College of Business + College of Health and Behavioral Studies + College of Education + Honors College + traditional collegiate culture + JMU Madison Vision (commitment to civic engagement) + Dukes athletics. Strong applicants signal Honors + College of Business or Health Sciences interest.

**Honors College** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated dorm + priority registration. ~1,100 students.

**College of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; strong Hospitality Management + Finance + Marketing.

**College of Health and Behavioral Studies** — strong Pre-PT, Pre-OT, Athletic Training, Nursing, Communication Sciences and Disorders programs.

**Madison Vision Series** — required curricular component asking students to engage with civic + ethical questions. Reflects the JMU culture of "engaged civic life."

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1140-1310, ACT 24-29. Honors trends ~100-150 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for Honors + scholarship consideration
- **Centennial Scholar** — top automatic merit for high-stats students
- **Honors College Scholar** — competitive separate scholarship
- **First-Year Innovation Award** — for entrepreneurial + creative students
- Strong programs: Business (College of Business), Communication Sciences and Disorders (top-15), Health Sciences (Pre-PT/OT pipeline), Nursing, Education, Music (audition required), Engineering (newer, growing)
- **JMU "Madison Experience"** — specific freshman year program emphasizing community + civic engagement
- D-I (Sun Belt — recently transitioned from CAA FCS to FBS) — Dukes football + basketball
- Greek life ~17-20% participation
- **Harrisonburg VA setting** — Shenandoah Valley college town, beautiful Blue Ridge Mountains. 2 hours from DC + Charlottesville. Real college town integrated with the city
- Strong Virginia + DC-area + Korean + Asian + International student communities
- Cost: in-state moderate, out-of-state moderate; merit aid offsets meaningfully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Centennial Scholar, Honors College Scholar, First-Year Innovation Award, Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- Virginia residents get strong in-state pricing + VGAP for low-income VA residents
