# Southern Methodist University (SMU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current SMU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 research university (Methodist heritage), ~7,100 undergrad enrollment, Dallas TX
- Admit rate: ~52-58%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + SMU supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority Nov 1
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
SMU's identity: Dallas private + Cox School of Business (strong corporate finance pipeline) + Lyle School of Engineering + Meadows School of the Arts + traditional Texas-collegiate culture + Greek-centric. Strong applicants signal Dallas business pipeline interest OR specific Meadows arts interest.

**Cox School of Business** — direct admit by major; one of the strongest undergrad business programs in the SW. Strong placement at Texas energy + finance + consulting firms.

**Meadows School of the Arts** — distinctive; combines theater, dance, music, art history, and arts management. Audition-required for performance majors.

**Lyle School of Engineering** — competitive direct admit; Computer Science + Mechanical + Civil strong.

Test scores: admitted middle-50 SAT 1320-1490, ACT 30-33.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for clear top choice — ED admit lift ~1.5x
- **President's Scholars Program** — full ride + summer abroad + leadership. Top scholarship; competitive separate review
- **Hunt Leadership Scholars** — second-tier merit + leadership cohort
- **Discovery Scholars** — first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship
- Strong programs: Business (Cox), Engineering (Lyle), Meadows arts, Pre-Law + Political Science, Communication, Sport Management, Energy Management (distinctive)
- D-I (American Athletic Conference) — Mustangs football; recently transitioned from C-USA, growing competitively
- Greek life ~50% participation — extremely Greek-centric culture
- **Dallas location** — strong internship pipeline (energy, finance, consulting, marketing, sports)
- Strong Texas + Latino + Asian + International student communities
- Cost significant — sticker $85K+/year — President's Scholars tier offsets fully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~80-85% of demonstrated need
- Substantial merit: President's Scholars (full ride + summer + leadership), Hunt Leadership, Discovery Scholars, Meadows Talent Awards (audition-based)
- Texas residents: marginal benefit; out-of-state students still attracted by Dallas + business + merit aid
