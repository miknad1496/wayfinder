# Grinnell College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,750 undergrad, Grinnell IA (rural Iowa, 50 miles east of Des Moines)
- Admit rate: ~10-12%
- Test policy: Test-blind permanently (Grinnell is one of very few selective LACs that does not consider test scores at all)
- Application: Common App + Grinnell supplements
- Early round: ED1 (Nov 15) + ED2 (Jan 1) — both binding
- Deadline: Nov 15 ED1 / Jan 1 ED2 + RD

## What admissions actually weights
Grinnell is institutionally distinct in four ways: (1) **Open curriculum** — Grinnell has NO general-education distribution requirements (only a first-year tutorial). Students design their own program with adviser. This is one of the most-extreme open curricula at any U.S. college (more open than Brown's, since Brown still has concentration requirements). (2) **Test-blind not just optional** — Grinnell does not consider SAT/ACT even if submitted. AOs explicitly read for academic depth via essays, recommendations, and curriculum rigor only. (3) **Wealthiest U.S. LAC by endowment per student** — Grinnell's endowment is ~$2.6B, divided over only 1,750 students ($1.5M+ per student), making it one of the wealthiest schools in higher ed per capita. This funds extraordinary financial aid + extraordinary student programming + new facilities. (4) **Social-justice heritage**: Grinnell was a stop on the Underground Railroad, founded by abolitionists in 1846, and the institutional identity remains progressive + activist. Active LGBTQ+ community, strong civic-engagement culture.

Strong programs: Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics + Statistics, Computer Science (signature program for an LAC — top-15 LAC CS), English, History, Anthropology, Sociology, Economics, Political Science, Russian Studies (signature small program).

The "Grinnellian" identity — quirky, intellectually serious, progressive, willing to be in rural Iowa for the right academic culture — is genuine. Applicants must signal acceptance of the location.

Test scores: BLIND. Don't include test scores; they will not be reviewed.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 admit rate ~25-30%; ED2 admit rate ~22%; RD ~9-10% — ED is meaningful leverage
- Test-blind is genuine — do not include scores in any application materials
- Greek life: NONE
- Iowa rural location: Grinnell IA is genuinely rural (population ~9,000), surrounded by farmland, harsh winters
- 60% of students study off-campus (study abroad + domestic exchange)
- Notable alumni: Hank Aaron's batting coach Ralph LaPointe attended; Robert Noyce (Intel co-founder) graduated 1949
- Strong feeder to PhD programs in social sciences + humanities
- Distinctive features: Bob's Underground (the campus pub run by students), the Burling Library, JRC (Joe Rosenfield Center)

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-blind for U.S. applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- No-loan policy for U.S. applicants — Grinnell replaces all student loans with grants
- Trustee Honor Scholarship (full-ride merit, separate competitive application)
- Massive endowment makes Grinnell among the most affordable selective LACs by net price

## Recent essay shifts
Grinnell's "Why Grinnell" supplement specifically asks how the open curriculum + test-blind policy + rural Iowa location fit the applicant's intellectual interests. Applicants who treat Grinnell as a backup to Williams/Amherst score worse than applicants who articulate why open curriculum specifically matters and why being in rural Iowa is acceptable (or attractive). Self-direction + intellectual independence are explicitly valued.
