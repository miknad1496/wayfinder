# Washington University in St. Louis (WashU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current WashU admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~7,500 undergrad enrollment, St. Louis MO
- Most recent admit rate: ~10-12% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; WashU-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 2
- RD deadline: ~Jan 4

## What admissions actually weights
WashU's identity: strong academics + collaborative culture + genuinely happy students (consistently top-rated student happiness). Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the academic culture (interdisciplinary openness, no required core, structured exploration).

Five undergraduate schools — most students apply to Arts & Sciences. Engineering (McKelvey), Architecture (Sam Fox), Business (Olin), Art (Sam Fox) all separate.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1480-1560, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
WashU's supplements include "Why WashU?" — looks for specific program references. Strong applicants name specific courses, faculty, research opportunities.

## Post-SFFA stance
WashU's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. WashU has been criticized historically for low Pell-eligible enrollment but has been increasing it.

## Demonstrated interest
WashU IS a school where demonstrated interest matters. ED commitment, visits, info session attendance all factor in.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH available, both binding. Significant ED admit boost
- Unhooked ED admit lift at WashU is among the strongest of peer schools (~2-2.5x vs RD)
- WashU's "scholarship arms race" — historically used merit scholarships aggressively to boost yield/profile. Some merit aid still available
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports, no athletic scholarships)
- Olin Business is highly regarded for undergraduates wanting business
- Pre-med pipeline strong (BJC Health System proximity, WashU Medical School integration)
- St. Louis location: more Midwestern feel, lower cost of living, urban setting around Forest Park

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens since 2021 (relatively recent shift to need-blind)
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $75K
- Reduced loans up to ~$100K
- Generous merit aid: Danforth Scholars (full tuition), Albright Scholars (engineering full tuition), John B. Ervin Scholars
- QuestBridge partner
