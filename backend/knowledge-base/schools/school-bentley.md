# Bentley University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Bentley admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private business-focused R2 university, ~4,200 undergrad enrollment, Waltham MA (Boston metro)
- Admit rate: ~52-58%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Bentley supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 15
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Bentley's identity: **U.S. business-only undergrad university** (most peer privates have multiple schools; Bentley is purely business + business-adjacent disciplines) + AACSB-accredited + strong corporate finance + accounting + analytics pipelines + Boston-metro location + integrated technology + business curriculum + Falcons athletics. Strong applicants articulate clear business career interest + appreciation for the focused (rather than broad) academic experience.

**Business-only model** — Bentley's distinctive structure means EVERY student takes the business core. Liberal arts requirements are lighter than peer LACs (English, history, math required but reduced). Students who want broad liberal arts should choose differently.

**Required Arts and Sciences major** — alongside business core, students declare a "major" (Accounting, Finance, Marketing, etc.) AND a "minor" in arts and sciences (English, Philosophy, History, etc.). Distinctive integration.

**Strong placement** — Bentley grads have one of the highest starting salaries among private U.S. universities (top-15 by some measures); accounting + finance pipeline at Boston firms (Fidelity, State Street, Wayfair, Bain, etc.) is significant.

**Center for Women and Business** — distinctive among business schools; advocates for + researches gender equity.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1430, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Bentley if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Trustee Scholar** — top merit + summer experience + leadership cohort
- **Forte Scholar** — competitive scholarship for women in business
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package
- Strong programs: Accounting (top-15), Finance, Information Systems Audit + Control, Marketing, Management, Business Analytics + Information Systems, Economics + Finance, Corporate Finance + Accounting, Sustainability Studies (newer)
- **Bentley Sports Studio** is distinctive — TV production studio for sports media training
- D-II athletics (NE-10) — Falcons; D-II is unusual for a school of this profile but real
- Greek life ~30% participation
- **Waltham MA setting** — Boston suburb (15 mi west of downtown). Charles River nearby; Cambridge + Harvard + MIT 25 min away. T transit accessible
- Strong international student community (~20-25%) — particularly Asian + South Asian + Eastern European
- Cost significant — sticker $80K+/year — meritsub-stantially offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~85-88% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Trustee Scholar, Forte Scholar, Posse, NMF package, Brennan Scholarship (top need-based)
- International applicants: highly need-aware
