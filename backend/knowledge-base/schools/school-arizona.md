# University of Arizona — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Arizona admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~36,000 undergrad enrollment, Tucson AZ
- Admit rate: ~85-88% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Arizona institutional application; rolling admission
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: May 1

## What admissions actually weights
Arizona's identity: large public flagship + Eller College of Management + Lunar + Planetary Laboratory (NASA partnerships — Apollo astronaut training site) + College of Optical Sciences (top-3 nationally) + Honors College + automatic merit scholarships for OOS high stats + Wildcats athletics. Strong applicants signal Honors + scholarship targeting OR specific interest in space sciences (LPL is unique).

**Lunar and Planetary Laboratory (LPL)** — UArizona is THE undergrad space science destination. NASA partner on multiple Mars missions; OSIRIS-REx asteroid sample return (Arizona-led) returned in 2023. Undergrads work on real mission data.

**College of Optical Sciences** — top-3 globally for optics + photonics. Distinctive undergrad B.S. in Optical Sciences + Engineering.

**Eller College of Management** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; Management Information Systems is top-5 nationally.

**Honors College** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated dorm.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1130-1370, ACT 22-29. Honors trends ~150-200 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for scholarship + Honors consideration
- **Wildcat Excellence Award** — automatic full-tuition for top OOS stats; meaningful financial flex
- **Distinguished Wildcat** — second-tier automatic merit
- **Excellence Award** — third-tier automatic merit
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package + research stipend
- **Stamps Scholarship** — full ride + summer enrichment
- Strong programs: Optical Sciences (top-3 globally), Astronomy + Planetary Sciences (LPL), Eller Business (esp. MIS), Geosciences, Anthropology (top-10 anthropology nationally), Mining + Geological Engineering, Atmospheric Sciences, Pharmacy (PharmD), Public Health
- D-I (Big 12 — transitioned from Pac-12) — Wildcats basketball + football; basketball especially competitive (Lute Olsen heritage + 1997 NCAA champion)
- Greek life ~15-20% participation
- **Tucson setting** — Sonoran Desert beauty, mountain views, much more affordable than Phoenix; ~200 days of sunshine; 2 hours from Phoenix
- Hot summers (105°F+); air-conditioned campus is real
- Strong Hispanic + Latino + Asian + International student communities (Arizona is HSI)
- Cost: in-state low, out-of-state moderate; merit aid offsets significantly

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial automatic merit: Wildcat Excellence (full tuition top OOS), Distinguished Wildcat, Excellence, NMF package, Stamps
- Arizona residents get strong in-state pricing + Arizona Promise (free tuition for qualifying low-income AZ residents)
- Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) — neighboring states reduced OOS
