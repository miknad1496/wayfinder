# Tufts University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Tufts admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~6,800 undergrad enrollment, Medford MA (Boston suburb)
- Most recent admit rate: ~9-10% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Tufts-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Tufts' identity: intellectually curious + civic-engaged + slightly quirky + collaborative. The school motto "Pax et Lux" reflects emphasis on peace + global engagement (international relations is a core strength).

Five undergraduate schools:
- School of Arts and Sciences (most students)
- School of Engineering
- Tufts University at Tisch College of Civic Life (interdisciplinary civic engagement)
- School of the Museum of Fine Arts at Tufts (SMFA — combined BFA/BA, separate admissions)
- Combined-Degree Programs

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1450-1540, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Tufts supplements famously include "Tell us about something that excites your intellectual curiosity" prompts. AOs look for genuine intellectual engagement — specific topics, ideas, problems the applicant has wrestled with.

## Post-SFFA stance
Tufts' post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Tufts has been historically committed to socioeconomic diversity.

## Demonstrated interest
Tufts DOES track demonstrated interest meaningfully — historically known for "Tufts Syndrome" (yield protection): rejecting overqualified applicants assumed to be using Tufts as safety. ED commitment is strong defense; visits, info session attendance also help.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump significant (~1.6-1.9x for unhooked applicants) AND ED is the strongest defense against yield protection
- Yield protection (Tufts Syndrome) is REAL: high-stat applicants applying RD without demonstrated interest get rejected at higher rates than expected. Solutions: visit, write thoughtful Why-Tufts essay, apply ED if it's genuinely top
- International Relations program is among the strongest undergraduate IR programs in the country (Fletcher School graduate proximity)
- Engineering at Tufts is well-regarded but smaller than peer engineering programs
- SMFA combined-degree program: 5-year BA/BFA, separate creative review
- Athletic recruiting modest (D3 sports, no athletic scholarships)
- Medford location: 15 min from Boston, suburban feel but Boston access

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need
- Loan-free for families under $60K
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
