# University of Texas at Austin — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UT Austin admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~42,000 undergrad enrollment, Austin TX
- Admit rate: ~31% overall, but auto-admit thresholds for TX residents change calculation
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2025-26 (returning to required possibly 2026-27)
- Application: ApplyTexas (Texas-specific) or Common App + UT-specific supplements
- Early round: NONE (rolling-priority deadline structure instead)
- Priority deadline: Oct 15 / Final: Dec 1

## What admissions actually weights
UT Austin uses Texas's distinctive "Top 10%" rule: Texas high school students in top 6% of their class are automatically admitted (was top 10%, now reduced). For non-auto-admit Texas students and OOS, admission is by holistic review.

Major-specific admits: UT admits BY MAJOR. McCombs Business, Computer Science, Engineering all have separate, more competitive admits than the general university.

Texas residents get MASSIVE preference: state law caps OOS enrollment at ~10% per class. OOS admit rates are dramatically lower for the same profile.

Test scores: median admit middle-50 SAT 1230-1480 (with auto-admit Texas students dragging down), ACT 27-34.

## Recent essay shifts
ApplyTexas/UT supplements include short essays — strong applicants articulate specific UT major + Austin engagement.

## Post-SFFA stance
Texas had been race-blind for years (House Bill 588 — "Top 10%" rule). Minimal change post-SFFA.

## Demonstrated interest
UT does NOT formally track demonstrated interest.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- TEXAS RESIDENTS, top 6% of class: automatic admit
- TEXAS RESIDENTS, NOT top 6%: holistic review, can still get in but it's more competitive
- OUT-OF-STATE: very competitive, OOS cap means only top-tier applicants succeed
- McCombs Business + CS Department: separate admits, much more competitive than the rest of UT
- Plan II Honors — small honors college, competitive
- Athletic recruiting MAJOR (Big 12 → SEC starting 2024, football culture central)
- Greek life ~15%
- Austin location: vibrant city, growing tech sector (Apple, Google, Tesla all expanding presence), live music scene

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For Texas residents: generous (TEXAS Grant + various). McCombs students get business-specific aid
- For OOS: less generous, OOS pay ~$58K vs in-state $11K
- Forty Acres Scholarship — full ride + summer enrichment, EXTREMELY competitive (~20-30 students/year)
- Plan II Honors students get additional scholarship opportunities
