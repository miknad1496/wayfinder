# Stony Brook University (SUNY) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Stony Brook admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university (SUNY system), ~17,000 undergrad enrollment, Stony Brook NY (Long Island)
- Admit rate: ~44-48% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or SUNY application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 15 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15 priority; final May 1

## What admissions actually weights
Stony Brook's identity: SUNY's research powerhouse + medical center adjacent (Stony Brook Medicine) + strong physics + Simons Center for Geometry & Physics + applied math + biomedical research. Strong applicants signal STEM/research targeting + understanding of the medical-school pipeline that Stony Brook delivers.

**Honors College** — selective, separate application required, GPA + essay + interview component for top candidates. Honors students get smaller seminars + thesis + dedicated programming.

**WISE (Women In Science and Engineering)** program — distinctive STEM pipeline for women; mentorship + research.

**University Scholars** — separate competitive scholarship program, ~150 students/year, full-tuition + research stipend.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
Stony Brook supplements include a "Why Stony Brook" + program-specific prompt for Honors College and University Scholars applicants. The supplement quality matters significantly for those programs.

## Post-SFFA stance
Stony Brook continued holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to NY residents + first-gen + Pell-eligible students.

## Demonstrated interest
Stony Brook does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but campus visit + virtual session attendance documented for top scholarship applicants.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 15** EA for Honors + University Scholars + best scholarship consideration
- **University Scholars** — full-tuition scholarship + research opportunities + summer experiences. Apply by Dec 1 for Fall — most competitive
- **Honors College** — separate competitive admission within Stony Brook. ~650 students total. Smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated dorm (Mendelsohn Quad)
- **WISE program** — for women in STEM, mentorship + research opportunities
- **Pre-med pipeline via Stony Brook Medicine** — significant clinical research access; co-op-style hospital exposure
- Strong programs: Computer Science (top-50), Applied Mathematics + Statistics, Physics (Simons Center for Geometry & Physics is on campus), Biomedical Engineering, Marine Sciences, Journalism (Newsday-affiliated), Nursing
- **Stony Brook Medicine** — academic medical center on campus; one of largest hospital systems in NYC metro
- Long Island setting — ~60 miles from NYC, accessible by LIRR
- Korean + Asian + South Asian student communities are very strong (~25-30% Asian/Asian-American)
- D-I athletics (CAA) — Seawolves; not the campus identity
- Greek life small (~6-8%)
- Beaches + state parks accessible (Sunken Meadow, Robert Moses)
- Cultural fit note — academically intense, less party-school than many SUNYs

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- Substantial merit: University Scholars (full-tuition), Provost Scholarship, Honors College Scholarship, Engineering Scholarships
- New York residents get strong in-state pricing + NYS Excelsior Scholarship (free tuition for qualifying low-income NY residents at SUNY/CUNY) + TAP
- Out-of-state tuition still relatively low compared to peer R1s — Stony Brook is a strong cost-conscious option for Korean + international families
