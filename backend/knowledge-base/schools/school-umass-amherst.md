# University of Massachusetts Amherst — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UMass Amherst admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~24,000 undergrad enrollment, Amherst MA
- Admit rate: ~62-66% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + UMass Amherst supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 5 for scholarship + Commonwealth Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
UMass Amherst's identity: New England public flagship + Isenberg School of Management + College of Information & Computer Sciences (CICS — top-25 nationally) + Five College Consortium (cross-registration with Amherst, Smith, Mt. Holyoke, Hampshire — distinctive) + Commonwealth Honors College + Minuteman athletics. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting + Commonwealth Honors interest.

**Five College Consortium** — UMass Amherst students can take classes at four nearby selective LACs (Amherst College, Smith, Mt. Holyoke, Hampshire). Free PVTA bus connects all five. Cross-registration adds significant academic depth that no other public flagship offers. Real differentiator.

**Commonwealth Honors College (CHC)** — selective separate admission; ~3,800 students total; Honors thesis + smaller seminars + dedicated dorms (CHC residential complex).

**Isenberg School of Management** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; Sport Management + Hospitality + Tourism + Finance especially strong.

**College of Information & Computer Sciences (CICS)** — top-25 nationally; selective direct admit for CS majors.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1450, ACT 28-33.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 5** for CHC + Isenberg + CICS direct admit + scholarship consideration
- **Stockbridge Award** — top merit + leadership programming
- **Chancellor's Award** — second-tier automatic merit for high-stats OOS
- **21st Century Leaders Scholarship** — first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship
- **Commonwealth Honors College** — competitive separate admission within UMass app; Honors thesis required
- Strong programs: Computer Science (CICS top-25), Business (Isenberg — esp. Sport Mgmt + Hospitality), Engineering (Mechanical, Chemical, Civil), Linguistics (top-15), Public Health, Education, Hotel + Restaurant Management
- **Isenberg Sport Management** — top-3 nationally; alumni at MLB + NFL + NBA front offices
- D-I (Atlantic 10 for basketball, MAC/FBS Independent for football) — Minutemen
- Greek life ~6-8% participation — small for a public R1
- **Amherst MA setting** — Pioneer Valley, beautiful New England small-town. ~30K residents + students from 5 colleges = vibrant culture. 90 min from Boston, 3 hours from NYC
- "ZooMass" reputation (party school) was decades ago — current campus is more academically focused
- Strong international + Asian + Latino student communities
- Cost: in-state moderate, out-of-state competitive with merit

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- Substantial merit: Stockbridge, Chancellor's Award, 21st Century Leaders, Isenberg Direct Admit Scholarships
- Massachusetts residents get strong in-state pricing + MASSGrant + State Tuition Waiver
- New England Regional Student Program (RSP) — neighboring states reduced OOS for selected majors
