# Columbia University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Columbia admissions site and Common Data Set before relying on production answers.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 Ivy League, ~6,500 undergrad enrollment across 3 undergraduate schools
- Most recent admit rate: ~3.9-4.5% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle (announced policy continuation)
- Application: Common App or Coalition; Columbia-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (ED) — BINDING. Deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Columbia evaluates by SCHOOL — three undergraduate schools have distinct admissions:
- Columbia College (CC, liberal arts): main admissions track, broad academic profile
- Columbia Engineering (SEAS): STEM focus, slightly different applicant profile
- General Studies: separate admissions for non-traditional students (transfers, returning students)

The Core Curriculum is central to Columbia's identity. Students must opt into a fixed set of foundational humanities/science courses regardless of major. Successful Columbia applicants articulate genuine interest in this Core (not generic "I love NYC").

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1560, ACT 34-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Columbia famously asks for "List your favorite books, movies, theatrical productions, music, songs, podcasts, websites, and more" — the "lists" question. The trick: be specific, varied, and authentic. Applicants who list expected highbrow choices (Tolstoy, Beethoven) without genuine engagement read as performative. Specific and weird beats predictable and impressive.

The "Why Columbia?" essay must reference the Core Curriculum substantively. Generic NYC-loves don't land.

## Post-SFFA stance
Columbia's post-SFFA approach has been similar to other Ivies — adversity essays expanded, race-conscious admissions ended, context-sensitive reading emphasized.

## Demonstrated interest
Columbia does NOT explicitly track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment matters significantly.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED at Columbia is binding. Unhooked ED admit lift is real (~1.5-2x vs RD for comparable applicants)
- Columbia + Sciences Po dual BA, Columbia + Trinity College Dublin dual BA: separate ultra-competitive admissions tracks
- Engineering vs CC choice matters strategically — engineering has different applicant profile, slightly higher admit rate for stats-strong applicants
- Athletic recruiting is significant in fall sports (rowing, fencing, squash particularly)
- The Core Curriculum is a real fit-test — students who don't want to take Lit Hum / CC don't end up happy at Columbia

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international (with strong international aid pool, but selective)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- Families under $66K income: zero parent contribution
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
