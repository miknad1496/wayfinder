# Binghamton University (SUNY) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Binghamton admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university (SUNY system), ~14,000 undergrad enrollment, Binghamton NY (upstate)
- Admit rate: ~40-44% overall (one of the most selective SUNYs)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or SUNY application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Discovery + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Jan 15 priority; final Mar 1

## What admissions actually weights
Binghamton's identity: "Public Ivy" of the SUNY system + strong School of Management (one of the most selective SUNY business programs) + Watson School of Engineering + Decker College of Nursing & Health Sciences + Harpur College of Arts & Sciences. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (SOM, Watson Engineering) since those have separate competitive admissions.

**School of Management (SOM) Direct Admit** — competitive for business applicants. Profile ~1400+ SAT + 3.85+ GPA. Non-direct = pre-business through Harpur, then internal transfer (achievable but competitive).

**Watson School of Engineering** — direct admit by major (CS, Mech, Bio-Med, etc.). Computer Science is the most competitive at Watson.

**Discovery Program** — first-gen / underrepresented student scholarship + summer bridge + mentorship — separate competitive application within SUNY app.

**Binghamton Scholars** — top-tier honors program; distinct from Harpur Honors. Apply if top stats.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1480, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
Binghamton supplements include "Why Binghamton" + a SOM-specific prompt for business applicants. The supplements matter for direct admit decisions.

## Post-SFFA stance
Binghamton continued holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to NY residents + first-gen + Pell-eligible students.

## Demonstrated interest
Binghamton does NOT formally track demonstrated interest. Campus visit common but not weighted.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 1** EA for SOM direct admit + Binghamton Scholars + Discovery + best scholarship
- **Binghamton Scholars Program** — top-tier merit scholarship + Honors-style cohort + research opportunities. Apply by Dec 1
- **Discovery Program** — for qualifying first-gen + underrepresented students. Includes summer bridge + mentorship + smaller cohort
- **Bartle Library Honor System** is real — students leave laptops out without theft. Cultural anchor
- Strong programs: School of Management (top-50 nationally), Computer Science (Watson), Biomedical Engineering, Nursing (Decker), Pharmacy + Pharmaceutical Sciences (PharmD direct entry), Music, Theater Production
- D-I athletics (America East) — Bearcats; not campus identity
- Greek life ~10% participation
- Tower Pond + Nature Preserve on campus — a natural beauty rare for an R1
- Binghamton is upstate NY — economically depressed area; campus is the dominant institution + employer
- 3.5 hours from NYC, 4 hours from Boston, 5 hours from DC
- Strong Korean + Chinese + South Asian + East European student communities
- "SUNY Bing" is a common shorthand — pride identity is real
- Cost is THE selling point — exceptional academic outcomes at SUNY pricing

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions (NY residents prioritized)
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common for OOS)
- Substantial merit: Binghamton Scholars (full-tuition + research), Presidential Scholarship, Provost Scholarship, Watson Engineering Scholarships
- New York residents get strong in-state pricing + NYS Excelsior Scholarship (free tuition for qualifying low-income NY residents at SUNY) + TAP
- Out-of-state pricing competitive for the academic quality — strong cost-conscious option for OOS + Korean/international families seeking selective SUNY value
