# Vanderbilt University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Vanderbilt admissions site and Common Data Set before relying.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~7,100 undergrad enrollment, Nashville TN
- Most recent admit rate: ~6-7% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27 cycle
- Application: Common App, Coalition, or QuestBridge accepted
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: ~Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Vanderbilt evaluates by school: Arts & Sciences (most students), Engineering, Peabody (education), Blair (music — audition required), Owen (graduate), and separate undergraduate Business Minor. Most undergraduates apply to A&S or Engineering.

Vanderbilt's identity: highly selective + Southern + strong financial aid + collaborative culture. The "Vandy applicant" tends to combine academic strength with collaborative leadership and genuine community engagement.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1500-1570, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts (2024-2026)
Vanderbilt's supplements include short answers about Vanderbilt-specific interests. AOs value specificity — naming actual programs (Immersion Vanderbilt, the Commons residential community, College Advisors program, specific research centers).

## Post-SFFA stance
Standard post-SFFA approach: expanded adversity-essay options, context-sensitive reading.

## Demonstrated interest
Vanderbilt DOES track demonstrated interest meaningfully. Visiting Nashville (where feasible), attending virtual info sessions, ED commitment all factor in. Vanderbilt has been increasingly competitive about demonstrated interest in recent years.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH available, both binding. Strong second-chance window if ED1 doesn't work out
- Unhooked ED admit lift at Vanderbilt is significant (~1.7-1.9x vs RD)
- Athletic recruiting ~12% of admits, SEC conference (football, basketball significant)
- Greek life ~40% of campus — central social structure
- Nashville location: vibrant city, strong music/healthcare industries, growing tech presence
- Commons residential program: all freshmen live together on the Commons in their first year — distinctive community-building feature

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and undocumented students
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need WITHOUT loans for ALL admitted students (Opportunity Vanderbilt — among the most generous aid policies)
- Families below median income: zero parent contribution
- Generous merit aid: Cornelius Vanderbilt Scholarship, Ingram Scholars Program, Chancellor's Scholarship — all competitive but more available than peer Ivies
- QuestBridge partner
