# George Washington University (GW) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~12,000 undergrad enrollment, Washington DC (Foggy Bottom)
- Admit rate: ~43% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently
- Application: Common App + GW supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING
- Deadline: Nov 1 ED1, Jan 5 ED2 / Jan 5 RD

## What admissions actually weights
GW's identity: DC location + politics/IR pipeline + diverse + pre-professional. Strong applicants articulate specific DC-internship interests.

The Elliott School of International Affairs is one of the strongest undergraduate IR programs nationally.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1300-1460, ACT 29-33.

## Strategic notes
- ED1 + ED2 both binding
- Yield protection real at GW — high-stat RD applicants without demonstrated interest get rejected
- Elliott School IR strongest program
- Athletic recruiting modest (Atlantic 10, no football)
- Greek life ~20%
- Foggy Bottom location: WORLD BANK + IMF + State Department literally next door
- DC internship advantage real and significant

## Aid + scholarship
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~80% of demonstrated need (varies)
- Presidential Academic Scholarship, Trustee Scholarship, Stephen Joel Trachtenberg Scholarship for DC-area students

## Recent essay shifts
- Supplements push for specificity about DC engagement — "I want to work in policy" without naming an organization, course, or faculty interest reads as filler. The strongest essays name a Foggy Bottom institution (e.g., a specific State Department bureau, IMF research division, or a GW center like the IPDGC).
