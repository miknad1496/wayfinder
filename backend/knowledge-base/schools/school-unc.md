# University of North Carolina (UNC Chapel Hill) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UNC admissions site + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~19,000 undergrad enrollment, Chapel Hill NC
- Admit rate: ~24% in-state, ~9% out-of-state (HUGE gap — NC residents heavily favored)
- Test policy: Required as of 2025-26 cycle (returned to required)
- Application: Common App + UNC-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Action — non-binding, non-restrictive. Deadline Oct 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
UNC's identity: "public Ivy" + strong school spirit + Carolina blue culture + diverse academics. Strong applicants articulate engagement with UNC's traditions and academic offerings.

NORTH CAROLINA RESIDENTS GET MASSIVE PREFERENCE: state law caps OOS enrollment at 18% per class. Result: in-state admit ~24%, OOS admit ~9% — among the largest in-state preferences in U.S.

Test scores: REQUIRED for 2025-26 cycle. Middle-50 SAT 1320-1490, ACT 29-33.

## Recent essay shifts
UNC supplements ask about specific interests. The "What community matters to you" prompt is high-leverage.

## Post-SFFA stance
UNC's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Combined with state OOS cap, UNC's class composition shifted post-SFFA.

## Demonstrated interest
UNC does NOT formally track demonstrated interest.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- IN-STATE NC RESIDENTS: UNC is a target/match for strong applicants. OOS applicants need exceptional profile
- EA is non-binding non-restrictive. Most applicants apply EA
- Kenan-Flagler Business School: junior-year admit (similar to UVA McIntire)
- Athletic recruiting MAJOR (ACC, basketball especially — UNC has won 6 NCAA basketball championships)
- The "Carolina Way" culture is genuine
- Robertson Scholars Program (cross-enrollment with Duke) — full scholarship, very competitive
- The Old Well, Polk Place, Carolina-blue tradition

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For NC residents: very generous (Carolina Covenant — debt-free for families under ~200% of poverty line)
- For OOS: meets demonstrated need but less generous (OOS cost ~$60K)
- Morehead-Cain Scholarship — full ride + summer stipends, the most prestigious merit scholarship
- Robertson Scholars Program — full ride
- QuestBridge partner
