# Arizona State University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current ASU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~80,000+ undergrad (largest US university by enrollment), Tempe AZ (multiple campuses + online)
- Admit rate: ~88-90% overall, BUT **Barrett Honors College ~55-60%** and **W. P. Carey Business direct admit ~70-75%**
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: ASU institutional application (Common App also accepted)
- Early action: rolling — apply early for best scholarship + Barrett consideration
- RD deadline: Aug 1 for Fall (rolling effectively closes earlier as cohorts fill)

## What admissions actually weights
ASU's identity: largest US university + #1 in Innovation (per US News for 9+ years) + Barrett Honors College (one of the largest + highest-performing honors programs in the nation) + extensive online programs + "New American University" charter. Strong applicants understand ASU is broadly accessible BUT Barrett Honors is the differentiator and worth the targeted effort.

**Barrett Honors College** is THE play for high-stats applicants. Barrett is essentially a "private LAC inside a mega-university" — own residence halls, own faculty, own seminars, Honors thesis required. Barrett admission requires separate application + GPA ~3.7+ + SAT ~1300+ (test-optional but matters at the margin) + supplemental essays + interview for top candidates. Worth applying even as a stretch — the experience is meaningfully different from non-Honors ASU.

**W. P. Carey School of Business** — direct admit competitive, one of largest business schools in U.S.; Honors students get small-cohort experience.

**Walter Cronkite School of Journalism** — top-3 journalism nationally; strong placements.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1140-1370, ACT 22-28. Barrett trends ~150-250 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
ASU supplements are minimal vs peer flagships. Single short essay most years. Barrett supplemental requires thoughtful response — generic answers don't get in.

## Post-SFFA stance
ASU continued holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to "no student left behind" charter — broad access remains the institutional identity.

## Demonstrated interest
ASU does NOT formally track demonstrated interest. Apply early matters more than visits.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Barrett Honors College** is the differentiator — apply if you want depth
- **New American University Scholarship** — automatic for high stats (no separate application). OOS students with high GPA + test scores can drop tuition by 50%+ — meaningful financial flex
- **Provost's Scholar / Fulbright Scholarship** — separate competitive applications, full-tuition + summer abroad + research stipend
- Strong programs: Engineering (Schools of Engineering #1 in size), Computer Science, Sustainability (top-5 globally), Journalism (Cronkite, top-3), Business (W.P. Carey), Aerospace Engineering, Architecture (Herberger), Music (Herberger), Film (Herberger New American Film School), Hugh Downs School of Communication
- **5 ASU campuses** — Tempe (main, classic college town), Polytechnic (engineering tech focus, 30 min east), West Valley (newer, Arizona's largest), Downtown Phoenix (urban, journalism + nursing + public service), Lake Havasu (small, undergrad seminar)
- Big 12 athletics (transitioned from Pac-12) — Sun Devils football + basketball + sun + heat
- Hot. Summer 110°F+ regularly. Air-conditioned campus is real
- Greek life ~12-15% participation
- ~1,000 student organizations — vast diversity in extracurriculars
- "Maroon and Gold" identity is real
- ASU Online — separate but legitimate online degree pathway

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- ASU's New American University scholarships are AUTOMATIC + LARGE for high-stats OOS — Provost's Award (~$15K/yr), Dean's Award (~$11K/yr), University Award (~$8K/yr). Stack-able with merit + Barrett scholarship
- Arizona residents get strong in-state pricing + Arizona Promise (free tuition for qualifying low-income AZ residents)
- Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) — neighboring states (CA, NV, OR, etc.) get reduced OOS pricing if they meet WUE criteria
