# Hampshire College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Hampshire admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC (alternative/experimental), ~700 undergrad enrollment, Amherst MA
- Admit rate: ~64-72% (lower selectivity reflects self-selecting applicant pool)
- Test policy: **Test-blind** (does not consider scores even if submitted)
- Application: Common App + Hampshire supplements (significant essay-focused application)
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Feb 15
- RD deadline: Feb 15

## What admissions actually weights
Hampshire's identity: **the most experimental LAC in the U.S.** + no traditional grades (narrative evaluations only) + no traditional majors (students design their own concentrations called "Divisions") + no traditional GPA/SAT requirements + Five College Consortium access (UMass + Amherst College + Smith + Mt. Holyoke) + Division III Project (year-long thesis culminating undergraduate work) + recently survived a near-closure crisis (2019-21) and re-emerged with renewed mission. Strong applicants signal genuine intellectual independence + comfort with self-direction + serious creative or scholarly project work.

**Three Divisions structure**:
- **Division I**: explore broadly, take courses across schools (basics)
- **Division II**: focused concentration, design your own field of study with faculty advisor
- **Division III**: year-long thesis or significant creative project — equivalent of a master's thesis at most schools

**Five College Consortium** — Hampshire students take ~25-30% of their courses at UMass + Amherst College + Smith + Mt. Holyoke. Free PVTA bus connects all five.

**No grades** — faculty write narrative evaluations. Students who want letter grades for grad school applications can request them retroactively. Many students never see a grade.

**Test-blind** — institutional + intentional commitment.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** if Hampshire is clear top choice
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- **Hampshire Scholar** — competitive merit + faculty mentorship
- **First-Gen Scholar** — for first-generation college students with stipend + community
- Strong programs: Cognitive Science (Hampshire was a pioneer; Marvin Minsky was a faculty), Film/Photography/Video, Creative Writing, Animation, Computer Science (Hampshire is unique for offering CS at this scale of LAC), Studio Art, Theater, Politics + Social Justice, Critical Social Inquiry
- **Hampshire Animation** — strong pipeline; alumni at Pixar + Disney + Aardman
- **Lemelson Center** — entrepreneurship + invention focus; distinctive among LACs
- D-III athletics minimal — Hampshire is not athletics-driven
- Greek life — none
- **Amherst MA setting** — Pioneer Valley, Five College access. 90 min from Boston, 3 hours from NYC. Beautiful rural-college aesthetic
- Strong international + LGBTQ+ + first-gen + arts-focused student communities
- **Survived 2019-21 financial crisis** — Hampshire restructured significantly; new mission focus on "social justice + interdisciplinary work + activism" — newer applicants should be aware of the changed institutional context
- Cost: sticker ~$70K/year — meets need fully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Hampshire Scholar, First-Gen Scholar, Posse, Music + Visual Arts Talent Awards
- Five College Consortium discount programs
