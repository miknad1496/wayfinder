# Swarthmore College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Swarthmore admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,600 undergrad enrollment, Swarthmore PA (suburb of Philadelphia)
- Admit rate: ~7-9% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Swarthmore supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 4
- RD deadline: Jan 4

## What admissions actually weights
Swarthmore's identity: intense intellectual rigor + Quaker values (collaboration over competition) + small + politically engaged. Strong applicants combine academic strength with genuine intellectual curiosity AND values orientation.

The Honors Program: distinguishing feature. Junior + senior students can take small seminar-based "Honors" tracks culminating in oral exams by external examiners. Real intellectual rigor.

Tri-College Consortium (Swarthmore + Bryn Mawr + Haverford): course exchange and joint resources.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1450-1540, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Swarthmore supplements include "Why Swarthmore?" + a question about community. Strong applicants articulate engagement with Quaker values, intellectual collaboration, social justice orientation.

## Post-SFFA stance
Swarthmore's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Swarthmore does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Significant ED admit lift (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked)
- Engineering at Swarthmore: 4-year ABET-accredited program OR 3+2 dual-degree (3 at Swat + 2 at Penn/Caltech/RPI for B.S. in engineering)
- Honors Program: small, intense, prestigious. Junior/senior application
- Athletic recruiting ~25% of admits (Centennial Conference)
- No Greek life (eliminated 2019)
- Swarthmore's reputation for rigorous workload is real — students consistently report intense academic pressure
- Philly-suburb location: 30 min train to Center City Philadelphia

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, undocumented students, AND international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
