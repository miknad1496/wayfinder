# UC San Diego — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~33,000 undergrad, La Jolla CA
- Admit rate: ~24% in-state, ~32% out-of-state (UCSD specifically reads OOS slightly more favorably than the UC system average)
- Test policy: TEST-BLIND through 2026-27 (UC system policy — scores not considered even if submitted)
- Application: UC application + 4 PIQs (max 350 words each)
- Early round: NONE
- Deadline: Nov 30 (UC system deadline — the no-extensions rule is real)

## What admissions actually weights
The two non-obvious things about UCSD admissions: (1) the seven-college residential system means applicants rank colleges (Revelle, Muir, Marshall, Warren, Roosevelt, Sixth, Seventh) at apply time and that ranking actually matters for fit even if it doesn't change admit odds — Revelle is most STEM/core-curriculum-heavy, Sixth/Seventh are newest and emphasize culture/innovation. (2) Major-specific competitiveness: Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Bioengineering, Cognitive Science (a UCSD signature program), and Public Health are hyper-competitive direct admits. Pre-major paths into CS exist but the internal admit GPA cut is high (~3.7 in technical pre-reqs).

UCSD is uniquely strong in oceanography (Scripps Institution), cognitive science (founded the field), neuroscience, biological sciences — applicants targeting those should articulate fit specifically.

PIQs are the entire writing portfolio — there's no main essay. AOs read PIQs in 4-5 minutes total per file. Specificity wins; generic "I love science" responses lose.

Test scores: TEST-BLIND. Don't include scores in the PIQs.

## Strategic notes
- UCSD admits more STEM applicants than UCLA/Berkeley combined — it's the STEM "comparable target" school in the UC system
- Capped major changes: getting INTO CS/CE/Bioengineering after admit is harder than getting into UCSD as those majors directly
- Sun Belt UC — La Jolla weather is real recruiting advantage
- Triton Athletics is Division I now (joined Big West) — recruited athletes admit edge applies
- Greek life ~11%, low for a UC of this size
- UCSD does NOT consider demonstrated interest (UC policy)

## Aid + scholarship
- Cal Grant + Blue and Gold Opportunity (full tuition for CA families under ~$80K)
- Regents Scholarship — competitive merit, ~1.5% of admits
- Limited OOS institutional aid — UCs are need-aware for OOS
- Chancellor's Associates Scholars Program (CASP) for first-gen and underrepresented students

## Recent essay shifts
PIQ #4 (the "challenge" prompt) is the highest-leverage of the 4 PIQs post-SFFA — it's where AOs can read for context that race-conscious admissions can no longer provide. PIQ #1 (leadership) is also higher-weight than pre-2023 because it's where AOs read for character/maturity signals.
