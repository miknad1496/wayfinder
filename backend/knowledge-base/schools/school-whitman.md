# Whitman College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Whitman admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,500 undergrad enrollment, Walla Walla WA
- Admit rate: ~52-58%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Whitman supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Whitman's identity: Pacific Northwest LAC + collegiate small-town vibe + outdoorsy culture + strong sciences + intentional community ("the unwritten rules of Whitman" — collaborative, not cutthroat) + wine country setting (Walla Walla AVA). Strong applicants signal genuine interest in the PNW LAC experience specifically (vs Reed's intellectual intensity, vs Lewis & Clark's Portland-urban edge).

**Encounters Program** — first-year required interdisciplinary humanities sequence. Students often cite it as their most formative academic experience.

**Strong sciences for an LAC** — Biology, Chemistry, Geology (the Walla Walla geology is academically valuable), Environmental Studies. Premed/pre-vet placement is meaningful.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1330-1490, ACT 30-33.

## Recent essay shifts
Whitman supplements ask "Why Whitman" and a personal-context prompt. Specific articulation of Walla Walla appeal + small-town residential life + the Encounters Program land. Generic answers fail.

## Post-SFFA stance
Whitman maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + underrepresented + low-income students.

## Demonstrated interest
Whitman tracks demonstrated interest. Visit (especially during fall when wine country is beautiful), virtual sessions matter meaningfully.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Whitman if clear top choice — ED admit lift is significant (~1.5-1.7x)
- **Whitman Scholar** — top merit ($30K-40K/yr) + summer research stipend
- **Garrett Fellow** — first-gen + low-income merit + leadership cohort
- **Forrest Award** — community-engagement-focused scholarship
- Strong programs: Biology, Chemistry, Geology + Geosciences, Environmental Studies, Politics, Psychology, Studio Art, Music, Asian Studies (sister-college relationship with Hong Kong programs)
- **Walla Walla setting** — small wine-country town (33K) in southeastern WA. ~4 hours from Portland, 4 hours from Seattle. Real college town
- **Outdoor culture is huge** — climbing, skiing (Bluewood + Anthony Lakes), hiking, wine tasting, biking, river rafting
- **Sweet Onions** as the Whitman mascot (literal — Walla Walla sweet onions are the local crop) → student athletes are "Blues" (mascot was the Missionaries until early 2000s, then changed)
- D-III athletics (NWC) — Blues
- Greek life ~30-35% participation — significant for an LAC
- "The Unwritten Rules" — collaborative academic culture, students don't curve-compete
- Strong international student community for an LAC (~7-9%)
- Cost is significant — sticker $80K+/year — meritsub-stantially offsets for top admits

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Whitman Scholar (top tier ~$30-40K/yr), Forrest Award, Garrett Fellow, Music Talent Awards (audition-based), Visual Arts Talent Awards (portfolio-based)
- Need-blind for Washington state residents (selectively)
- QuestBridge partner
