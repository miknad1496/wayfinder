# University of South Carolina — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current USC-Columbia admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~27,000 undergrad enrollment, Columbia SC
- Admit rate: ~64-70% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or USC institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Oct 15 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Dec 1 priority; final Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
USC-Columbia's identity: SEC flagship + Gamecocks football + South Carolina Honors College (consistently ranked #1 or top-3 honors college nationally — THE differentiator) + Darla Moore School of Business + International Business top-1 nationally + Capstone Scholars Program. Strong applicants signal Honors College targeting + understand International Business is the academic crown jewel.

**South Carolina Honors College (SCHC)** — selective separate admission; ~700 students/cohort. **Consistently ranked #1 honors college in the nation** (per multiple rankings). Honors thesis + smaller seminars + dedicated programming + priority registration + Honors residence hall. Apply if top stats — the Honors experience genuinely competes with elite privates.

**International Business** at the Darla Moore School — **ranked #1 in the nation for 25+ consecutive years** (per US News). 1+1 program with international partner schools; mandatory study-abroad + foreign language + business core. Distinctive globally.

**Capstone Scholars Program** — second-tier honors-style program; smaller cohort + Honors-style seminars + Capstone residence hall.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1230-1390, ACT 26-31. SCHC trends ~150-200 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Oct 15** for SCHC + scholarship + Capstone consideration (extremely early deadline)
- **McNair Scholars** — full ride + summer abroad + research stipend. Top scholarship; competitive separate review including interview
- **Carolina Scholars** — second-tier merit
- **Stamps Scholars** — separate national scholarship program with USC partnership
- **National Merit Finalists** — guaranteed package
- **Top Scholar** — high-stat automatic merit
- Strong programs: International Business (#1), Business (Moore), Honors College (top-3 nationally), Communication + Journalism, Hospitality + Retail Management, Pharmacy (PharmD), Nursing, Education
- D-I (SEC) — Gamecocks football + basketball + women's basketball (national champions multiple times under Dawn Staley)
- Greek life ~20-25% participation
- **Columbia SC setting** — state capital, mid-size city (135K), 90 min from Charleston, 2 hours from Atlanta
- Strong Korean + Asian + Black + Latino student communities (significantly diverse for SE flagship)
- Cost: in-state low, out-of-state moderate; merit aid offsets meaningfully

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: McNair Scholars (full ride+), Carolina Scholar, Stamps Scholarship, NMF package, Top Scholar, Departmental Scholarships
- South Carolina residents get strong in-state pricing + LIFE Scholarship (S.C. residents with stats — automatic ~$5K/yr)
