# Pepperdine University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Pepperdine admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private religious-affiliated (Churches of Christ heritage) university, ~3,700 undergrad enrollment, Malibu CA
- Admit rate: ~38-43%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Pepperdine supplements
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Pepperdine's identity: small private + Christian-affiliated (Churches of Christ heritage but admits all faiths) + Malibu coastal campus + strong international study-abroad culture (Pepperdine has campuses in London, Florence, Heidelberg, Buenos Aires, Lausanne, Shanghai, Washington DC) + business + film. Strong applicants articulate genuine fit with the values-based community AND specific interest in the international experience.

**Seaver College** (the undergrad college) is the one most applicants apply to. Five schools within Pepperdine; Seaver is the residential undergrad, others are graduate.

**International Programs** are THE differentiator — ~60-70% of Pepperdine students study abroad at one of the international campuses (vs ~10-20% national LAC average). Build the application around this if it appeals.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1430, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
Pepperdine supplements include questions about values fit + community contribution. Specific examples of community service + faith-related reflection (or thoughtful articulation of values without that frame) land. Generic answers don't.

## Post-SFFA stance
Pepperdine maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to underrepresented and international students.

## Demonstrated interest
Pepperdine tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit (especially during fall preview events), virtual sessions, supplements showing real understanding of the school all count.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Christian-affiliated but admits all faiths** — explicit in their messaging. ~40-50% of students identify as Christian; ~50-60% from other backgrounds. Convocation attendance required (one chapel-style event per week)
- **International Programs** — almost every undergrad does at least one semester abroad. Apply familiar with which 3-4 international campuses interest you
- **Stalin Hahn Tennis Center** + **Firestone Fieldhouse** — D-I athletics are a real thing here despite small enrollment
- Strong programs: Business (top-50, fully accredited AACSB), Communication, Sports Medicine, Public Relations, International Studies, Religion & Philosophy, Psychology
- **Surf-and-study culture** — Malibu campus has Pacific views; cars are essential
- D-I athletics (West Coast Conference) — Waves basketball + tennis + golf
- Greek life ~30% participation — significant, particularly for non-religious students
- Strong international student presence (~10-12%)
- Korean + Asian student community is meaningful
- 30 minutes from LA + Hollywood (when traffic allows) — strong career pipeline in entertainment + finance + tech
- Cost is significant — sticker $90K+/year — but merit aid is available

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~85-90% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Presidential Scholarship (~$25K-30K/yr), Regents Scholarship, Pepperdine Scholarship, Athletic Scholarships (D-I)
- Religious heritage scholarships (Church of Christ + other denominational scholarships available)
- International applicants: highly need-aware
