# Florida State University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current FSU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 land grant university, ~33,500 undergrad enrollment, Tallahassee FL
- Admit rate: ~32-37%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or FSU institutional application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Oct 17 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Mar 1

## What admissions actually weights
FSU's identity: Florida public flagship + Seminole Tribe partnership (mascot is real cultural anchor — Seminole Tribe of Florida endorses) + College of Business + College of Communication + film school (Asolo Conservatory MFA pipeline + undergrad Film) + Honors Program + Garnet & Gold athletic culture. Strong applicants signal Honors targeting since broad admission is selective.

**University Honors Program** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming + priority registration.

**College of Business** — direct admit by major; AACSB-accredited; strong Insurance + Real Estate + Hospitality (Dedman School of Hospitality is top-15 nationally).

**College of Communication & Information** — top-25 nationally; strong Public Relations + Journalism.

**College of Motion Picture Arts (CMPA)** — selective film school, separate admission; one of top-15 undergrad film schools.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1280-1390, ACT 28-31. Honors trends ~80-100 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Oct 17** for Honors + Presidential + scholarship consideration (very early deadline)
- **Presidential Scholar** — full ride + summer experiences + leadership + research stipend
- **Garnet & Gold Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Service Scholar** — first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship + leadership
- **National Merit Finalist** — guaranteed package
- Strong programs: Business (esp. Insurance + Real Estate + Hospitality at Dedman), Communication + Information (top-25), Film (CMPA), Music (College of Music), Education, Criminology + Criminal Justice (top-3 nationally), Public Administration, Meteorology
- **Tallahassee setting** — state capital, mid-size city (200K), Spanish-moss-and-magnolia Southern aesthetic. 4 hours from Atlanta, 4 hours from Orlando + Disney
- D-I (ACC) — Seminoles football + basketball; football dynastic in 90s; baseball strong
- Greek life ~25% participation — significant; Greek life central to social life
- **Garnet & Gold + Doak Campbell Stadium** — football culture is real
- Strong Latino + Cuban-American + Caribbean + International student communities
- Florida residents get exceptional in-state pricing + Bright Futures Scholarship

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (gap common, especially OOS)
- Substantial merit: Presidential Scholar (full ride+), Garnet & Gold Scholar, Service Scholar, NMF package
- Florida residents get strong in-state pricing + Bright Futures Scholarship (state-funded for FL HS grads with stats)
