# Lewis & Clark College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Lewis & Clark admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,200 undergrad enrollment, Portland OR
- Admit rate: ~74-78%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Lewis & Clark supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Feb 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Dec 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Lewis & Clark's identity: PNW LAC + Portland-suburban location (Palatine Hill + Tryon Creek State Park adjacent) + strong study abroad (top-3 LAC participation) + International Affairs + Foreign Languages + Environmental Studies + Anthropology + traditional Pacific Northwest progressive culture. Strong applicants signal interdisciplinary curiosity + Portland-PNW fit + genuine global engagement.

**Overseas + Off-Campus Programs** — distinctive among LACs. ~70% of L&C students study abroad. The school operates its own programs in 14+ countries (East Africa, Russia, Japan, Australia, Argentina, etc.).

**International Affairs** — strong undergrad IR program; alumni at State Department + UN + NGOs.

**Environmental Studies** — strong; PNW environmental ecosystem (forests, rivers, Pacific Ocean) integrated into curriculum.

**Lewis & Clark Law School** — graduate; one of top environmental law programs nationally. Undergrads can pre-feed into JD via 3+3 program.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1430, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for Lewis & Clark if clear top choice
- **Trustee Scholar** — top merit + summer enrichment + leadership cohort
- **Dean's Scholarship** — second-tier automatic merit
- **Pioneer Scholarship** — for first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship + community cohort
- **Davis United World College Scholars** — for IB + UWC graduates
- Strong programs: International Affairs, Environmental Studies, Foreign Languages (Asian Studies + East Asian Languages strong), Anthropology, Sociology, Psychology, Music, History, English, Pre-Law (Law School pipeline)
- **3+3 Law program** — undergrads can complete bachelor's in 3 years + JD in 3 years (instead of 4+3)
- **Pioneer Log** student newspaper — historic, well-regarded
- D-III athletics (NWC) — Pioneers
- Greek life — none
- **Portland OR setting** — campus is on Palatine Hill in southwest Portland (Riverview neighborhood). 15 min from downtown Portland. PNW outdoor culture (hiking + biking + Forest Park nearby + Pacific Coast 90 min)
- Strong international + LGBTQ+ + progressive + arts-focused student communities
- "Lewis & Clarkers" identity emphasizes intellectual + creative + social-justice orientation
- Cost significant — sticker $80K+/year — generous merit + need aid

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Trustee Scholar, Dean's Scholarship, Pioneer Scholarship, Davis UWC, Music + Theater Scholarships (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
