# Centre College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Centre admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,400 undergrad enrollment, Danville KY
- Admit rate: ~67-73%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Centre supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED, deadline Dec 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Dec 1
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
Centre's identity: traditional Southern LAC + has **hosted 2 Vice-Presidential debates** (2000, 2012) which is institutional pride + Centre Commitment (4-year guarantee: graduate in 4 years OR 5th year free) + study abroad + small intentional cohort + Greek-influenced Southern collegiate culture. Strong applicants signal serious LAC fit + Kentucky-Southeast-Midwest comfort.

**Centre Commitment** — distinctive among LACs. Centre guarantees:
- (a) Graduation in 4 years
- (b) Study abroad opportunity
- (c) Internship or research experience
- If any commitment isn't met, Centre covers the 5th year tuition. Strong differentiator.

**Vice-Presidential Debates** — Centre hosted in 2000 (Cheney/Lieberman) and 2012 (Biden/Ryan). Cultural anchor + brand.

**Norton Center for the Arts** — on campus. Hosts professional touring concerts, theater, dance. Distinctive cultural amenity for a small Kentucky LAC.

**3-2 Engineering** — bachelor's from Centre + bachelor's from Columbia + Vanderbilt + Wash U + Kentucky engineering schools. 5-year combined.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1190-1370, ACT 26-31.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED Dec 1** for Centre if clear top choice — ED admit lift meaningful
- **Brown Fellow** — full ride + summer abroad + leadership cohort. Top Kentucky-based undergraduate scholarship
- **Lincoln Scholar** — top merit + summer experience
- **Posse Scholar** — Posse Foundation partnership; full ride for cohort
- Strong programs: Economics + Business (strong placement at SE banks + finance), Religion + Philosophy, Government (politics is the Centre wheelhouse), Education, Music, Pre-Med, Pre-Law (Vanderbilt + Kentucky law school pipeline)
- D-III athletics (SAA) — Colonels; football historically strong for an LAC of this size
- Greek life ~50-60% participation — significant; Greek life central to social life (especially for in-state and Southern students)
- **Danville KY setting** — small town (~17K), 35 min from Lexington (Kentucky's college town), 90 min from Louisville. Real college town integrated with the town
- Strong Kentucky + Southeast + International student communities
- Cost: sticker $55K+/year (lower than peer LACs) — Centre is among the more affordable selective LACs

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Brown Fellow (full ride), Lincoln Scholar, Posse, Trustees' Scholarship, Music Scholarships (audition-based)
- Kentucky residents: in-state-equivalent through some scholarship structures + Kentucky Educational Excellence Scholarship (KEES) for Kentucky HS grads
