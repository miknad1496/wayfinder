# University of Virginia (UVA) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UVA admissions site + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1, ~17,000 undergrad enrollment, Charlottesville VA
- Admit rate: ~17% in-state, ~14% out-of-state
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + UVA-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision (binding) + Early Action (non-binding, non-restrictive). ED Nov 1, EA Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
UVA's identity: "public Ivy" academics + Jeffersonian honor culture + collaborative student-self-governance. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with UVA's distinctive culture (the Honor Code, the Lawn rooms tradition, student self-governance).

Three undergraduate schools:
- College of Arts & Sciences (most students)
- McIntire School of Commerce — junior-year admit, internal application
- School of Engineering and Applied Science

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1410-1510, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
UVA supplements ask about specific interests + UVA-specific engagement. Generic Jefferson-loves don't land.

## Post-SFFA stance
UVA's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. UVA has been working to maintain socioeconomic diversity post-SFFA.

## Demonstrated interest
UVA does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is a strong signal.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED + EA BOTH offered. ED is binding, EA is not. Many students apply EA + ED to different schools
- Unhooked ED admit lift is real (~1.4-1.6x vs RD)
- McIntire Commerce: junior-year admit only. Pre-business pipeline through Commerce is strong but requires GPA + internal application
- Athletic recruiting significant (ACC conference)
- The Honor Code: single-sanction expulsion for lying/cheating/stealing. Real cultural feature
- Greek life ~30%
- Charlottesville location: small college town, beautiful setting

## Aid + scholarship policy
- For Virginia residents: generous (AccessUVA — meets full demonstrated need without loans for families under $80K)
- For OOS: meets full need but more limited — OOS pay $61K vs in-state $22K
- Jefferson Scholars Program — full ride + research stipend, extremely competitive
- QuestBridge partner
