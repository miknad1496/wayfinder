# University of Alabama — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current UA admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~33,000 undergrad enrollment, Tuscaloosa AL
- Admit rate: ~80-85% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Apply Yourself; rolling admission with priority deadlines
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 15 for best scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: May 1 (rolling effectively closes earlier)

## What admissions actually weights
Alabama's identity: massive Crimson Tide football culture + automatic merit scholarships drive significant OOS enrollment + Honors College + Culverhouse Business + Capstone College of Nursing + traditional Southern public collegiate. Strong applicants signal Honors targeting since broad accessibility means standard admit is mostly automatic; the real question is which scholarship + Honors tier.

**The HEADLINE: automatic merit scholarships for high stats** — Alabama publishes its merit chart. Specific GPA + test score combos automatically qualify for full-tuition or full-ride OOS scholarships. **This is THE reason high-stats students from anywhere consider Alabama.** Examples (verify current — these change):
- Presidential Scholars: ~$28K/yr automatic (high-stats threshold)
- UA Scholar: full tuition automatic (specific GPA + SAT)
- Foundation Scholar: full ride + summer + research (top tier — competitive)

**Honors College** — automatic invitation for high-stats; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming + priority registration.

**Computer-Based Honors Program (CBHP)** — distinctive STEM Honors-within-Honors; selective separate admission.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1080-1330, ACT 22-30. Honors trends ~150-250 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 15** for priority scholarship + Honors + automatic merit consideration
- **Foundation Scholars** — full ride + summer + leadership + research stipend. Most prestigious. Apply by Dec 1
- **National Merit Finalists** — separate guaranteed full-ride package + laptop stipend (Alabama is famously generous to NMF)
- **University Fellows** — top Honors-within-Honors, separate competitive
- Strong programs: Business (Culverhouse), Engineering (especially Aerospace, Mechanical), Communication + Public Relations, Nursing, Pre-Med, Education
- D-I (SEC) — Crimson Tide football + basketball; football is THE cultural anchor (Bear Bryant + Nick Saban heritage)
- Greek life ~35-40% participation — significant; Greek life central to social culture, especially for in-state and certain OOS demographics
- **Tuscaloosa AL setting** — college town (100K) where UA dominates. 1 hour from Birmingham, 3 hours from Atlanta + Nashville
- Strong Korean + Asian + International student communities — driven by merit scholarships
- Cost: in-state low, out-of-state moderate; National Merit scholarships make it effectively free for top students

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- **The merit scholarship engine is THE differentiator** — chart-based automatic scholarships for high-stats students (verify current chart at admissions site)
- Alabama residents get strong in-state pricing
- Out-of-state pricing competitive with merit aid; many OOS attend due to scholarship + football experience
