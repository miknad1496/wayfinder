# Bowdoin College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Bowdoin admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,800 undergrad enrollment, Brunswick ME
- Admit rate: ~7-9% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional permanently (Bowdoin was the FIRST school to go test-optional, in 1969)
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Bowdoin supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 5
- RD deadline: Jan 5

## What admissions actually weights
Bowdoin's identity: small + intellectual + outdoorsy + collaborative + tight-knit. The "Bowdoin Hello" tradition (everyone says hi on the campus paths) reflects the cultural norm.

Test policy: Bowdoin was the FIRST elite school to go test-optional (1969). Current policy: permanently test-optional. Submitting strong scores (1500+) helps; not submitting doesn't hurt.

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1430-1530, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Bowdoin supplements include "Why Bowdoin?" — focused on fit and community. The "common good" essay (~250 words) is a Bowdoin signature — they want to see civic engagement orientation.

## Post-SFFA stance
Bowdoin's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options. Bowdoin has historically had strong Pell-eligible enrollment.

## Demonstrated interest
Bowdoin does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant. Visiting Brunswick (where feasible) is read positively given Maine's relative isolation.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Significant ED admit lift (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked)
- Athletic recruiting ~25% of admits (NESCAC, strong in skiing, hockey, sailing)
- No Greek life (eliminated 1997)
- The Pine Tree State location is real — coastal Maine is beautiful but isolated
- Bowdoin Outing Club is a major student org
- Bowdoin's food (consistently ranked top-3 college dining) is a real campus discussion topic
- Distinctive: students apply "polar bear cub" identity (mascot) genuinely

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens, permanent residents, and international students (Bowdoin is need-blind for international, distinguishing it from many LACs)
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
