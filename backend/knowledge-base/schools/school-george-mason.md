# George Mason University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current GMU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~26,000 undergrad enrollment, Fairfax VA (DC metro)
- Admit rate: ~88-91% overall
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or GMU institutional application; rolling
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 1 for scholarship + Honors consideration
- RD deadline: Mar 1

## What admissions actually weights
GMU's identity: largest Virginia public university by enrollment + DC-suburb location + Schar School of Policy + Government + Antonin Scalia Law School (graduate) + Costello College of Business + Honors College + diverse + commuter-heavy + tech corridor (Mason adjacent to Northern Virginia tech ecosystem). Strong applicants signal Honors + scholarship targeting + DC career pipeline interest.

**Schar School of Policy and Government** — top-30 nationally for public policy + government; undergrad B.A. in Government and International Politics is a feeder to DC government + think tanks + nonprofits + Capitol Hill.

**Costello College of Business** — direct admit; AACSB-accredited; strong Cybersecurity + Information Systems + Finance.

**Honors College** — selective separate admission; smaller seminars + Honors thesis + dedicated programming + priority registration.

**Volgenau School of Engineering** — direct admit; Computer Science (especially Cybersecurity, AI, Software Engineering) is strong due to Northern Virginia tech ecosystem.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1130-1340, ACT 24-30. Honors trends ~150-200 points higher.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply EA Nov 1** for Honors + Mason Scholar + scholarship consideration
- **Mason Scholar** — top automatic merit for high stats
- **Honors College Scholar** — competitive separate scholarship
- **Patriots Distinction** — for first-gen / underrepresented + scholarship
- **National Merit Finalist** — guaranteed package
- Strong programs: Computer Science (esp. Cybersecurity — top-25), Government and International Politics (Schar — DC pipeline), Business (Costello — esp. Information Systems), Conflict Analysis and Resolution (Carter School — distinctive), Public Health, Education, Communication, Forensic Science (top-5)
- **Carter School for Peace and Conflict Resolution** — distinctive among public R1s; undergrad + grad programs in conflict resolution + peace studies
- D-I (Atlantic 10) — Patriots; not the campus identity
- Greek life ~5-8% participation — small
- **Fairfax VA setting** — DC suburb (~25 min Metro to downtown DC). Strong internship pipeline at federal agencies + think tanks + DC nonprofits + Northern VA tech firms
- Strong international + Asian + South Asian + African + Latino student communities — among most diverse public R1s in the country
- Cost: in-state moderate, out-of-state competitive

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially
- Substantial merit: Mason Scholar, Honors College Scholar, Patriots Distinction, NMF package, Volgenau Engineering Scholarships
- Virginia residents get strong in-state pricing + Virginia Guaranteed Assistance Program (VGAP)
