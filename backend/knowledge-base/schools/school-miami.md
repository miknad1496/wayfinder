# University of Miami — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Miami admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 research university, ~12,500 undergrad enrollment, Coral Gables FL
- Admit rate: ~19-23%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Miami supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 1 + Jan 1
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
University of Miami's identity: Miami location + Miami Herbert Business School + Frost School of Music + Rosenstiel School of Marine + Atmospheric + Earth Science + Pre-Med pipeline (Miller School of Medicine adjacency) + Hurricanes football + diverse Latino + International student community. Strong applicants signal Latin America / international fit + specific Business + Marine Science + Music interest.

**Miami Herbert Business School** — top-50 nationally; AACSB-accredited; strong Latin American business focus + International Finance + Real Estate + Marketing. Direct admit by major.

**Frost School of Music** — top-tier music school with audition-required programs; Music Business + Performance + Music Theory + Music Engineering distinctive.

**Rosenstiel School of Marine, Atmospheric and Earth Science** — top-5 marine science nationally; field-research access (Florida Keys + Caribbean); undergrad B.S. in Marine Science.

**Pre-Med via Miller School of Medicine** — Miami undergrads have research access; med-school placement is meaningful.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1340-1470, ACT 30-33.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 1** for clear top choice — ED admit lift ~1.5-1.7x
- **Singer Scholar** — full ride + summer research stipend
- **George E. Merrick Scholar** — second-tier merit
- **Stamps Scholar** — separate national scholarship
- **Foote Fellow** — competitive Honors thesis + research stipend
- Strong programs: Business (Herbert — esp. International), Marine Science (Rosenstiel), Music (Frost — esp. Music Business + Music Engineering), Communication, Pre-Med, Architecture, Latin American Studies, Education
- D-I (ACC) — Hurricanes football + basketball; baseball strong; football has dynastic heritage (multiple national championships in 80s-90s)
- Greek life ~14-16% participation
- **Coral Gables setting** — beautiful Miami suburb, palm-lined campus. Near downtown Miami. Beach 15 min, Wynwood 20 min
- Strong Latino + Cuban-American + International student communities (Miami is the most international US college campus by % international students)
- Hurricane season (June-Nov) — real (school named for the storms)
- Cost significant — sticker $85K+/year — meritsub-stantially offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~85-90% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Singer Scholar (full ride), Merrick, Stamps, Foote Fellow
- Florida residents: state-funded Bright Futures Scholarship applies + private aid
