# Cooper Union — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Private specialty college (Engineering + Architecture + Art), ~900 undergrad, New York NY (East Village, downtown Manhattan)
- Admit rate: ~13% (Engineering ~16%, Architecture ~18%, Art ~10% — varies by school)
- Test policy: Test-optional; portfolio + Hometest required for Architecture + Art
- Application: Common App + Cooper Union supplements + portfolio (Architecture + Art) + Hometest (Architecture + Art) + early DRP for Architecture
- Early round: ED (Dec 1) for Engineering only — Architecture + Art have ED + RD with portfolio milestones
- Deadline: Dec 1 ED Engineering / Jan 5 Engineering RD / Architecture + Art have multi-stage portfolio process

## What admissions actually weights
Cooper Union is institutionally peculiar in five ways no other U.S. college matches: (1) **Founded 1859 with the explicit mission "education as free as air and water"** — Peter Cooper's vision was full-tuition merit-based education. From 1902-2014, Cooper Union was tuition-FREE for all admitted students. (2) **Returned to partial tuition in 2014** but with a strong commitment to financial aid: most students still receive 50%+ scholarships covering tuition, and the institutional goal is to return to full tuition-free status. The 50% Tuition Scholarship is automatic for all admitted students. (3) **Three schools, distinct admit processes**: Albert Nerken School of Engineering, Irwin S. Chanin School of Architecture, School of Art. Each admits separately with different criteria. The schools share humanities + general-ed but admit by school. (4) **Hometest for Architecture + Art**: Cooper Union's signature pre-admit assignment — applicants receive a creative prompt (Architecture: design problem; Art: artistic problem) to complete at home over several days. The Hometest is the dominant admit factor for Architecture + Art (more than essays, GPA, or scores). (5) **NYC East Village location**: Cooper Square at 3rd Avenue and East 7th — central downtown Manhattan, integrated with the NYC art + architecture + tech ecosystems.

Strong programs: Engineering (Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Chemical — small but rigorous, ABET-accredited), Architecture (Bachelor of Architecture, 5-year professional degree — top-15 nationally), Art (Bachelor of Fine Arts — top-10 nationally, particularly strong in studio practice + interdisciplinary art).

The Cooper Union "type" — intensely creative, intellectually serious, willing to do the Hometest — is a real cultural fit signal.

## Strategic notes
- 50% Tuition Scholarship is automatic for ALL admitted students — guaranteed half-tuition
- Architecture + Art admit is HEAVILY portfolio + Hometest based, not essay/transcript
- Engineering admit is more traditional (essays + scores + transcript)
- Greek life: NONE
- Notable alumni: Lee Krasner, Daniel Libeskind, Milton Glaser (designed "I ❤️ NY"), Russell Banks
- Distinctive features: Foundation Building (1859), Great Hall (where Lincoln gave his "Right Makes Might" speech in 1860), 41 Cooper Square (recent academic building)
- Located on the east side of the Bowery — surrounded by NYU + The New School
- Strong professional integration: Cooper Union has a Continuing Education program + summer programs + adult-learning ecosystem

## Aid + scholarship
- 50% Tuition Scholarship: AUTOMATIC for all admits (no need-based component)
- Additional need-based aid: Cooper Union meets up to 100% of demonstrated need above the 50% guarantee
- No-loan policy for low-income aid recipients
- Strong endowment relative to size

## Recent essay shifts
Cooper Union's "Why Cooper Union" supplement varies by school. Engineering: focus on small-program rigor + NYC tech ecosystem. Architecture: portfolio is the entire game — essays support but don't dominate. Art: portfolio is the entire game — essays signal artistic vision. Architecture + Art applicants who don't take the Hometest seriously fail dramatically; the Hometest is the actual admit-defining moment.
