# University of Colorado Boulder — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current CU Boulder admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Public R1 university, ~31,000 undergrad enrollment, Boulder CO
- Admit rate: ~78-82% overall, but **Engineering direct admit ~60-65%** and **Leeds Business direct admit ~50%**
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or CU Boulder Application
- Early action: Non-binding EA priority deadline Nov 15 for best scholarship + Honors + direct admit
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
CU Boulder's identity: Pac-12 (now Big 12 as of 2024) flagship + outdoor culture + strong Aerospace Engineering (NASA + Boeing co-op pipeline) + Leeds School of Business + Atlas Institute (creative tech) + relaxed-but-serious vibe. Strong applicants signal direct-admit targeting (Engineering, Leeds Business, Atlas, Architecture) since those are competitive.

**Direct admit to College of Engineering & Applied Science** — engineering applicants should apply ED or EA for best chance. Aerospace + Computer Science + Mechanical are the most competitive within engineering. Non-direct = transfer in via Engineering Pre-Admission program (achievable but not guaranteed).

**Direct admit to Leeds School of Business** — competitive for business applicants. Non-direct = Pre-Business through College of Arts & Sciences then internal transfer.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1230-1390, ACT 26-32. Engineering trends ~50-100 points higher.

## Recent essay shifts
CU Boulder supplements include "Why CU Boulder" — articulate why Boulder specifically (vs other Mountain West publics). The supplement matters for direct admit decisions.

## Post-SFFA stance
CU Boulder maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued attention to Colorado residents + first-gen students.

## Demonstrated interest
CU Boulder formally tracks demonstrated interest — campus visit, virtual sessions, supplements that show real understanding of the school all count.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply BY Nov 15** EA for priority scholarship + direct admit consideration
- **Norlin Scholars** — full ride + summer experiences + leadership development. Application opens Nov 1 — must apply by Dec 1
- **Boettcher Foundation Scholarship** (CO residents only) — full ride
- **Honors Program** — automatic invitation to high stats; smaller seminars + Honors thesis
- **Atlas Institute** — distinctive interdisciplinary creative-tech program; selective; project-based; students often dual-major in CS + Art
- Strong programs: Aerospace Engineering (NASA partnership — top-5 globally), Computer Science (CU Boulder PROGRAMS at LASP for actual NASA missions), Environmental Sciences (also top-5), Geology, Astrophysics, Atmospheric & Oceanic Sciences (NCAR adjacent), Physics, Music (College of Music), Architecture
- The **CU + Boulder + boulder-the-rock** intersection — outdoor lifestyle is a real cultural anchor. Climbing, skiing, cycling
- Big 12 athletics (transitioned from Pac-12) — Buffaloes football + basketball
- Greek life ~10-12% participation
- "Pearl Street" pedestrian mall is the downtown anchor; campus integrated with Boulder city
- 30 min from Denver, 90 min from skiing
- High altitude (5,400 ft) — real for endurance athletes
- Strong international student community

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets demonstrated need only partially (significant gap common, especially OOS)
- Substantial merit: Norlin Scholarship (full ride + summer experiences), Esteemed Scholar (high stats automatic), Provost Scholarship, Engineering Scholarships, College of Music Scholarships
- Colorado residents get strong in-state pricing + Colorado Promise (free tuition for qualifying low-income CO residents) + College Opportunity Fund
