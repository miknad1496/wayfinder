# U.S. Air Force Academy (USAFA) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

## Snapshot
- Type: Federal service academy (Department of the Air Force, includes Space Force commissions), ~4,200 undergrad ("cadets"), Colorado Springs CO (foothills of Rocky Mountains)
- Admit rate: ~12%
- Test policy: REQUIRED
- Application: USAFA-specific online application (NOT Common App)
- Early round: NONE — single application cycle
- Deadline: Dec 31

## What admissions actually weights
USAFA is institutionally distinct from West Point + USNA in three ways: (1) **Air Force + Space Force commissioning** — USAFA graduates commission as 2nd Lieutenants in the U.S. Air Force OR the U.S. Space Force (Space Force was created 2019, opening a new commissioning track). 5-year active duty obligation (10 years if pilot, due to ~$10M training investment). (2) **Aviation focus**: USAFA is uniquely aviation-oriented — cadets fly gliders + powered aircraft + complete soaring/parachuting/airmanship programs as part of curriculum. The "wings" tradition is institutional core. (3) **Colorado Springs location** — the campus is at 7,000+ feet elevation in the Rocky Mountain foothills. The Cadet Chapel (architecturally iconic, 17 spires representing 17 modes of religious practice) is a national monument. Climate is dramatically different from East Coast academies — sun, altitude, dry air, snow.

Otherwise the structure mirrors USMA + USNA:
- Congressional nomination required (same process)
- 5-year service obligation post-graduation
- Free tuition + room + board + monthly stipend (~$1,200/month)
- Honor Code: "We will not lie, steal, or cheat, nor tolerate among us anyone who does"
- CFA + DODMERB medical
- Basic Cadet Training (6-week boot camp before academic year)

Strong programs: Aerospace Engineering (signature — top USAFA major and one of the strongest aerospace programs in the U.S.), Astronautical Engineering (designed for space-track cadets), Computer Science (cyber operations focus), Mathematics, Physics, Operations Research, Foreign Languages, Behavioral Sciences (uniquely strong leadership-psychology program).

Test scores: REQUIRED. Middle-50 SAT 1290-1450, ACT 28-32.

## Strategic notes
- Same prep timeline + Congressional nomination as USMA/USNA
- USAFA pilot track requires excellent vision (corrected vision is acceptable post-2010s; PRK/LASIK accepted with waiting period)
- Space Force track is new (since 2020) — about 25% of recent USAFA classes have commissioned into Space Force
- Athletics: D1 in Mountain West Conference; football, hockey, basketball
- Greek life: NONE
- Distinctive features: Cadet Chapel (17 spires, national monument), Falcon Stadium, the Terrazzo (central plaza), the Honor Code Display (large lit sign on campus)
- 7,000+ ft altitude affects athletic + lifestyle adjustment

## Aid + scholarship
- TUITION-FREE
- Monthly stipend
- All books, room, board, uniforms, health care covered
- Pilot track training valued at ~$10M (paid via service obligation)

## Recent essay shifts
USAFA admissions essays focus on "Why Air Force?" or "Why Space Force?" — applicants must articulate genuine commitment to aerospace + space domains. Applicants with prior pilot experience (general aviation, glider, drone), Civil Air Patrol leadership, or strong STEM credentials in aerospace + cyber score better. Generic "I want to fly" essays are insufficient — applicants must articulate what missions or capabilities they want to develop.
