# Northeastern University — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Northeastern admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1, ~16,000 undergrad enrollment (main Boston campus + global campuses), Boston MA
- Admit rate: ~6-7% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Northeastern-specific supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 1, ED2 Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Northeastern's identity: experiential learning + co-op program + global campuses + entrepreneurial. The CO-OP PROGRAM is THE central feature: students do 1-3 paid co-op rotations (6 months each) during college, dramatically reshaping the academic timeline.

Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the co-op model + specific career pipeline interests. Generic Boston-loves don't land.

Multiple campuses (NUin program — first year in London/Greece/Paris/Australia for some students).

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1450-1530, ACT 33-35.

## Recent essay shifts
Northeastern supplements ask about co-op interests + career goals.

## Post-SFFA stance
Northeastern's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Northeastern DOES track demonstrated interest aggressively. ED commitment, visits, info sessions all factor in. Yield protection is real and significant.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. ED bump is significant (~1.7-2x for unhooked)
- Yield protection real and aggressive at Northeastern
- The co-op program: 4 years of school + 1-2 years of paid co-op = 5-6 year graduation timeline. Real lifestyle consideration
- Co-op GPA cuts: students need to maintain academic standing to remain co-op eligible. Pre-med + pre-law pipelines benefit hugely from clinical/legal co-ops
- Athletic recruiting modest (Hockey East, basketball, baseball)
- Greek life ~5% (small)
- Boston location: vibrant urban setting, on the T (orange line)
- Khoury College of Computer Sciences is highly regarded
- DMSB (D'Amore-McKim School of Business) competitive

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need (newer policy)
- Limited loans for upper-income families
- Generous merit aid: University Scholars (full tuition + research stipend)
