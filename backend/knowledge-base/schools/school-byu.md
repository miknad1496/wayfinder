# Brigham Young University (BYU) — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current BYU admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private R1 research university owned by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS), ~33,000 undergrad enrollment, Provo UT
- Admit rate: ~64-69% overall
- Test policy: Standardized testing required for non-LDS applicants; LDS students may apply test-optional
- Application: BYU institutional application (Common App NOT accepted)
- RD deadline: Dec 1

## What admissions actually weights
BYU's identity: largest LDS-affiliated university + tuition is dramatically subsidized by the LDS Church for members + Honor Code is enforced (no premarital sex, no alcohol/coffee/tea, modest dress, weekly church attendance, regular interviews) + strong ROI metrics + Cougars athletics + Marriott School of Business + computer science. Strong applicants signal genuine LDS-faith fit (or non-LDS willing to commit to the Honor Code + religion classes).

**Honor Code** — IS the cultural and disciplinary anchor. ALL students sign it; LDS endorsements required from local clergy for LDS students; non-LDS students need clergy endorsement from their own faith tradition.

**Tuition is dramatically lower for LDS members** — ~$3K/semester for LDS members, ~$6K for non-LDS. **One of the lowest tuitions of any private R1 nationally.** Non-LDS pricing still significantly below most peer privates.

**Marriott School of Business** — top-50 nationally; AACSB-accredited; strong placement (LDS network + corporate hiring of BYU grads is significant).

**Computer Science** — top-25; CS Principles + AI Robotics strong programs.

**Mandatory religion classes** — All students (LDS and non-LDS) take 14 credit hours of religious courses (Doctrine & Covenants, Old Testament, New Testament, Book of Mormon).

Test scores: admitted middle-50 SAT 1240-1410, ACT 27-32.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **For LDS students** — straightforward admission process; need clergy endorsement
- **For non-LDS students** — need clergy endorsement from your own faith + must commit to Honor Code (no exceptions for non-LDS)
- **Mission deferment** — LDS students often defer 18-24 months for missions; standard practice
- **Two-year track** — common for LDS students to complete undergrad in 2.5-3 years post-mission due to AP credit + accelerated load
- Strong programs: Business (Marriott), Computer Science, Engineering (Mechanical, Civil, Chemical), Linguistics (top language program due to mission diaspora), Animation (top-3 globally — Pixar pipeline is real), Communications, Public Health, Family Studies, Religious Studies
- **Animation program** — top-3 in the world; Disney + Pixar pipeline is unmatched. Selective separate admission within BYU
- D-I (Big 12 — transitioned from independent/MWC 2023) — Cougars football + basketball
- Greek life — none (no fraternities/sororities at BYU; faith communities replace this)
- **Provo UT setting** — smaller city near SLC, mountain access, family-oriented
- ~99% LDS students; ~1% non-LDS — significantly homogeneous environment
- Cost: tuition extraordinarily low for the academic quality

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Modest need-based aid (most aid is merit + church-subsidized tuition)
- LDS members: tuition heavily subsidized by LDS Church
- Non-LDS members: still pay below-private-rate tuition (~$13K/year)
- BYU Scholarships + Departmental Scholarships available
