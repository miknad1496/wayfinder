# Stevens Institute of Technology — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Stevens admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private STEM-focused research university, ~4,300 undergrad enrollment, Hoboken NJ (across the Hudson from NYC)
- Admit rate: ~38-44%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Stevens supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 15
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Nov 15
- RD deadline: Feb 1

## What admissions actually weights
Stevens's identity: small private STEM + Manhattan-skyline campus (literally — Hoboken faces NYC across the Hudson) + finance + tech + co-op tradition + accelerated graduate-degree pathways. Strong applicants signal STEM seriousness AND understand the proximity-to-Wall-Street advantage that drives Stevens's distinctive financial-engineering pipeline.

**Quantitative Finance (B.S. + M.S.)** — Stevens is one of the few undergrad-level programs with deep ties to Wall Street financial-engineering quant roles. Combine with Computer Science or Engineering for "fintech" pipeline.

**Co-op Program** — similar to Drexel/Northeastern but smaller scale. ~30% of students do at least one full co-op cycle. Strong placements at NYC investment banks, finance, defense contractors, tech firms.

**Pinnacle Scholars + Stevens Scholars** — top merit scholarship programs with Honors-style cohort.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1410-1530, ACT 32-34. Stevens trends higher than peer regional STEM schools.

## Recent essay shifts
Stevens supplements include "Why Stevens" + a STEM-specific prompt. Articulate why this specific Hoboken location + small-school feel + NYC proximity matters. Generic answers fail.

## Post-SFFA stance
Stevens maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + underrepresented STEM students.

## Demonstrated interest
Stevens tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit (Hoboken is accessible from NYC by PATH train), virtual sessions matter.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Stevens if it's a clear top choice — ED admit lift is meaningful (~1.4-1.6x for unhooked applicants)
- **Pinnacle Scholars** — top merit + Honors cohort. Apply by Dec 1 — separate competitive review
- **Stevens Scholars** — second-tier merit + research opportunities
- **Edwin A. Stevens Scholars** — first-gen / underrepresented STEM students with full-tuition + research mentorship
- Strong programs: Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Quantitative Finance (rare undergrad offering), Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering (especially structural — Hoboken's industrial heritage), Cybersecurity, Maritime Systems, Music + Technology
- **5-year accelerated B.S./M.S. programs** — distinctive; many engineering/CS students complete combined undergrad + master's in 5 years total
- **Hoboken** — small dense urban setting; PATH train to NYC = 8 minutes to World Trade Center / lower Manhattan = full Wall Street + tech access
- D-III athletics (Empire 8) — Ducks; not the campus identity
- Greek life ~25-30% participation — significant
- Strong Korean + Asian + South Asian + Eastern European student communities
- Cost is significant — sticker $85K+/year — but merit aid + co-op earnings + 5-year accelerated savings offset
- Hoboken nightlife + restaurants + waterfront walks are real cultural anchors

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~80-85% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: Pinnacle Scholars (full-tuition + summer + research), Stevens Scholars, Engineering Scholars, Athletic Scholarships, ROTC
- New Jersey residents get marginal in-state-equivalent pricing through some scholarship structures (limited)
