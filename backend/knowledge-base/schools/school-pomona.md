# Pomona College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Pomona admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,700 undergrad enrollment, Claremont CA (Claremont Colleges Consortium)
- Admit rate: ~7-9% (Class of 2027 cycle)
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App or Coalition + Pomona supplements
- Early round: Early Decision 1 + Early Decision 2 — both BINDING. ED1 Nov 15, ED2 Jan 8
- RD deadline: Jan 8

## What admissions actually weights
Pomona's identity: intellectual + collaborative + happy + Southern California weather + Claremont Colleges Consortium access. Strong applicants articulate genuine engagement with the consortium structure (5 colleges sharing a campus, students can take classes at all 5).

The 5 Claremont Colleges (5C):
- Pomona (largest, broadest)
- Claremont McKenna (CMC) — economics/government focus
- Harvey Mudd — STEM-focused
- Scripps — women's college
- Pitzer — interdisciplinary, environmental focus

Test scores (when submitted): median admit middle-50 SAT 1430-1530, ACT 32-34.

## Recent essay shifts
Pomona supplements include "Why Pomona?" + "Choose your own personal essay topic" — flexibility rewards creativity.

## Post-SFFA stance
Pomona's post-SFFA approach included expanded adversity-essay options.

## Demonstrated interest
Pomona does NOT formally track demonstrated interest, but ED commitment is significant.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- ED1 + ED2 BOTH binding. Significant ED admit lift (~1.5-1.8x for unhooked)
- Claremont Colleges Consortium: Pomona students can take classes at any of the 5 Cs without extra cost. Significantly expands course availability
- Athletic recruiting ~20-25% of admits (SCIAC conference)
- Sagehens athletics
- No Greek life (Pomona doesn't have it; some 5C colleges have very small Greek presence)
- The consortium structure can feel like 4,000-undergrad community when you include all 5 Cs, despite Pomona itself being 1,700
- Beautiful weather, mountain views, 35 min from LA

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-blind for U.S. citizens and permanent residents
- Need-aware for international applicants
- Meets 100% of demonstrated need without loans for ALL admitted students
- No merit-only scholarships at undergrad
- QuestBridge partner
