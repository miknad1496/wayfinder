# Sarah Lawrence College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Sarah Lawrence admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~1,400 undergrad enrollment, Bronxville NY (~30 min from Manhattan)
- Admit rate: ~50-56%
- Test policy: **Test-blind** (does not consider scores even if submitted) — among the longest-running test-blind policies (since 2003)
- Application: Common App + Sarah Lawrence supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 1
- RD deadline: Jan 1

## What admissions actually weights
Sarah Lawrence's identity: **non-traditional academic structure** is THE differentiator. No traditional grades (narrative evaluations only — though letter grades available on request for grad school). Don tutorials (one-on-one weekly meetings with faculty for upper-level seminars). Conference work (independent project per course, often a 25-50 page paper or creative project). Strong writing-intensive curriculum + theater + dance + creative writing pipeline. Strong applicants signal genuine engagement with the don tutorial model — generic "I want small classes" answers fail.

**Don tutorials**: every upper-level seminar has weekly one-on-one meetings with the professor (45 min each). The student leads the conversation. Total faculty access is unprecedented for an LAC. This is THE academic anchor.

**No grades (officially)**: faculty write narrative evaluations instead of letter grades. Letter grades are calculated and available on request for grad school applications. Many students never see a grade until applying to law/med school.

**Conference work**: every course has an independent project component — students choose a topic + work with the professor + produce a final project. Stack on top of standard coursework.

Test-blind (since 2003). The test-blind policy is institutional + intentional. Don't waste prep cycles.

## Recent essay shifts
Sarah Lawrence supplements emphasize self-direction, intellectual curiosity, and your relationship with previous teachers. Specific narrative about how you've worked one-on-one with mentors lands; generic answers fail.

## Post-SFFA stance
Sarah Lawrence maintained holistic review post-SFFA. The test-blind + narrative-evaluation culture creates space for non-traditional academic profiles.

## Demonstrated interest
Sarah Lawrence tracks demonstrated interest. Visit + virtual sessions matter meaningfully — especially for showing you understand the don tutorial model.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Sarah Lawrence if clear top choice — ED admit lift is meaningful
- **Westchester Honor Award** — for high-achieving NY-residents
- **Sarah Lawrence Scholar Award** — top merit scholarship + summer experience
- **Greenough Tutorial Scholar** — celebrates the don tutorial model + leadership
- **Posse Foundation partnership** — first-gen + low-income leadership scholarships
- Strong programs: Writing (one of the strongest undergrad creative writing programs in the U.S. — Joan Didion is an alum), Theater (BFA-equivalent training), Dance, Visual Arts, Music, Psychology, History, Literature, Languages
- **Theater + Dance + Writing pipeline** — Sarah Lawrence is THE LAC choice for serious creative writers + theater students who don't want a conservatory
- **Bronxville setting** — small upscale suburb of NYC. 30 minutes by train (Metro-North) to Grand Central. Real urban access without urban dorm life
- D-III athletics (HVMAC) — Gryphons; not the campus identity
- Greek life — none
- **Curtin lectures** + NY Times Talks held on campus regularly — significant cultural programming
- Strong international + arts-focused + LGBTQ+ student communities
- "Sarah Lawrence" tradition of independent thought is real — students often describe it as the most intellectually rigorous undergrad experience available outside grad school
- Cost is significant — sticker $90K+/year (one of highest LAC list prices) — but generous merit + need aid offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~92-95% of demonstrated need on average (some packages include loans)
- Substantial merit: Sarah Lawrence Scholar, Westchester Honor, Greenough Tutorial, Performing Arts Scholarships (audition-based for theater/dance)
- QuestBridge partner
