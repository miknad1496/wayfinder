# Dickinson College — Wayfinder Deep Knowledge

> Wayfinder advisory file. Verify against current Dickinson admissions + Common Data Set.

## Snapshot
- Type: Private LAC, ~2,100 undergrad enrollment, Carlisle PA
- Admit rate: ~37-43%
- Test policy: Test-optional through 2026-27
- Application: Common App + Dickinson supplements
- Early decision: Binding ED1 + ED2, deadlines Nov 15 + Jan 15
- Early action: Non-binding EA, deadline Dec 1
- RD deadline: Jan 15

## What admissions actually weights
Dickinson's identity: founded in 1773 (signed by Benjamin Rush, the same year as the Declaration of Independence — distinctive American founding heritage) + strong international study-abroad culture + the **"Useful Knowledge"** mission (applied liberal arts, sustainability focus) + small Pennsylvania college town setting. Strong applicants signal genuine fit with the international + sustainability + applied-LAC orientation.

**Study abroad** — Dickinson has its own programs in Europe + Asia + Latin America (~80% of students study abroad, top-3 LAC for study abroad participation). The school operates its own programs in places like Bologna, Bremen, Toulouse, Yaounde, Querétaro, Beijing — distinctive vs schools that just send students through third-party providers.

**Center for Sustainability Education** — Dickinson is a leader in sustainability-focused liberal arts. Many programs integrate sustainability + ethics into traditional disciplines.

**International Studies** — distinctive strength; many alumni in foreign service + diplomatic corps + NGOs.

Test scores (when submitted): admitted middle-50 SAT 1290-1430, ACT 28-32.

## Recent essay shifts
Dickinson supplements emphasize global engagement + Useful Knowledge mission articulation. Specific examples of cross-cultural engagement + applied-knowledge projects land.

## Post-SFFA stance
Dickinson maintained holistic review post-SFFA. Continued commitment to first-gen + international + diverse students.

## Demonstrated interest
Dickinson tracks demonstrated interest. Campus visit + virtual sessions matter meaningfully.

## Strategic notes for applicants
- **Apply ED1 Nov 15** for Dickinson if clear top choice — ED admit lift ~1.4-1.6x
- **John Dickinson Scholarship** — top merit ($35K+/yr) + leadership programming
- **Robert and Loretta Cole Scholarship** — for first-gen students with merit + need
- **Posse Foundation partnership** — full leadership scholarship
- **Davis United World College Scholars** — for IB + UWC graduates
- Strong programs: International Studies, Environmental Studies, Sustainability, Foreign Languages (one of strongest LAC language programs — 13 languages taught), Political Science, Anthropology, Earth Sciences, Computer Science (newer, growing)
- **13 foreign languages taught** — including Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Russian, Italian, Portuguese, German, French, Spanish, Hebrew, Greek, Latin. Distinctive for a small LAC
- **Carlisle PA setting** — historic small town, 25 min from Harrisburg, 2 hours from DC + Philadelphia, 3 hours from NYC. Real college town integrated with the town
- D-III athletics (Centennial Conference) — Red Devils
- Greek life ~25-30% participation
- **The Quad + Old West** — historic 18th-century buildings, beautiful traditional collegiate aesthetic
- Strong international + diverse + multilingual student communities (~10% international, way above LAC average)
- Cost significant — sticker $80K+/year — meritsub-stantially offsets

## Aid + scholarship policy
- Need-aware admissions
- Meets ~95-98% of demonstrated need on average
- Substantial merit: John Dickinson Scholarship (top tier), Robert and Loretta Cole Scholarship, Davis UWC Scholars, Benjamin Rush Scholarship, Performing Arts Scholarships (audition-based)
- QuestBridge partner
