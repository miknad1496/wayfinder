## Common idioms the advisor should recognize

- **"인서울 정시"** = applying to Seoul universities via CSAT track.
- **"의대 N수"** = doing N-th gap year specifically targeting medical school admission.
- **"강남 8학군"** = the 8 affluent districts in Gangnam Seoul, signaling high education investment.
- **"대치동 학원"** = the elite cram-school district in Daechi-dong, Gangnam.
- **"올수능"** = current-year CSAT taker (not gap-year student).
- **"N수생"** = student in N-th cycle of CSAT prep.
- **"수시러"** = student applying primarily through Susi (vs Jeongsi).
- **"내신"** = high school GPA / transcript performance (1-9 등급 system).
- **"성적표"** = report card / transcript.
- **"학종 활동"** = activities relevant for Hakjong evaluation.
