# AP Precalculus — Wayfinder Study Guide

> Wayfinder advisory file. Surfaces when user asks about AP Precalc prep, FRQ

## Math of a 5
~75% total. MCQ Part A (no calculator) 21/28 + Part B (calculator) 9/12 + FRQs ~80% on each.

## THE differentiator (5-scorer vs 4-scorer)
**SHOW WORK + INTERPRET.** Even when the calculator gives the answer, write the formula symbolically first, then interpret in context.

A 4-scorer plugs into the calculator and writes the answer. A 5-scorer:
1. Writes the formula symbolically
2. Substitutes values
3. Computes (or uses calculator)
4. **Writes a sentence interpreting the answer in the context of the problem**

The interpretation sentence is the difference between 3/4 and 4/4 on most rubrics.

## The 4 FRQ types are FIXED every year (preparable specifically)
1. **FRQ 1: Function Concepts (calculator)** — given a function (often piecewise or table), evaluate, find rates of change, interpret in context
2. **FRQ 2: Modeling Non-Periodic (calculator)** — recognize the function family from data
3. **FRQ 3: Modeling Periodic (NO calculator)** — sinusoidal model, the 5-step recipe
4. **FRQ 4: Symbolic Manipulations (NO calculator)** — algebra-heavy, every step shown

## Function-family recognition (FRQ 2)
Recognize the family from data BEFORE choosing the model:
- **Constant differences** in y for equal x-steps → LINEAR
- **Constant ratios** of consecutive y → EXPONENTIAL
- **Constant 2nd differences** in y → QUADRATIC
- **Logarithmic growth** (decreasing rate, no asymptote) → LOG
- **Periodic oscillation** → SINUSOIDAL

## Sinusoidal 5-step recipe (FRQ 3)
For any sinusoidal model y = A·sin(b(x − h)) + k OR y = A·cos(b(x − h)) + k:
1. **Midline:** k = (max + min) / 2
2. **Amplitude:** A = (max − min) / 2
3. **Frequency:** b = 2π / period
4. **Choose sin or cos** based on starting position (does it start at midline ascending? Use sin. At max? Use cos.)
5. **Phase shift h** — where the chosen function "starts" relative to x = 0

## Critical mode setting
**RADIAN mode for trig.** This is the default for AP. Set it before exam day; verify on test morning.

## Unit circle memorization (non-negotiable)
Every common-angle sin/cos/tan must be instant recall for the no-calculator section. Drill until you have:
- 0, π/6, π/4, π/3, π/2 and their multiples
- sin(π/3) = √3/2, cos(π/3) = 1/2, tan(π/3) = √3 (not just memorized — visualize on the circle)

## What's NOT tested
**Only Units 1-3 are tested on the AP exam.** Unit 4 (parameters/vectors/matrices) is taught in some courses but does NOT appear on the AP exam. Don't over-invest in Unit 4 prep.

## Exam structure
- Section I Part A: 28 MCQ, 80 minutes, NO calculator, 28% of score
- Section I Part B: 12 MCQ, 40 minutes, calculator, 14% of score
- Section II Part A: 2 FRQs, 30 minutes, calculator, 18% of score
- Section II Part B: 2 FRQs, 30 minutes, NO calculator, 22% of score
- Total: 3 hours

## Score distribution (new exam, limited data)
- AP Precalc was first administered 2024. ~50%+ score 4 or 5 in early years (highly self-selected pool).

## Top traps
- Forgetting to interpret the answer in context (3/4 instead of 4/4)
- Working in degree mode instead of radians on the trig FRQ
- Skipping algebra steps on FRQ 4 (each line of work earns points)
- Misidentifying function family in FRQ 2 (linear vs exponential confusion)
