## Math of a 5
~75% total. MCQ ~45/60 correct. FRQ ~80% on each free-response question. The 5 is achievable — about 16% of the test-taking pool earns it (one of the lower 5-rates among AP science exams). This is the "the math of a 5 is achievable, not abstract" framing — show it on day one of prep.

## THE differentiator (5-scorer vs 4-scorer)
**Particulate-level reasoning.** Every "why" answer must cite a principle (Coulomb's law / IMF / Q vs K / Le Châtelier) AND apply it to atomic-level mechanism. A 4-scorer says "the boiling point is higher because of strong bonds." A 5-scorer says "London dispersion forces increase with molecular size because larger electron clouds are more polarizable, so heavier alkanes have higher boiling points."

If a "why" answer doesn't reference particulates (atoms, electrons, IMFs, ions, molecules) AND a named principle, the 5-point ceiling is closed.

## Top traps that cost 4-scorers their 5
1. **Mass-spec answer ≠ tallest peak.** It's the weighted average of all isotopes. The tallest peak is just the most-abundant isotope.
2. **Cu and Cr exceptions** to electron config (4s¹3d⁵ for Cr, 4s¹3d¹⁰ for Cu — half-full and full d-subshell stability).
3. **Cation removal:** electrons leave from highest n FIRST, not by reverse Aufbau. Fe → Fe³⁺ removes 4s² then 3d¹.
4. **Bond energy formula:** ΔH = bonds BROKEN minus bonds FORMED (NOT formed minus broken).
5. **Half-life:** ONLY first-order reactions have constant t½. Zero-order and second-order do not.
6. **ΔH(kJ) vs ΔS(J/K)** — students miss the unit conversion when computing ΔG = ΔH − TΔS.
7. **E°cell DOES NOT scale with stoichiometry.** Doubling the equation does not double the standard cell potential. (ΔG° does scale; E°cell does not.)

## Big themes (the conceptual map)
- Coulombic interactions determine attraction strength
- IMFs determine bulk physical properties (BP, MP, viscosity)
- Particulate reasoning is the secret password
- Q vs K predicts reaction direction (Q > K → reverse, Q < K → forward)
- ΔG° unifies thermo + equilibrium + electrochem: ΔG° = −RT ln K = −nFE°cell
- Bonded vs nonbonded electron pairs control molecular geometry (VSEPR)

## Memorization fuel (commit these to instant recall)
- **7 strong acids:** HCl, HBr, HI, HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄, HClO₃
- **Spontaneity sign table:** ΔH−ΔS combinations and what's spontaneous when
- **Integrated rate law shapes:** zero-order ([A] vs t linear), first-order (ln[A] vs t linear), second-order (1/[A] vs t linear)
- **Henderson-Hasselbalch:** pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA])
- **Thermo trio:** ΔG° = −RT ln K = −nFE°cell

## Exam structure
- Section I: 60 MCQ, 90 minutes, calculator, 50% of score
- Section II: 7 FRQs (3 long + 4 short), 105 minutes, calculator, 50% of score
- Total: 3 hours 15 minutes
