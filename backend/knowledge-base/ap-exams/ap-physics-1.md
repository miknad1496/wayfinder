# AP Physics 1 — Wayfinder Study Guide

> Wayfinder advisory file. Surfaces when user asks about AP Physics 1 prep,
> exam strategy, or FRQ technique.

## Math of a 4
MCQ ~27 of 50 + complete every FRQ (don't skip parts).

## Math of a 5
MCQ ~33 of 50 + commentary depth on every FRQ explanation. Show symbolic algebra BEFORE substituting numbers.

## THE differentiator (5-scorer vs 4-scorer)
**Drawing the right diagram + showing symbolic algebra before plugging in numbers.** A correct numerical answer with no work shown often earns only 1 of 3 points.

A 4-scorer writes the equation, plugs in numbers, gets the answer. A 5-scorer:
1. Draws the FBD (free-body diagram) or motion diagram or energy bar chart
2. Writes the equation symbolically (F_net = ma → T − mg = ma)
3. Solves symbolically for the unknown (T = m(g+a))
4. THEN plugs in numbers
5. Includes a sentence of physical interpretation

That extra structure is what unlocks the rubric points.

## The PSM (Problem-Solving Method)
Use this universal sequence on every FRQ:
- **READ** the problem twice. Identify what's asked.
- **DRAW** — FBD for force problems, motion diagram for kinematics, energy bar chart for energy, before-after for momentum/collisions
- **LIST** knowns + sign convention (define +x direction)
- **PICK YOUR WEAPON** — choose the right physics tool (decision tree below)
- **SOLVE** symbolically first, then numerically
- **CHECK** units, sign, magnitude

## Pick Your Weapon decision tree
Match the question to the right tool:
- "Speed at time t" → kinematics (v = v₀ + at)
- "Speed at position x" → energy conservation (½mv² = mgh + ...)
- "Force at this instant" → Newton's 2nd Law (F_net = ma)
- "Velocity AFTER collision" → momentum conservation (Σp_before = Σp_after)
- "Brief impact / large force / short time" → impulse-momentum (FΔt = Δp)
- "Object moves in circle" → centripetal (a_c = v²/r)
- "Restoring force / oscillation" → SHM (x(t) = A cos(ωt + φ))

## Free Points Routine (first 60 seconds of FRQ section)
On scratch margin, write all kinematic + force + energy + momentum + SHM + fluid equations BEFORE reading question 1. This eliminates the "blanking on a formula" failure mode and lets you focus on choosing weapons.

## Lab linearization tricks (the 5-scorer's secret weapon)
Lab questions ask you to linearize data. Memorize:
- **Free fall:** d vs t² → slope = g/2
- **Pendulum:** T² vs L → slope = 4π²/g
- **Spring oscillation:** T² vs m → slope = 4π²/k
- **Hooke's Law:** F vs x → slope = k

The lab FRQ rewards explicit linearization. Show: which variables on each axis, what slope physically represents, what intercept means.

## 2024-25 redesign note
**AP Physics 1 NOW INCLUDES FLUIDS** (Unit 8 added in 2024-25 redesign). Don't skip fluid statics + fluid dynamics. Continuity equation (A₁v₁ = A₂v₂) and Bernoulli's equation are tested.

## Exam structure
- Section I: 50 MCQ, 80 minutes, calculator + formula sheet, 50% of score
- Section II: 5 FRQs, 100 minutes, calculator + formula sheet, 50% of score
- Total: 3 hours

## Score distribution
- 5: ~10% / 4: ~16% / 3: ~22% / 2: ~30% / 1: ~22% (one of the lower 5-rates)
