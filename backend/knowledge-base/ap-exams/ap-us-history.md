# AP US History (APUSH) — Wayfinder Study Guide

> Wayfinder advisory file. Surfaces when user asks about APUSH prep, DBQ/LEQ
> strategy, or score targets.

## Math of a 4 (target was 4)
~62%. MCQ 35/55 + SAQ 6/9 + DBQ 4/7 + LEQ 4/6.

## Math of a 5
~75%. MCQ 42+/55 + SAQ 7-8/9 + DBQ 6/7 + LEQ 5-6/6.

The 4 is reachable for most prepared students; the 5 requires hitting the DBQ stretch points (6 documents + HIPP for 3 + complexity).

## THE differentiator (5-scorer vs 4-scorer)
**SPECIFICITY wins.** "The reformers worked hard" earns 0 points. "Jane Addams founded Hull House in Chicago in 1889" earns the point. EVERY claim needs a name, a date, an event, a place. This is the universal APUSH lesson — drill it explicitly.

A 4-scorer cites generic categories ("Progressive reformers improved cities"). A 5-scorer cites specific people, events, dates, organizations, and laws. The rubric explicitly rewards specificity.

## Course structure
**9 periods (1491-Present), 7 themes:** NAT (American + National Identity), MIG (Migration + Settlement), GEO (Geography + Environment), WXT (Work + Exchange + Technology), PCE (Politics + Power), ASO (American + Regional Culture), IDE (Ideas + Beliefs + Culture). Every FRQ tags one or more themes.

## DBQ rubric (7 points)
**Floor (4 points):**
- Thesis (1 point) — defensible thesis with line of reasoning
- Context (1 point) — broader historical context BEFORE the time period
- 3 documents used as evidence (1 point)
- Outside evidence (1 point) — one specific piece NOT in the documents

**Stretch (3 more points to reach 7):**
- 6 documents (1 point) — use 6 of the 7 docs
- HIPP for 3 documents (1 point) — analyze Historical situation, Intended audience, Purpose, OR Point of view for at least 3 docs (any 3 of the 7 lenses, you pick easiest)
- Complexity (1 point) — show nuance: counterevidence, multiple causes, change-over-time, comparison

## LEQ rubric (6 points)
Same DNA as DBQ but no documents — need 2 specific examples + reasoning + complexity. Most-missed point: complexity. Easiest path to complexity: acknowledge a counterargument and refute it specifically.

## SAQ "ABC method"
Each Short Answer has 3 parts (A/B/C). Most efficient pattern:
- **Part A:** state your answer directly in 1 sentence
- **Part B:** specific year/event/explain — name the example, year, give the cause
- **Part C:** different example — DON'T repeat your B example, pick a contrasting one

## HIPP for sourcing (DBQ)
Pick easiest 3 of the 7 docs to source. For each, address ONE of:
- **H** istorical situation — what was happening when this was written
- **I** ntended audience — who was meant to receive this
- **P** urpose — why was this created
- **P** oint of view — what bias or perspective shapes this

You only need ONE of HIPP per doc, NOT all 4. Pick the easiest angle for each doc.

## Universal Evidence Bank
7-8 events that work for almost any prompt — memorize these cold:
1. **American Revolution** (causes, ideology, consequences)
2. **Constitutional Convention + Federalist Papers**
3. **Civil War + Reconstruction** (causes, amendments, end of Reconstruction)
4. **Gilded Age Industrialization** (railroads, robber barons, immigration)
5. **Progressive Era** (muckrakers, women's suffrage, trust-busting, settlement houses)
6. **New Deal** (relief/recovery/reform, Wagner Act, Social Security)
7. **WWII** (mobilization, internment, end of isolationism, GI Bill)
8. **Civil Rights Movement** (Brown, MLK, Civil Rights Act, Voting Rights Act)

## Top traps
- **"States rights to do what?"** — Civil War cause = slavery, NOT generic states' rights
- DBQ: only using 3 docs leaves 3 stretch points on the table
- LEQ: forgetting the complexity point (most-missed of the 6)
- SAQ Part B: not giving a specific example, just restating the claim

## Exam structure
- Section I Part A: 55 MCQ, 55 minutes, 40% of score
- Section I Part B: 3 SAQs (4th optional), 40 minutes, 20% of score
- Section II Part A: 1 DBQ, 60 minutes (incl. 15 min reading), 25% of score
- Section II Part B: 1 LEQ (3 prompt choices), 40 minutes, 15% of score

## Score distribution
- 5: ~13% / 4: ~17% / 3: ~22% / 2: ~26% / 1: ~22%
