# AP Spanish Language and Culture — Unit 5: Los Desafíos Mundiales — Wayfinder Teaching Brain

> Surfaces in chat when student mentions topics in this unit.

## Unit at a Glance

- **Theme:** Global Challenges — Economy, Health, Population, Environment, Wellbeing, Tolerance, Conflict

## Big Ideas

1. **Economic inequality** within and between Spanish-speaking countries.
2. **Health challenges:** access disparities, COVID-19 impact, chronic disease.
3. **Environmental challenges:** deforestation, climate change, water scarcity.
4. **Migration** from Latin America.
5. **Conflict and human rights:** Venezuela crisis, Mexican drug war, indigenous rights.

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Vocabulary:**
  - **La economía:** pobreza, desigualdad, desempleo, riqueza, brecha (gap), clase social.
  - **Los desafíos:** desafío, problema, crisis, conflicto.
  - **La inmigración:** inmigrante, refugiado, frontera, asilo, deportación.
  - **Derechos humanos:** derechos humanos, libertad, justicia, discriminación, igualdad.
  - **El medio ambiente (review):** contaminación, calentamiento global, deforestación.
- **Migration patterns:**
  - **Mexico → US:** historic largest flow.
  - **Central American (Honduras, Guatemala, El Salvador) → US:** violence, poverty drivers.
  - **Venezuela → Colombia/Peru/Brazil:** ~7 million Venezuelans displaced (since 2014).
  - **Cuba → US:** historic exile flows.
- **Drug trafficking:**
  - **Mexican cartels** drive violence.
  - **Colombian cocaine.**
  - **Drug war's human cost.**
- **Indigenous rights:**
  - **Movements** in Bolivia, Mexico (Zapatistas), Guatemala, Ecuador, Peru.
  - **Constitutional recognition** of indigenous nations (Bolivia 2009).
- **Gender issues:**
  - **Femicide (femicidio):** murder of women because they are women.
  - **Movement Ni Una Menos** (started Argentina 2015).
  - **Gender pay gap, women's representation.**

### Adds for [4]

- **Venezuelan crisis (2014+):**
  - **Economic collapse,** hyperinflation, food/medicine shortages.
  - **Political:** Maduro vs opposition.
  - **~7 million emigrated** (one of largest displacements globally).
- **Central American migration:**
  - **Northern Triangle (Honduras, Guatemala, El Salvador):** gang violence, poverty.
  - **Migrant caravans** highly visible.
- **Mexican drug war (2006+):**
  - **President Calderón declared war.**
  - **~250,000+ deaths.**
  - **Cartels powerful.**

### Adds for [5]

- **Why challenges complex:**
  - **No single cause** (poverty, governance, history, geopolitics).
  - **No simple solution.**
- **Spanish-speaking diaspora** integral to addressing.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Vocabulary

Translate:
(a) refugee.
(b) inequality.
(c) human rights.

- (a) **refugiado/a.**
- (b) **desigualdad.**
- (c) **derechos humanos.**

### Example 2 [4] — Migration

Compare migration drivers from Mexico vs Central America to US.
- **Mexico (historic):** economic opportunity, family reunification.
- **Central America (recent):** gang violence, extortion, government failure, poverty (Northern Triangle).
- **Both:** US labor demand, established communities pull.

### Example 3 [5] — Femicide

What is femicide and why is it a major issue in Latin America?
- **Femicide (femicidio):** killing of women because they are women.
- **High rates** in Mexico, Argentina, Central America, others.
- **Movement Ni Una Menos** (started Argentina 2015) protests.
- **Demand:** legal recognition (femicide as distinct crime), protection, prevention.
- **Reflects** broader gender violence and impunity.

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Drug war ≠ war on drugs (US version).** Mexican drug war refers to specific 2006+ Mexican government conflict.
2. **Venezuelan crisis ongoing,** hugely consequential.
3. **Hispanic ≠ all Spanish-speaking** — Brazil is Portuguese-speaking.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "La crisis venezolana (2014+) ha desplazado a más de 7 millones de venezolanos hacia países vecinos como Colombia y Perú, una de las migraciones más grandes del mundo, causada por colapso económico, hiperinflación y conflicto político."
2. "El femicidio — el asesinato de mujeres por ser mujeres — afecta gravemente a varias naciones hispanohablantes; el movimiento Ni Una Menos, surgido en Argentina en 2015, exige reconocimiento legal y protección."
3. "La migración centroamericana hacia EE.UU. desde el Triángulo Norte (Honduras, Guatemala, El Salvador) responde a la violencia de pandillas, la extorsión y la pobreza, agravadas por debilidad institucional."

## If You Do Nothing Else for This Theme

*Master Venezuelan crisis. Master Central American migration drivers. Master femicide and Ni Una Menos. Master indigenous rights movements. Master Mexican drug war.*
