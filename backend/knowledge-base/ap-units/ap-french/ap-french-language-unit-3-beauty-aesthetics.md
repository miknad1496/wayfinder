# AP French Language and Culture — Unit 3: La Beauté et l'Esthétique — Wayfinder Teaching Brain

> Surfaces in chat when student mentions topics in this unit.

## Unit at a Glance

- **Theme:** Beauty and Aesthetics — Beauty Concepts, Architecture, Visual/Performing Arts, Fashion, Literature

## Big Ideas

1. **France** = global cultural reference (fashion, cuisine, art).
2. **Visual arts:** Impressionism (Monet, Renoir), Post-Impressionism (Cézanne, Gauguin), modernism.
3. **Architecture:** Gothic cathedrals, Versailles, Eiffel Tower, modern (Pompidou).
4. **Literature:** Hugo, Balzac, Camus, Sartre, Beauvoir, Proust.
5. **Fashion** centered in Paris.

### Adds for [4]

- **Impressionism (~1870s+):** painted light, en plein air, modern Parisian life.
- **Existentialism (Sartre, de Beauvoir):** existence precedes essence; freedom and responsibility.
- **Nouvelle Vague (1960s):** experimental cinema; jump cuts; handheld cameras.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why French art exported globally:** colonial spread + French cultural prestige + Parisian art world.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Identify

For each:
(a) Monet.
(b) Hugo.
(c) Eiffel Tower.

- (a) **Impressionist painter; *Impression, Sunrise*, Water Lilies.**
- (b) **Writer; *Les Misérables*, *Notre-Dame de Paris*.**
- (c) **Built 1889; for World's Fair; symbol of Paris.**

### Example 2 [4] — Impressionism

What characterized Impressionism?
- **Painted light** rather than fixed objects.
- **En plein air** (outdoors).
- **Quick brushwork; visible strokes.**
- **Modern Parisian subjects** (cafés, races, leisure).
- **Reaction against academic style.**

### Example 3 [5] — Existentialism

What is Sartre's existentialism in essence?
- **"Existence precedes essence."**
- **Humans aren't born with predetermined nature;** create themselves through choices.
- **Radical freedom** entails radical responsibility.
- **Anguish of choice.**
- **De Beauvoir applied** to gender (*Le Deuxième Sexe*): "On ne naît pas femme, on le devient."

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Van Gogh = Dutch** but active in France.
2. **Notre-Dame is Gothic** (medieval, not Baroque).
3. **Eiffel Tower** initially controversial; now icon.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "L'impressionnisme français (Monet, Renoir, Degas) a révolutionné la peinture en privilégiant la lumière et l'instant fugitif sur la précision académique, peint en plein air avec des coups de pinceau rapides et visibles."
2. "L'existentialisme de Sartre — 'l'existence précède l'essence' — soutient que les humains se définissent par leurs choix dans une liberté radicale ; Simone de Beauvoir a appliqué cette pensée au genre dans *Le Deuxième Sexe* (1949) : 'On ne naît pas femme, on le devient.'"
3. "Paris a longtemps été la capitale culturelle mondiale : peinture, mode, gastronomie, littérature et philosophie ont rayonné depuis la Seine, attirant artistes du monde entier (Picasso, Hemingway, Stein)."

## If You Do Nothing Else for This Theme

*Master French Impressionism. Master key writers (Hugo, Camus, Sartre, Beauvoir). Master French symbols (Eiffel, Notre-Dame). Master existentialism and feminism (Beauvoir).*
