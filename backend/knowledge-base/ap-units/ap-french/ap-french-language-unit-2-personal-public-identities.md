# AP French Language and Culture — Unit 2: Les Identités Personnelles et Publiques — Wayfinder Teaching Brain

> Surfaces in chat when student mentions topics in this unit.

## Unit at a Glance

- **Theme:** Personal and Public Identities — Self-Identity, National/Ethnic Identities, Beliefs, Heroes, Historical Figures

## Big Ideas

1. **French national identity** rooted in Republic, secularism, language.
2. **Francophone identities** vary widely.
3. **Heroes:** Napoleon, de Gaulle, Joan of Arc, Marie Curie.
4. **Belle Époque, May 1968** as cultural reference points.
5. **Multiculturalism** debated — France's universalist ideal vs lived diversity.

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Vocabulary:**
  - **L'identité:** identité personnelle, nationale, religieuse, culturelle, sexuelle.
  - **L'ethnicité:** origine, héritage, ancêtres, immigration, intégration.
  - **Croyances:** religion, foi, athéisme, laïcité.
  - **Personnages historiques:** héros, leaders, figures, écrivains, artistes.
- **Major French historical/cultural figures:**
  - **Charlemagne** (~800 CE).
  - **Joan of Arc (Jeanne d'Arc).**
  - **Louis XIV (Sun King).**
  - **Napoleon Bonaparte.**
  - **Charles de Gaulle** (WWII Free France leader; Fifth Republic).
  - **Marie Curie** (physicist; first woman Nobel; only person to win in 2 sciences).
  - **Voltaire, Rousseau, Sartre, Beauvoir** (philosophers).
  - **Hugo, Balzac, Camus, Proust** (writers).
- **Symbolic identity:**
  - **Marianne:** allegorical figure of French Republic.
  - **Tricolore** (French flag): bleu-blanc-rouge.
  - **La Marseillaise:** national anthem.
  - **Liberté, égalité, fraternité.**
- **Multiculturalism debates:**
  - **Universalism:** French ideal that all citizens are equal regardless of background.
  - **Critique:** doesn't recognize cultural diversity.
  - **Tensions:** banlieue unrest (2005), debates over hijab and other religious symbols.

### Adds for [4]

- **May 1968 (Mai 68):**
  - **Student protests** + general strike.
  - **Cultural rupture** in France.
  - **Reference point** for later movements.
- **Charles de Gaulle:**
  - **WWII Free France leader.**
  - **Founded Fifth Republic (1958).**
  - **Decolonization** of France's empire (Algeria 1962, etc.).

### Adds for [5]

- **Why French universalism contested:**
  - Recognition of difference seen as challenging Republic's principle.
  - But invisibility of difference ignores discrimination.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Identify figure

For each, identify contribution:
(a) Marie Curie.
(b) Charles de Gaulle.
(c) Joan of Arc.

- (a) **Physicist; first woman Nobel; only person Nobel in 2 sciences (physics + chemistry).**
- (b) **WWII Free France leader; founded Fifth Republic.**
- (c) **15th century; led French to victories in Hundred Years' War; later canonized.**

### Example 2 [4] — French symbolism

Identify French national symbols.
- **Marianne:** allegorical female figure of Republic.
- **Tricolore (bleu-blanc-rouge):** flag.
- **La Marseillaise:** anthem.
- **Liberté, égalité, fraternité:** motto.
- **Coq gaulois:** Gallic rooster.

### Example 3 [5] — Universalism

Explain French universalism and its tensions.
- **Principle:** all French citizens equal regardless of ethnic, religious background.
- **Strict:** no official ethnic statistics; no recognition of "minority" identity.
- **Tension:** in practice, discrimination by ethnicity, religion exists.
- **Critique:** invisibility prevents addressing real disparities.
- **Defense:** prevents fragmentation; promotes shared citizenship.

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **France's universalism distinct** from US/British multiculturalism.
2. **Marianne ≠ specific person;** allegorical.
3. **De Gaulle was 20th century,** not earlier.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "L'identité française repose sur la République, la laïcité, et la langue française — un universalisme qui considère tous les citoyens égaux indépendamment de leur origine, mais qui peut occulter les inégalités réelles."
2. "Charles de Gaulle a marqué le 20e siècle français : leader de la France Libre pendant la Seconde Guerre mondiale, fondateur de la Cinquième République (1958), il a aussi présidé à la décolonisation."
3. "Marianne incarne allégoriquement la République française ; le tricolore (bleu-blanc-rouge), La Marseillaise et la devise « Liberté, égalité, fraternité » constituent les symboles fondamentaux de l'identité républicaine."

## If You Do Nothing Else for This Theme

*Master French symbolism (Marianne, tricolore, devise). Master key historical figures. Master universalism. Master May 1968 reference.*
