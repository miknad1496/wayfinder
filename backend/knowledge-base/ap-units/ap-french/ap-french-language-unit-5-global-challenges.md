# AP French Language and Culture — Unit 5: Les Défis Mondiaux — Wayfinder Teaching Brain

> Surfaces in chat when student mentions topics in this unit.

## Unit at a Glance

- **Theme:** Global Challenges — Economy, Health, Population, Environment, Tolerance, Conflict, Migration

## Big Ideas

1. **France faces** common European challenges: aging population, immigration debates, terrorism.
2. **Francophone Africa:** development challenges (poverty, conflict, climate).
3. **Migration** to France from former colonies and beyond.
4. **Banlieue tensions:** marginalization in suburbs.
5. **Climate change** affects France (heat waves, agriculture).

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Vocabulary:**
  - **L'économie:** pauvreté, inégalité, chômage, classe sociale.
  - **Les défis:** défi, problème, crise.
  - **L'immigration:** immigré, réfugié, frontière, intégration, sans-papiers.
  - **Droits humains:** droits humains, liberté, justice, discrimination, égalité.
- **France's challenges:**
  - **Aging population.**
  - **Immigration debates** (especially North African, Sub-Saharan).
  - **Banlieue tensions** (2005 riots).
  - **Terrorism** (Charlie Hebdo 2015, Bataclan 2015, Nice 2016).
  - **Yellow Vests (Gilets jaunes, 2018+):** working-class protest movement.
- **Francophone Africa challenges:**
  - **Poverty, conflict, climate change** (Sahel drought).
  - **Migration to Europe.**
- **Climate impacts in France:**
  - **2003 heat wave** killed ~15,000.
  - **Wildfires** in Mediterranean France.
  - **Coastal erosion.**

### Adds for [4]

- **Banlieue:** French suburbs; many concentrate poverty, immigrant communities.
- **Laïcité tensions:** debates over Islam, hijab, separatism.
- **Yellow Vests (Gilets jaunes):** sparked by fuel tax; broader anti-elite movement.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why French immigration challenges complex:**
  - **Colonial legacy:** Algeria especially.
  - **Universalist ideal vs lived discrimination.**

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Vocabulary

Translate:
(a) refugee.
(b) inequality.
(c) human rights.

- (a) **réfugié.**
- (b) **inégalité.**
- (c) **droits humains.**

### Example 2 [4] — Banlieue

What are French banlieues and why are they politically charged?
- **Banlieue:** suburb; many concentrate immigrant + lower-income populations.
- **Distance from city centers** physically and socially.
- **2005 riots:** death of two teens fleeing police; widespread unrest.
- **Marginalization:** unemployment, discrimination, identity tensions.
- **Periodic flashpoints** continue.

### Example 3 [5] — Yellow Vests

What were the Yellow Vests (Gilets Jaunes)?
- **Started Nov 2018.**
- **Triggered by fuel tax increase.**
- **Movement of working/lower-middle class** against economic policies.
- **Yellow safety vests** symbol (required in cars).
- **Weekly protests for years;** sometimes violent.
- **Macron eventually withdrew tax increase.**
- **Reflects broader inequality** and rural-urban divide.

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Banlieue ≠ all bad** — varies enormously.
2. **Yellow Vests ≠ left or right** — populist working-class.
3. **France has had multiple major terror attacks** in 2010s+.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "Les banlieues françaises — souvent associées aux populations immigrées et à la pauvreté — vivent un mélange de marginalisation économique et culturelle ; les émeutes de 2005, déclenchées par la mort de deux adolescents fuyant la police, ont révélé ces tensions."
2. "Le mouvement des Gilets jaunes (depuis novembre 2018), né d'une augmentation de la taxe sur les carburants, est devenu une expression plus large des classes populaires et moyennes contre les politiques perçues comme favorisant les élites urbaines."
3. "L'Afrique francophone fait face à des défis majeurs : pauvreté, conflits (notamment au Sahel), changement climatique, et migration vers l'Europe — défis qui sont indissociables de l'héritage colonial français."
