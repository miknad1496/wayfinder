# AP French Language and Culture — Unit 6: La Vie Contemporaine — Wayfinder Teaching Brain

> Surfaces in chat when student mentions topics in this unit.

## Unit at a Glance

- **Theme:** Contemporary Life — Education, Career, Travel, Holidays, Sports, Lifestyles, Communications

## Big Ideas

1. **French daily life** patterns: bread daily, leisurely meals, café culture.
2. **Holidays:** Bastille Day (July 14), Christmas (Noël), Toussaint.
3. **Sports:** soccer (football), Tour de France (cycling), Roland-Garros (tennis), rugby.
4. **35-hour workweek** (legal); long vacations.
5. **Tech:** WhatsApp, Instagram, French startup scene.

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Vocabulary:**
  - **L'éducation:** école, lycée, université, devoirs, examen.
  - **Le travail:** emploi, salaire, carrière, retraite (retirement), congés (leave/vacation).
  - **Les loisirs:** vacances, voyage, sport, hobby.
  - **Les fêtes:** célébration, fête, tradition.
- **Major French holidays:**
  - **Le 14 juillet (Bastille Day):** national holiday; commemorates 1789 storming of Bastille; military parade on Champs-Élysées; fireworks.
  - **Noël (Christmas):** family meal Dec 24 (réveillon).
  - **Toussaint (Nov 1):** All Saints; visit cemeteries.
  - **Pâques (Easter).**
  - **Fête de la Musique (June 21):** free music in streets nationwide.
- **Sports:**
  - **Football** (soccer): Ligue 1; les Bleus (national team); 1998 + 2018 World Cup wins.
  - **Tour de France:** prestigious cycling race (since 1903).
  - **Roland-Garros (French Open):** Grand Slam tennis.
  - **Rugby:** XV de France national team.
- **Mealtime patterns:**
  - **Lunch (déjeuner):** ~12-2 PM; can be 1-2 hours.
  - **Dinner (dîner):** ~7-9 PM.
  - **Bread:** baguette daily; cheese course before dessert.
  - **Wine** part of meal culture.
- **Workweek and vacation:**
  - **35-hour workweek** legal (since 2000).
  - **5 weeks paid vacation** legal minimum.
  - **August** = traditional vacation month (many businesses close).

### Adds for [4]

- **Café culture:**
  - **Sitting at café for hours** is normal.
  - **"Apéro"** = pre-dinner drink with friends.
  - **Brasserie, bistrot, café** distinct types.
- **Tour de France:**
  - **3-week race;** ~3,500 km.
  - **Stages cover varied terrain** (mountains, time trials, sprints).
  - **Maillot jaune** (yellow jersey): leader.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why 14 juillet matters:** founding myth of Republic; ongoing commemoration of 1789 Revolution.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Holiday

What is le 14 juillet?
- **Bastille Day:** national holiday.
- **Commemorates** July 14, 1789 storming of Bastille fortress.
- **Modern celebration:** military parade Champs-Élysées; fireworks.
- **Founding event** of French Revolution.

### Example 2 [4] — French workweek

Describe French work and vacation norms.
- **35-hour workweek** legal (since 2000, Aubry Law).
- **5 weeks paid vacation** minimum.
- **August traditional** vacation month.
- **Strong worker protections:** harder to fire than in US.
- **Cultural emphasis on work-life balance.**

### Example 3 [5] — Café culture

What's distinctive about French café culture?
- **Sitting and lingering** central; not just coffee-and-go.
- **People-watching, reading, writing, conversing.**
- **"Apéro"** before dinner = drinks with friends.
- **Cafés open into late night.**
- **Historic cafés** (Café de Flore, Les Deux Magots) hosted intellectuals.

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **14 juillet, not 4 juillet** (US Independence Day).
2. **Football = soccer** in French (NOT American football).
3. **35-hour week is legal max** but practice varies.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "Le 14 juillet, fête nationale française, commémore la prise de la Bastille en 1789 ; il est célébré par un défilé militaire sur les Champs-Élysées et des feux d'artifice à travers le pays."
2. "La semaine de 35 heures (loi Aubry, 2000) et les cinq semaines de congés payés minimum reflètent la priorité française accordée à l'équilibre travail-vie personnelle ; août reste le mois de vacances traditionnel."
3. "La culture du café — où l'on s'assied longuement pour observer, lire ou converser — fait partie intégrante de la vie sociale française ; les cafés historiques de Paris (Café de Flore, Les Deux Magots) ont accueilli des générations d'intellectuels."

## If You Do Nothing Else for This Theme

*Master 14 juillet. Master 35-hour workweek. Master café culture. Master French sports (football, Tour de France, Roland-Garros). Master meal customs.*
