## Unit at a Glance

- **Exam weight:** 12–17% of the AP Physics 2 exam
- **Sub-topics covered:** magnetic fields; force on moving charge (F = qv×B); force on current-carrying wire; magnetic field of straight wire / loop / solenoid; Faraday's law; Lenz's law.
- **Where this unit appears on the exam:** Right-hand rule for force direction. Faraday's law (EMF = −dΦ/dt). Lenz's law for induced current direction.

## Big Ideas

1. **Magnetic force on moving charge:** F = qv × B (perpendicular to both).
2. **Right-hand rule** gives direction of force.
3. **Magnetic field around current-carrying wire:** circles around wire.
4. **Faraday's law:** changing magnetic flux induces EMF (EMF = −dΦ_B/dt).
5. **Lenz's law:** induced current opposes the change in flux.

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Magnetic field B:**
  - **Vector quantity.**
  - **Units:** Tesla (T) = N/(C·m/s) = kg/(C·s).
- **Magnetic force on moving charge:**
  - **F = qv × B** (cross product).
  - **Magnitude:** F = qvB sin θ.
  - **Direction:** right-hand rule (thumb along v, fingers curl toward B, palm pushes in F direction for positive charge; reverse for negative).
- **Force on current-carrying wire:**
  - **F = IL × B** (or magnitude F = ILB sin θ).
- **Magnetic field of straight wire:**
  - **B = μ₀I/(2πr)** (μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ T·m/A).
  - **Direction:** right-hand rule (thumb along current, fingers curl in B direction).
- **Magnetic field of loop:** at center: B = μ₀I/(2R).
- **Magnetic field of solenoid:** B = μ₀nI (n = turns per unit length).
- **Magnetic flux:**
  - **Φ_B = B · A · cos θ** (B normal to area through angle θ).
  - **Units:** Weber (Wb) = T·m².
- **Faraday's law of induction:**
  - **EMF = −dΦ_B/dt** (rate of change of flux).
  - **Negative sign:** Lenz's law direction.
- **Lenz's law:**
  - **Induced current opposes change** in flux.
  - **If flux increasing,** induced current creates opposing field.
  - **If flux decreasing,** induced current maintains field.

### Adds for [4]

- **Charged particle in B field:**
  - **Circular motion** (force perpendicular to velocity).
  - **r = mv/(qB).**
  - **T = 2πm/(qB).**
- **Motional EMF:** EMF = BLv (rod of length L moving at v in field B perpendicular).
- **Right-hand rule shortcuts:**
  - **For wire:** thumb along I, fingers curl with B.
  - **For force:** v × B for + charge (palm pushes); reverse for − charge.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why magnetic forces don't do work:** F perpendicular to v means W = F·d = 0.
- **Why induction important:** basis of generators, transformers, motors.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Force on charge

Electron moving 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to B = 0.01 T. Force?
- **F = qvB = (1.6×10⁻¹⁹)(10⁶)(0.01) = 1.6×10⁻¹⁵ N.**

### Example 2 [3] — Field around wire

Field 0.05 m from wire carrying 10 A?
- **B = μ₀I/(2πr) = (4π×10⁻⁷)(10)/(2π·0.05) = 4×10⁻⁵ T.**

### Example 3 [4] — Right-hand rule

Positive charge moves +x; B is in +y direction. Force direction?
- **v × B = x̂ × ŷ = ẑ.**
- **Force in +z direction.**

### Example 4 [4] — Faraday's law

Coil with 100 turns, area 0.01 m². B changes from 0 to 0.5 T in 0.1 s. EMF?
- **ΔΦ_B per turn = ΔB · A = 0.5 · 0.01 = 0.005 Wb.**
- **For 100 turns:** total ΔΦ = 0.5 Wb.
- **EMF = ΔΦ/Δt = 0.5/0.1 = 5 V.**

### Example 5 [5] — Lenz's law

Bar magnet's north pole approaches a coil from above. Direction of induced current?
- **Approaching N pole = increasing downward flux.**
- **Lenz's law:** induced current opposes → creates upward flux.
- **Right-hand rule:** current flows counterclockwise as viewed from above.

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Wrong direction in cross product.** Use right-hand rule carefully.
2. **Negative charges:** force opposite to v × B direction.
3. **Forgetting Lenz's law sign.** Induced opposes the CHANGE, not the field.
4. **Confusing motional EMF and induced EMF.**
5. **Magnetic force perpendicular to v** — does no work; KE unchanged.

## Rubric-Aware Tactics

**For force on charge:** identify v and B; apply right-hand rule.
**For Faraday's law:** identify how flux changes; calculate EMF.
**For Lenz's law:** identify direction of change; induced current opposes.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "Magnetic force on a moving charge: F = qv × B. Magnitude F = qvB sin θ; direction by right-hand rule (reversed for negative charges). The force is always perpendicular to velocity, so it does no work and KE remains constant."
2. "Magnetic field around a long straight wire: B = μ₀I/(2πr), with field lines circling the wire (right-hand rule with thumb along I)."
3. "Faraday's law: induced EMF equals the negative rate of change of magnetic flux through a loop, EMF = −dΦ_B/dt, where Φ_B = B·A·cos θ."
4. "Lenz's law: the induced current flows in a direction that opposes the change in magnetic flux. If flux is increasing, induced current creates a field opposing that increase; if decreasing, induced current creates a field maintaining it."
5. "Charged particle in a uniform magnetic field undergoes circular motion with radius r = mv/(qB) and period T = 2πm/(qB) — independent of speed."
