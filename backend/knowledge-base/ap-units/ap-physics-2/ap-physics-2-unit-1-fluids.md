## Unit at a Glance

- **Exam weight:** 10–12% of the AP Physics 2 exam
- **Sub-topics covered:** density, pressure, Pascal's principle, buoyancy (Archimedes), continuity, Bernoulli's equation.
- **Where this unit appears on the exam:** Pressure with depth (P = ρgh) tested constantly. Buoyant force calculations. Bernoulli for fluid flow. Hydraulic systems.

## Big Ideas

1. **Pressure increases with depth:** P = P₀ + ρgh.
2. **Pascal's principle:** pressure transmitted equally throughout fluid (hydraulic systems).
3. **Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid** (Archimedes).
4. **Continuity:** A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (mass conservation in steady flow).
5. **Bernoulli:** P + ½ρv² + ρgy = constant (energy conservation along streamline).

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Density:** ρ = m/V. Units: kg/m³.
- **Pressure:** P = F/A. Units: Pa = N/m² = kg/(m·s²).
- **Pressure with depth:**
  - **P = P₀ + ρgh.**
  - P₀ = pressure at surface (atmospheric if open to air, ~10⁵ Pa).
  - h = depth below surface.
- **Pressure is isotropic** in static fluid (acts equally in all directions).
- **Pascal's principle:**
  - **Pressure applied to enclosed fluid** transmitted equally throughout.
  - **Hydraulic lift:** F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂ → small force on small piston lifts heavy load on big piston.
- **Buoyancy (Archimedes' principle):**
  - **Buoyant force = ρ_fluid · V_displaced · g.**
  - **= weight of displaced fluid.**
  - **Floating object:** buoyant force = weight of object → displaced volume × ρ_fluid = volume × ρ_object → fraction submerged = ρ_object/ρ_fluid.
- **Continuity equation:**
  - **A₁v₁ = A₂v₂** (steady, incompressible flow).
  - **Volume flow rate Q = Av** is constant.
  - **Narrower pipe → faster flow.**
- **Bernoulli's equation:**
  - **P + ½ρv² + ρgy = constant** along streamline.
  - **Conservation of energy** for ideal fluid.
  - **Higher velocity → lower pressure** (fixed height).

### Adds for [4]

- **Pascal's principle math:**
  - F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂.
  - But also: A₁d₁ = A₂d₂ (volumes match).
  - Work done equal: F₁d₁ = F₂d₂.
- **Buoyancy detail:**
  - **Object less dense than fluid:** floats.
  - **Object more dense:** sinks.
  - **Object same density:** suspends.
  - **Helium balloon:** ρ_He < ρ_air → floats.
- **Bernoulli applications:**
  - **Wing lift:** air flows faster over curved top → lower pressure → lift up.
  - **Venturi tube:** narrower → faster → lower pressure.
  - **Spray bottle:** rapid air flow over tube reduces pressure → liquid drawn up.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why fluids interesting:** mediate everything from blood flow to weather.
- **Why Bernoulli has limitations:** assumes ideal fluid (no viscosity), incompressible, steady flow.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Pressure at depth

A swimmer is 5 m below the surface of a pool. Find the absolute pressure (P₀ = 10⁵ Pa).
- **P = P₀ + ρgh = 10⁵ + 1000 · 10 · 5 = 10⁵ + 5·10⁴ = 1.5×10⁵ Pa.**

### Example 2 [3] — Buoyant force

A 0.1 m³ object is fully submerged in water. Find the buoyant force.
- **F_b = ρVg = 1000 · 0.1 · 10 = 1000 N.**

### Example 3 [4] — Floating fraction

A wooden block (ρ = 600 kg/m³) floats in water. What fraction is submerged?
- **Fraction submerged = ρ_object/ρ_fluid = 600/1000 = 0.6 = 60%.**

### Example 4 [4] — Continuity

Pipe narrows from 4 cm² to 1 cm². If water flows at 2 m/s in wide section, what's velocity in narrow?
- **A₁v₁ = A₂v₂.**
- **4·2 = 1·v₂ → v₂ = 8 m/s.**

### Example 5 [5] — Bernoulli

Water at 1 m/s and 10⁵ Pa enters a horizontal pipe; exits at 5 m/s. Find exit pressure.
- **P₁ + ½ρv₁² = P₂ + ½ρv₂²** (horizontal, so ρgy terms cancel).
- 10⁵ + 0.5·1000·1² = P₂ + 0.5·1000·25.
- 100000 + 500 = P₂ + 12500.
- **P₂ = 88000 Pa.**

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Forgetting atmospheric pressure** in absolute pressure calculations.
2. **Confusing gauge and absolute pressure.** Gauge = above atmospheric.
3. **Wrong buoyant force formula.** ρ_FLUID, V_DISPLACED, g.
4. **Continuity with same fluid.** Use cross-sectional area, not radius directly.
5. **Bernoulli sign errors** for height terms.

## Rubric-Aware Tactics

**For pressure questions:** identify atmospheric vs gauge. Apply P = P₀ + ρgh.
**For buoyancy:** identify displaced volume; ρ of fluid.
**For flow:** continuity for steady incompressible flow.
**For Bernoulli:** identify two points, conservation along streamline.
