## Unit at a Glance

- **Exam weight:** 14–17% of the AP Physics C: E&M exam
- **Sub-topics covered:** electrostatics with conductors; capacitance (parallel-plate, spherical, cylindrical); energy stored in capacitor; dielectrics; capacitors in series/parallel.
- **Where this unit appears on the exam:** Capacitance derivation. Energy stored. Dielectric effects. Series/parallel capacitor combinations.

## Big Ideas

1. **Conductor in equilibrium:** E = 0 inside; charge on surface.
2. **Capacitance:** C = Q/V (depends on geometry).
3. **Parallel-plate:** C = ε₀A/d.
4. **Energy stored:** U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/(2C).
5. **Dielectric** increases capacitance: C = κC₀.

## Tier-Tagged Content

### Foundational [3] — needed for any score

- **Conductor in electrostatics:**
  - **E = 0 inside** (free charges rearrange).
  - **Excess charge on surface.**
  - **E perpendicular to surface;** magnitude σ/ε₀ at surface.
  - **Conductor is equipotential.**
- **Capacitance:**
  - **C = Q/V** (charge stored per volt).
  - **Units:** Farad (F) = C/V.
- **Parallel-plate capacitor:**
  - **C = ε₀A/d** (vacuum, plate area A, separation d).
  - **E between plates:** σ/ε₀ = Q/(ε₀A).
  - **V = Ed.**
- **Other geometries:**
  - **Spherical (between concentric shells):** C = 4πε₀ ab/(b−a).
  - **Cylindrical (length L, radii a, b):** C = 2πε₀L/ln(b/a).
- **Energy stored in capacitor:**
  - **U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/(2C).**
  - **Energy density of E field:** u = ½ε₀E².
- **Capacitors in series:**
  - **1/C_eq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... .**
  - **Same Q;** voltages add.
- **Capacitors in parallel:**
  - **C_eq = C₁ + C₂ + ... .**
  - **Same V;** charges add.
- **Dielectrics:**
  - **Insulating material between plates.**
  - **Increases capacitance:** C = κC₀ (κ ≥ 1).
  - **κ depends on material** (vacuum 1, water 80, glass 5-10).
  - **Mechanism:** dielectric polarizes; reduces E inside.

### Adds for [4]

- **Why parallel-plate C = ε₀A/d:**
  - Treat plates as infinite charged planes: E = σ/ε₀ = Q/(ε₀A).
  - V = Ed = Qd/(ε₀A).
  - C = Q/V = ε₀A/d.
- **Energy density derivation:**
  - **u = U/Volume = (½CV²)/(Ad) = (½)(ε₀A/d)(E²d²)/(Ad) = ½ε₀E².**
  - **General:** energy stored in E field per unit volume.
- **Dielectric effects:**
  - **C → κC.**
  - **If V held constant** (battery connected): Q → κQ.
  - **If Q held constant** (isolated): V → V/κ.

### Adds for [5]

- **Why dielectric increases C:** electric field inside reduces; same Q yields lower V; thus C = Q/V increases.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1 [3] — Parallel-plate capacitance

Plates 0.01 m² area, 0.001 m apart, vacuum. C?
- **C = ε₀A/d = (8.85×10⁻¹²)(0.01)/(0.001) = 8.85×10⁻¹¹ F = 88.5 pF.**

### Example 2 [3] — Energy stored

Capacitor C = 10 μF charged to V = 100 V. Energy?
- **U = ½CV² = 0.5·(10⁻⁵)·(10⁴) = 0.05 J.**

### Example 3 [4] — Series and parallel

Three capacitors: 2 μF, 4 μF, 6 μF.
- **Parallel:** C_eq = 2 + 4 + 6 = 12 μF.
- **Series:** 1/C_eq = 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/6 = 6/12 + 3/12 + 2/12 = 11/12. C_eq = 12/11 ≈ 1.09 μF.

### Example 4 [4] — Dielectric

Capacitor C₀ = 5 μF in vacuum; insert dielectric κ = 4.
- **New C = κC₀ = 20 μF.**

### Example 5 [5] — Energy density

In a region E = 1000 V/m, find energy density.
- **u = ½ε₀E² = 0.5·(8.85×10⁻¹²)·(10⁶) = 4.43×10⁻⁶ J/m³.**

## Top Traps & Common Errors

1. **Wrong series/parallel combinations.** Capacitors are OPPOSITE of resistors.
2. **Energy depends on which is held constant** (V or Q) when changing geometry.
3. **Dielectric always κ ≥ 1** (cannot decrease C).
4. **Conductor inside has E = 0** (charges screen).

## Rubric-Aware Tactics

**For capacitance:** identify geometry; apply formula or derive via E and V.
**For series/parallel:** capacitors opposite of resistors.
**For dielectric:** identify if Q or V constant.

## "Phrases That Score" — verbatim language for FRQs

1. "Capacitance C = Q/V depends on geometry. For parallel plates: C = ε₀A/d."
2. "Energy stored in a capacitor: U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/(2C). Energy density in the field: u = ½ε₀E²."
3. "Capacitors in series: 1/C_eq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... (charges equal; voltages add). In parallel: C_eq = C₁ + C₂ + ... (voltages equal; charges add). Opposite of resistors."
4. "Inserting a dielectric (κ ≥ 1) increases capacitance to κC₀. If voltage is held constant (battery connected), charge increases by factor κ. If charge is held constant (isolated), voltage decreases by factor 1/κ."
5. "In a conductor in electrostatic equilibrium: the electric field inside is zero, charges reside on the surface, the surface is an equipotential, and the field just outside is perpendicular to the surface with magnitude σ/ε₀."
